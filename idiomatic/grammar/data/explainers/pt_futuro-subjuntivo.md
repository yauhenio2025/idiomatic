## SCRIPT

This is not about expressing the future; it is about where future marking belongs. Seven selection errors and eight later remediation notes show that the pattern persisted.

Picture a future gate. When a dependent clause beginning with one of these expressions describes an event that is still pending, it passes through that gate.

TL: se, quando, logo que, assim que

Inside that gate, Brazilian Portuguese normally uses the future subjunctive. The main clause can still use the ordinary spoken future. The memory line is: after a future gate, do not put “will” inside the gate.

The first recorded sentence puts the spoken future in both clauses.

TL: quando eu vou voltar, eu vou ajustar os cartões

Keep it in the main clause, but switch the time clause.

TL: quando eu voltar, eu vou ajustar os cartões

Regular singular forms often resemble the infinitive. Irregular verbs reveal the tense more clearly. Here is another recorded contrast.

TL: quando eu vou ter mais tempo, vou testar

TL: quando eu tiver mais tempo, vou testar

A useful shortcut starts from the third-person plural of the completed past: remove its final two letters. Listen to the past form, then the future-gate form:

TL: tiveram tempo; quando eu tiver tempo

TL: fizeram isso; assim que eu fizer isso

TL: foram pacientes; se eu for paciente

An open future condition follows the same rule. The notes record this Spanish-shaped version and its Portuguese repair.

TL: se eu vou viajar

TL: se eu viajar

The trigger alone is not enough. In this fresh contrast, the first line is a present habit; the second is still pending.

TL: Quando tenho tempo, leio.

TL: Quando tiver tempo, vou ler.

Do not force the future subjunctive onto a present fact, repeated habit, or completed past event. Use it when the dependent event is future and still pending.

Self-test one. Say: “When I have the manuscript, I’m going to start the review.”

[PAUSE]

Answer:

TL: Quando eu tiver o manuscrito, vou começar a revisão.

Self-test two. Correct the future condition.

TL: Se eles vão conseguir financiamento, vão ampliar o projeto.

[PAUSE]

Answer:

TL: Se eles conseguirem financiamento, vão ampliar o projeto.

Self-test three. Say: “As soon as she does the analysis, she will publish the report.”

[PAUSE]

Answer:

TL: Assim que ela fizer a análise, vai publicar o relatório.
