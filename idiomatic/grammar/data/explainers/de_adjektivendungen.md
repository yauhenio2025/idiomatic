## SCRIPT

Adjective endings are the strongest verified German fossil in the record: six verbatim errors spanning 2019 to 2024. They reach definite articles, no-article plurals, possessives, and the indefinite-article family. Here are the six recorded forms:

TL: Der härtester Konkurrent

TL: Er hat sehr tiefgründiger Schlüsse daraus gezogen.

TL: Sie haben keinen kohärentes Weltbild.

TL: Meine ultimative Ziel ist, daraus einen Film zu machen.

TL: Es kommen noch zwölf weiterer.

TL: Ich brauche ein größtmöglichstes Publikum.

Use this memory rule: one clear signal per noun phrase. First decide gender, number, and case. Then look left. If the determiner already displays the signal, the adjective normally takes the quieter weak ending. If no determiner carries it, the adjective must carry a strong ending. An indefinite article, possessive, or negative determiner sometimes leaves the signal unstated; then the adjective fills the gap.

Contrast one: the definite article already marks masculine nominative, so the adjective needs the weak ending.

TL: der härteste Konkurrent

Not two masculine markers in a row.

Contrast two: with no article, the adjective carries the plural accusative signal. The recorded sentence becomes:

TL: Er hat sehr tiefgründige Schlüsse daraus gezogen.

The same strong plural ending follows a bare numeral.

TL: Es kommen noch zwölf weitere.

Contrast three: the two recorded neuter nouns require agreement across the entire phrase. In nominative or accusative neuter, the endingless determiner leaves a gap, and the adjective supplies the strong ending.

TL: Sie haben kein kohärentes Weltbild.

TL: Mein ultimatives Ziel ist, daraus einen Film zu machen.

Contrast four is a separate-looking trap with the same cure: distinguish the adjective’s stem from its agreement ending. The stem already contains the maximum-degree meaning, so adding another superlative marker duplicates it. Add only the neuter ending.

TL: Ich brauche ein größtmögliches Publikum.

Do not guess from sound. Diagnose the noun phrase in this order: noun features, case, determiner signal, adjective ending.

Self-test one. Say: the new report was convincing.

[PAUSE]

TL: Der neue Bericht war überzeugend.

Self-test two. Say: the editorial team publishes critical analyses.

[PAUSE]

TL: Die Redaktion veröffentlicht kritische Analysen.

Self-test three. Say: our new program works reliably.

[PAUSE]

TL: Unser neues Programm funktioniert zuverlässig.
