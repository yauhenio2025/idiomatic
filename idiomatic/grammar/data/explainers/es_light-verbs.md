## SCRIPT

Three recorded corrections expose one durable habit: reaching automatically for a general-purpose verb before retrieving the Spanish noun’s natural partner. In the decision and error phrases, English “make” may reinforce that choice. Two wrong sides preserve full learner phrases:

TL: hacer estas decisiones

TL: hacer errores

In the photo correction, the wrong side preserved only the learner’s bare verb:

TL: hacer

The teacher supplied the target phrase:

TL: tomar fotos

The memory rule is simple: the noun chooses the light verb. Do not translate “make” or “do” first. Retrieve the whole verb-and-noun bundle as if it were one long word.

Contrast one: decisions pair with the verb of taking, while plans can pair with the general-purpose verb of making.

TL: tomar una decisión

TL: hacer un plan

The recorded decision phrase was taught again later, and its noun form also recurred. That is a sign to store the whole family together.

TL: tomar decisiones

TL: la toma de decisiones

Contrast two: in the Colombian lesson target, photographs pair with the verb of taking. This choice is also common across much of Latin America.

TL: tomar una foto

TL: tomar varias fotos

In Spain, the version with the general-purpose verb is common and standard. The choice practiced here follows the lesson record; this is a regional distribution, not a universal ban.

Contrast three: errors pair with the verb of committing. The same partner is used for an offence, while a correction can use the general-purpose verb.

TL: cometer un error

TL: cometer varios errores

TL: hacer una corrección

Contrast four: other abstract nouns choose other partners. A literal translation will not predict them reliably.

TL: dar un paso

TL: sacar una conclusión

TL: poner algo bajo control

When you learn an abstract noun, attach one typical verb immediately. The useful memory unit is not the isolated noun; it is a small action phrase.

Self-test one. Say: we have to make a decision today.

[PAUSE]

TL: Tenemos que tomar una decisión hoy.

Self-test two. Say: I made two mistakes in the report.

[PAUSE]

TL: Cometí dos errores en el informe.

Self-test three. Following the lesson target, say: tomorrow we are going to take several photos.

[PAUSE]

TL: Mañana vamos a tomar varias fotos.
