## SCRIPT

This error is systematic, persistent, and very fixable. Gender alone produced 193 marked rows across four years, and the same agreement system was still being reviewed in 2023 and 2024. Two ending families cause much of the trouble. Hear their high-frequency core:

TL: o problema, o programa, o sistema, o tema, o idioma

TL: a mensagem, a viagem

The memory hook is: the first family usually wears the masculine article; the second overwhelmingly wears the feminine article.

The first pattern covers a Greek-derived family. It is a strong family resemblance, not a promise about every noun with those final letters. The second pattern is highly reliable for ordinary nouns, though human-reference words can behave differently. Learn a new noun with its article whenever possible.

The first recorded contrast shows the feminine article and adjective where this masculine family requires the opposite agreement:

TL: os programas... uma programa nova

The noun controls both the article and the adjective, so repair the whole phrase, not only one word.

TL: os programas... um programa novo

Now reverse the instinct for the longer ending. This phrase is recorded and needs feminine agreement.

TL: um mensagem

TL: uma mensagem

The demonstrative changes too. The notes contain this contrast.

TL: este viagem

TL: esta viagem

Gender also controls the numeral. This recorded contrast runs in the other direction.

TL: duas meses

TL: dois meses

Agreement radiates outward from the noun. Articles, demonstratives, possessives, adjectives, the numbers one and two, and the hundreds can all reveal its gender. That is why memorizing the noun alone is not enough. Store a short phrase, hear its article, and let every neighboring word follow it.

For Brazilian Portuguese, keep the same agreement inside contractions as well. Once the noun’s gender is settled, the surrounding choices become mechanical. Do not translate the article from Spanish or Italian; retrieve the Portuguese noun-and-article package.

Self-test one. Choose the complete phrase for a new system.

TL: um sistema novo ou uma sistema nova?

[PAUSE]

Answer:

TL: um sistema novo

Self-test two. Say “an urgent message,” including the article.

[PAUSE]

Answer:

TL: uma mensagem urgente

Self-test three. Say “two trips.”

[PAUSE]

Answer:

TL: duas viagens
