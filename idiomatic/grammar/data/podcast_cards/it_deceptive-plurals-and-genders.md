That third class is the early warning: the final vowel does not determine gender. The article does. That's the ear training done — move on to the next card.

[CARD]
TITLE: Store the package, not the noun
SVG: it_deceptive-plurals-and-genders_c2f.svg

The rule this card teaches: never store an Italian noun as one naked citation form. Store a three-part package — singular phrase, plural phrase, one agreeing neighbor:
TL: il problema; i problemi; problemi complessi
[PAUSE:1200]
TL: la città; le città; città intelligenti
[PAUSE:1200]
TL: l'uovo; le uova; uova fresche
[PAUSE:1500]
That package holds the gender, the article shape, the plural, and the agreement — and it stops your other Romance languages from supplying an article by reflex.
SHOW: singular + plural + agreeing neighbor = one memory
When you're ready, flip the card for the most famous liar.

[SIDE]
TITLE: The learned masculine family
SVG: it_deceptive-plurals-and-genders_c2b.svg

The most famous liars: a family of high-frequency learned nouns whose final vowel looks feminine — and which are all masculine:
TL: il problema, il sistema, il programma, il tema, il clima
[PAUSE:1200]
Their plurals and every agreeing word follow masculine:
TL: i problemi complessi
TL: due sistemi diversi
[PAUSE:1200]
And the repair must radiate across the whole phrase — fixing only the article leaves the phrase disagreeing with itself:
TL: un problema serio
TL: questo sistema complesso
SHOW: article **and** adjective — gender radiates
[PAUSE:1500]
Store the core members with their articles; history explains them, but the article is the fact you retrieve. Move on to the next card.

TITLE: Refusers and reverse traps

Two more families. First the reverse traps — feminine nouns wearing a masculine-looking vowel:
TL: la mano; le mani
TL: la foto; le foto

[PAUSE:1200]

TL: la città; le città
TL: il film; i film
TL: la crisi; le crisi
[PAUSE:1200]

TITLE: Gender-changers and body parts
SVG: it_deceptive-plurals-and-genders_c4b.svg

The most theatrical group changes gender between singular and plural:
TL: l'uovo fresco
TL: le uova fresche
[PAUSE:1200]
The same pattern survives in a small, high-value set:
TL: il paio; le paia
[PAUSE:1000]
Body parts add meaning to the choice — the feminine plural reads the parts as a natural set, the masculine as separate items:
TL: le braccia di una persona
TL: i bracci di una gru
SHOW: human arms — feminine set · crane arms — masculine items
[PAUSE:1200]
For everyday anatomy, make the feminine set plurals automatic:
TL: le dita; le ginocchia; le ossa.
[PAUSE:1500]
A closed set, not a productive rule — learn each pair as a contrast. Move on to the next card.
