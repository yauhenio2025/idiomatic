[CARD]
TITLE: The branch: look left of the adjective
SVG: de_adjective-endings_c2f.svg

The rule this card teaches: the adjective pattern is chosen by what stands to its left. A definite article has already done the loud signaling, so the adjective goes quiet — the weak pattern:
TL: der neue Bericht
TL: mit dem neuen Bericht
[PAUSE:1200]
An indefinite article, a negative determiner or a possessive sometimes leaves a hole — the mixed pattern, quiet where the determiner speaks, strong where it doesn't:
TL: ein neuer Bericht
TL: kein neues Programm
[PAUSE:1200]
And with no determiner at all, the adjective carries nearly all the signal itself — the strong pattern:
TL: neuer Bericht
TL: mit neuem Material
[PAUSE:1500]
Three branches, but one job: someone has to carry the signal.
SHOW: definite → weak · ein/kein/mein → mixed · nothing → strong
Flip the card for the holes that caused your fossils.

[SIDE]
TITLE: The holes in the mixed family
SVG: de_adjective-endings_c2b.svg

The mixed family has three famous holes: masculine nominative, neuter nominative, neuter accusative. There the determiner shows no ending, so the adjective must supply the strong signal:
TL: ein neuer Bericht
TL: ein neues Ziel
[PAUSE:1200]
Two of your recorded errors live in exactly that neuter hole:
TL: kein kohärentes Weltbild
SHOW: kein — no neuter signal → the adjective says **-es**
TL: mein ultimatives Ziel
SHOW: mein — same hole, same fix
[PAUSE:1200]
Everywhere else the determiner speaks clearly and the adjective settles into the quiet form:
TL: eine neue Studie
TL: mit einem neuen Bericht
[PAUSE:1500]
Ask one question: does the determiner leave the decisive cell exposed? Holes mapped — move on to the next card.

[SIDE]
TITLE: Weak branch: only two endings
SVG: de_adjective-endings_c3b.svg

After a definite determiner the adjective has exactly two choices. The quiet ending lives in five cells — the three nominative singulars plus feminine and neuter accusative:
TL: der neue Bericht
TL: die neue Studie
TL: das neue Programm
[PAUSE:1200]
Everywhere else — masculine accusative, every dative and genitive, all plurals — the longer ending:
TL: den neuen Bericht
TL: mit der neuen Studie
TL: wegen des neuen Programms
TL: die neuen Berichte
[PAUSE:1500]
Two endings, five quiet cells — that is the entire weak table. Move on to the next card.

TITLE: Strong branch: the adjective leads
SVG: de_adjective-endings_c4b.svg

With no determiner, the adjective borrows the sound of the missing definite article. Nominative singular, three genders:
TL: guter Rat
TL: klare Sprache
TL: kaltes Wasser
[PAUSE:1200]
Dative, where your record needed it:
TL: mit gutem Grund
TL: mit kaltem Wasser
TL: mit neuen Ideen
[PAUSE:1200]

TL: tiefgründige Schlüsse
TL: zwölf weitere Kapitel
[PAUSE:1200]
One boundary keeps the tree honest: a predicate adjective after linking verbs takes no ending at all.
TL: Der Bericht ist neu.
SHOW: predicate — no ending
[PAUSE:1500]
Copy the article's sound, but only inside a noun phrase. Move on to the next card.

Let's land it. Three things to take into your German.
First, the route — never start at the final syllable:
SHOW: 1. gender/number → case → determiner → adjective → noun
TL: mit unserer neuen Strategie
[PAUSE:1000]
Second, the three branches are one signaling job: after a definite article the adjective is quiet; the mixed family's holes make it speak; alone, it leads.
SHOW: 2. der → quiet · ein/kein/mein → fill the hole · alone → lead
TL: ein neues Ziel
[PAUSE:1000]
Third, the boundary: predicate and adverbial adjectives stay bare.
SHOW: 3. after "is" — no ending
TL: Der Bericht ist neu.
