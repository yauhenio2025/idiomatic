TITLE: The final-E trap
SVG: fr_gender-is-memory_c2b.svg

The trap this side disarms: a final letter E proves nothing about gender. The clearest evidence is three pairs of nouns that sound identical, end in E — and split genders with their meanings. Method versus fashion:
TL: ce mode; la mode
SHOW: method (m) · fashion (f)
[PAUSE:1000]
Party versus part — one added vowel, a different feminine noun:
TL: le parti a publié son programme
SHOW: the party published its program
TL: la première partie du livre
SHOW: the first part of the book
[PAUSE:1000]
And book versus pound, the unit of weight:
TL: le livre; la livre
SHOW: book (m) · pound (f)
[PAUSE:1500]
These aren't exceptions to an ending rule — there is no ending rule. They're separate words, each stored with its own packet. Final E filed under "proves nothing" — move on to the next card.
