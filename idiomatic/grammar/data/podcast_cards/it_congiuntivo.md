[MUSIC:intro]
This card is about the Italian subjunctive — the mood you have become expert at avoiding. Your record holds zero subjunctive errors, and that is not mastery: it's a corpus of sentences built to never need one. You named it yourself as the weakness. Listen to what the mood actually does:
TL: So che Marco è pronto.
SHOW: presented as known — indicative
[PAUSE:1200]
TL: Dubito che Marco sia pronto.
SHOW: placed inside doubt — subjunctive
[PAUSE:1200]
The event never moved in time. The speaker's stance moved, and the mood makes that stance audible. Once more, just the switch:
TL: è pronto
TL: sia pronto
[PAUSE:1500]
Keep that switch in your ear — and flip the card.

[SIDE]
TITLE: The switch is a vowel
SVG: it_congiuntivo_c1b.svg

Ear training first: like its Spanish cousin, the Italian switch is mostly one vowel. First-conjugation verbs flip their final A to I:
TL: funziona, funzioni
[PAUSE:1000]
The other conjugations flip toward A:
TL: crede, creda
[PAUSE:1000]
TL: viene, venga
[PAUSE:1200]
And the high-frequency irregulars are sound anchors — retrieve them whole, never derive them under pressure:
TL: sia, abbia, vada, faccia, possa, voglia, debba, sappia, venga, dia
[PAUSE:2000]
That's the ear training done — move on to the next card.

[CARD]
TITLE: The frame controls the clause
SVG: it_congiuntivo_c2f.svg

The rule this card teaches: a main frame plus a dependent clause — and the frame decides how that clause is presented. Will and influence open the subjunctive:
TL: Voglio che tu legga il rapporto.
SHOW: wanting
[PAUSE:1000]
TL: Il direttore chiede che il testo sia più breve.
SHOW: requesting
[PAUSE:1000]
TL: Bisogna che il governo intervenga.
SHOW: requiring
[PAUSE:1200]
But the little word che only opens the clause — it triggers nothing by itself. A factual frame keeps the indicative:
TL: È chiaro che il piano funziona.
SHOW: fact frame — indicative
[PAUSE:1500]
The question is never "which word did I just say" — it's "what does my frame do to the clause". Flip the card for the compression rule.

[SIDE]
TITLE: The compression rule
SVG: it_congiuntivo_c2b.svg

The compression rule, and its anti-avoidance twin. When the subject stays the same, Italian prefers a plain infinitive — no clause, no mood decision:
TL: Voglio partire domani.
SHOW: one subject — infinitive
[PAUSE:1000]
When the desired actor is somebody else, the full clause appears:
TL: Voglio che tu parta domani.
SHOW: new subject — che + subjunctive
[PAUSE:1500]
Both directions matter for you. Don't force the subjunctive where the same-subject infinitive is natural — and don't quietly erase the second person just to escape the mood.
SHOW: don't erase the other person to dodge the mood
Compression rule pocketed — move on to the next card.

[SIDE]
TITLE: Opinions — and the boundary
SVG: it_congiuntivo_c3b.svg

This side widens the inputs: doubt, opinion, emotion. Withheld commitment flips the clause:
TL: Non credo che questa soluzione basti.
SHOW: doubt — subjunctive
[PAUSE:1000]
And in careful Italian, even an affirmative opinion frame takes the subjunctive as the reliable default:
TL: Penso che sia una buona idea.
SHOW: opinion frame + che → subjunctive
[PAUSE:1000]
But the phrase meaning "in my opinion" introduces a direct assertion — indicative:
TL: Secondo me, è una buona idea.
SHOW: direct assertion — indicative
[PAUSE:1200]
Emotion works the same way — the event can be perfectly real:
TL: Sono contento che tu sia venuto.
SHOW: the person is right there — presentation, not reality
[PAUSE:1500]
Reality is not the dividing line; presentation is. Boundary drawn — move on to the next card.

[SIDE]
TITLE: Sought vs known — and a guardrail
SVG: it_congiuntivo_c4b.svg

The last input is existence. A known, identified person or thing states facts in the indicative:
TL: Conosco un traduttore che sa lavorare in fretta.
SHOW: known — indicative
A sought one is described by a desired possibility:
TL: Cerco un traduttore che sappia lavorare in fretta.
SHOW: sought — subjunctive
[PAUSE:1200]
That is exactly your teacher's embedded target — the needed technology is not yet identified:
TL: Abbiamo bisogno di una nuova tecnologia che riesca a ridurre i costi.
[PAUSE:1500]
And one guardrail: an if-clause is not automatically subjunctive territory. An open condition stays indicative; a hypothetical takes the imperfect subjunctive with a conditional result — and the conditional never enters the if-clause:
TL: Se ho tempo, vengo.
TL: Se avessi tempo, verrei.
SHOW: never a conditional inside the if-clause
[PAUSE:1500]
Guardrail set — move on to the next card.
