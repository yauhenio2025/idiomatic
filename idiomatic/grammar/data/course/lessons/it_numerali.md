## SCRIPT

[CARD]
TITLE: Italian numbers change construction as well as value
REF: 12.1, 12.3, 12.6, 12.8, 12.10, 12.13

A cardinal counts, an ordinal locates something in a series, and a collective or approximative form presents a quantity as a group or an estimate. Keep these grammatical jobs separate.
TL: Hanno invitato venti persone, ma ne sono arrivate una quindicina.
EN: They invited twenty people, but about fifteen arrived.
[PAUSE:900]
Italian also has dedicated frames for all of a numbered set, percentages, fractions, and groups of people. The surrounding article, noun, and verb can matter as much as the numeral itself.
TL: Tutti e tre i candidati hanno superato il primo colloquio.
EN: All three candidates passed the first interview.
SHOW: exact count -> cardinal · position in a series -> ordinal · approximate group -> collective numeral · complete numbered set -> tutti e + number
Flip for the word-building rules behind the cardinal system.

[SIDE]
TITLE: Build most cardinals as one written word
REF: 12.1, 12.2

The tens combine directly with the units. The final vowel of venti and the tens in -anta disappears before uno and otto; a final tre normally carries a written accent.
TL: ventuno · ventotto · trentatré · centottantuno
EN: twenty-one · twenty-eight · thirty-three · one hundred and eighty-one
[PAUSE:900]
Cento compounds directly. Mille becomes mila after another numeral, while milione and miliardo are separate nouns.
TL: millequaranta · tremila · due milioni · sette miliardi
EN: one thousand and forty · three thousand · two million · seven billion
SHOW: tens + uno/otto -> drop final vowel · compound-final tre -> tré · one thousand -> mille · multiple thousands -> -mila · million/billion -> separate noun

[CARD]
TITLE: Cardinal nouns determine agreement and linkage
REF: 12.1, 12.2, 12.3, 12.4, 12.5

A numeral ending in uno normally modifies a plural noun; before a following noun, final -uno may appear as -un. When uno stands alone, solo or unico can make the meaning just one explicit.
TL: Nel corso ci sono sessantuno studenti, ma un unico tutor.
EN: There are sixty-one students on the course, but only one tutor.
[PAUSE:900]
Milione and miliardo behave as nouns and take di before an immediately following noun. The link disappears when another numeral follows them inside the same number.
TL: La città ha due milioni di abitanti, non due milioni cinquecentomila abitanti.
EN: The city has two million inhabitants, not two million five hundred thousand inhabitants.
SHOW: number ending in uno -> normally plural noun · just one -> solo/unico · X milioni + noun -> di · X milioni + further numeral + noun -> no di

[SIDE]
TITLE: Some number forms behave like ordinary nouns
REF: 12.2, 12.5

Uno and zero can be named and pluralized when talking about digits. Zero used as a quantity normally selects a plural noun, while pari and dispari mean even and odd.
TL: Il codice ha due uni e tre zeri; il totale è zero punti.
EN: The code has two ones and three zeros; the total is zero points.
[PAUSE:900]
Collective plural forms such as migliaia and centinaia take di before their noun. Fixed expressions may preserve elision or a lexicalized small number.
TL: Migliaia di persone hanno risposto in quattro e quattr'otto.
EN: Thousands of people replied in no time at all.
SHOW: named digits -> uno/uni, zero/zeri · zero + counted noun -> plural · thousands/hundreds of -> migliaia/centinaia di · even/odd -> pari/dispari

[CARD]
TITLE: Tutti e includes every member of a numbered set
REF: 12.6

Use tutti or tutte plus e plus the numeral. Before a stated noun, put its definite article after the numeral and agree both tutti and the article with the noun.
TL: Tutte e quattro le finestre erano aperte.
EN: All four windows were open.
[PAUSE:900]
With no following noun, the article is absent. Ambedue is a more literary equivalent of both and can modify a noun or stand predicatively.
TL: I progetti sono ambedue validi, ma li controllerò tutti e due.
EN: Both projects are valid, but I will check both of them.
SHOW: all N + noun -> tutti/tutte e N + article + noun · all N alone -> tutti/tutte e N · literary both -> ambedue
Eleven source transformations follow.

[SIDE]
TITLE: Altro before a numeral adds a further quantity
REF: 12.6

English another plus a number becomes altri or altre plus the number. Altro agrees with the noun that is counted, even when a measurement abbreviation intervenes.
TL: Servono altre tre sedie e altri due metri di cavo.
EN: Another three chairs and another two metres of cable are needed.
[PAUSE:900]
When the added quantity is one, ordinary un altro or un'altra supplies the singular pattern.
TL: Aspettiamo un'altra settimana.
EN: We will wait another week.
SHOW: another N masculine items -> altri + N · another N feminine items -> altre + N · one more -> un altro/un'altra

[CARD]
TITLE: Ordinals agree like adjectives
REF: 12.8

Primo through decimo have their own forms. Above ten, ordinary ordinals are generally built by removing the cardinal's final vowel and adding -esimo.
TL: primo · secondo · decimo · undicesimo · ventottesimo
EN: first · second · tenth · eleventh · twenty-eighth
[PAUSE:900]
An ordinal normally precedes its noun and agrees in gender and number. Names of monarchs and popes instead place the ordinal after the name without an article.
TL: È la cinquantesima volta; Giovanni ventitreesimo fu eletto nel 1958.
EN: It is the fiftieth time; John XXIII was elected in 1958.
SHOW: cardinal stem + esimo/a/i/e · ordinary noun -> ordinal before noun · monarch or pope -> name + ordinal

[SIDE]
TITLE: First and last can select people as well as positions
REF: 12.8

An ordinal can stand with an article when the noun is understood. A person who performs an action first or last is commonly linked to the infinitive with a.
TL: Marta è stata la prima ad arrivare e l'ultima a partire.
EN: Marta was the first to arrive and the last to leave.
[PAUSE:900]
Penultimo means next to last, while ennesimo means an unspecified repeated position and often conveys irritation.
TL: Questa è l'ennesima richiesta arrivata al penultimo minuto.
EN: This is the umpteenth request to arrive at the next-to-last minute.
SHOW: the first/last to act -> il/la primo/a or ultimo/a + a + infinitive · next to last -> penultimo · umpteenth -> ennesimo

[CARD]
TITLE: Collective nouns turn exact numbers into groups or estimates
REF: 12.10

Paio, dozzina, the nouns in -ina, centinaio, and migliaio introduce the counted noun with di. The -ina forms commonly mean approximately the base number.
TL: Ho comprato un paio di quaderni e una ventina di buste.
EN: I bought a couple of notebooks and about twenty envelopes.
[PAUSE:900]
Their irregular plurals follow noun agreement: paia, centinaia, and migliaia are feminine. Bare plural centinaia and migliaia express hundreds or thousands.
TL: Centinaia di persone hanno atteso per un paio d'ore.
EN: Hundreds of people waited for a couple of hours.
SHOW: pair -> paio di · dozen -> dozzina di · about N -> una N-ina di · hundreds/thousands -> centinaia/migliaia di
Eleven source approximations follow.

[SIDE]
TITLE: An article or range can make a plain numeral approximate
REF: 12.10

Un before a cardinal can mean about that many. Circa, suppergiù, and the preposition su with an article offer more explicit approximations.
TL: Mancano un trenta chilometri; il viaggio costerà circa cento euro.
EN: About thirty kilometres remain; the trip will cost about one hundred euros.
[PAUSE:900]
Two nearby numbers can be joined with o, juxtaposed, or sometimes hyphenated to indicate a range.
TL: Avrà sedici o diciassette anni.
EN: He or she must be sixteen or seventeen years old.
SHOW: about N -> un + N · explicit approximation -> circa/suppergiù/sui + N · narrow range -> N o N

[CARD]
TITLE: Multiplicatives and fractions use noun-sensitive frames
REF: 12.11, 12.12, 12.13

Doppio and triplo can be adjectives. As nouns meaning twice or three times an amount, they take a masculine article and introduce the comparison with di.
TL: La stanza costa il doppio di quella accanto.
EN: The room costs twice as much as the one next door.
[PAUSE:900]
Percentages use a masculine determiner plus per cento and are normally singular. Fractions use a cardinal numerator and an ordinal denominator; mezzo agrees before a noun but stays e mezzo after it.
TL: Il sessanta per cento è favorevole; ho letto tre quarti del libro in due ore e mezzo.
EN: Sixty per cent is in favour; I read three quarters of the book in two and a half hours.
SHOW: twice/three times X -> il doppio/triplo di X · percentage -> il N per cento + singular verb · fraction -> cardinal + ordinal plural · half -> mezzo + noun / noun + e mezzo
Eleven hygiene-gated number completions follow.

[SIDE]
TITLE: Per distributes; in counts a group; measurements follow the adjective
REF: 12.14, 12.15, 12.17

Per expresses a rate per unit. A and the bare article are frequent with speed and price, while a ... a ... distributes people or things in equal groups.
TL: Consuma sei litri per cento chilometri; entrate a due a due.
EN: It uses six litres per hundred kilometres; enter two by two.
[PAUSE:900]
In plus a numeral presents people as a group. Dimensions normally put the numeral and measurement after alto, largo, lungo, profondo, or lontano.
TL: Siamo in cinque; il tavolo è lungo due metri.
EN: There are five of us; the table is two metres long.
SHOW: per each -> per · N at a time -> a N a N · group of N -> in N · dimension -> adjective + N + unit

[CARD]
TITLE: Arithmetic has a compact spoken grammar
REF: 12.16

Più adds, meno subtracts, per or moltiplicato per multiplies, and diviso divides. The result can follow uguale, fa, or sono, and the linking word may also be omitted.
TL: Sette più tre fa dieci; ventotto diviso quattro uguale sette.
EN: Seven plus three equals ten; twenty-eight divided by four equals seven.
[PAUSE:900]
Italian reads the full expression in order. When the exercise asks for a result in words, apply the ordinary cardinal spelling rules as well as doing the calculation.
TL: Ventinove per quattro fa centosedici.
EN: Twenty-nine times four equals one hundred and sixteen.
SHOW: + -> più · - -> meno · × -> per/volte/moltiplicato per · ÷ -> diviso · = -> uguale/fa/sono or zero linker
Sixteen hygiene-gated calculations follow; the already-complete duplicated example is omitted.

[SIDE]
TITLE: Powers and square measurements use different constructions
REF: 12.16

Use al quadrato and al cubo for powers. Higher powers use elevato alla plus an agreeing ordinal.
TL: Tre al quadrato fa nove; due elevato alla quinta potenza fa trentadue.
EN: Three squared is nine; two to the fifth power is thirty-two.
[PAUSE:900]
For area and volume measurements, place quadrato or cubo after the unit of measure.
TL: L'ufficio misura cento metri quadrati.
EN: The office measures one hundred square metres.
SHOW: squared -> al quadrato · cubed -> al cubo · to the Nth power -> elevato alla N-esima potenza · square metres -> metri quadrati

[CARD]
TITLE: Read telephone numbers digit by digit or in groups
REF: 12.18

A telephone number can be read as a series of individual digits. Unlike English, repeated digits are normally spoken separately, without doppio or triplo.
TL: Il numero termina con cinque uno uno sette.
EN: The number ends in five double one seven.
[PAUSE:900]
Pairs may instead be read as two-digit cardinals. If the total has an odd number of digits, the first group can contain three digits.
TL: 51 17 53 si legge cinquantuno, diciassette, cinquantatré.
EN: 51 17 53 is read fifty-one, seventeen, fifty-three.
SHOW: digit reading -> say every digit · repeated digit -> repeat it, no doppio/triplo · pair grouping -> read each pair as a cardinal

[SIDE]
TITLE: Match numeral spelling to the register and scale
REF: 12.7, 12.18

Ordinary prose often spells out numbers through ten; above ten, either words or figures can be appropriate. Large round quantities frequently combine figures with mila, milioni, or miliardi written as words.
TL: Sono arrivate otto risposte; servono ancora 500 mila firme.
EN: Eight replies arrived; another five hundred thousand signatures are needed.
[PAUSE:900]
Whatever notation you choose, keep Italian decimal commas and the numeral's spoken grammar distinct from English conventions.
TL: Il valore è 10,8: dieci virgola otto.
EN: The value is 10.8: ten point eight.
SHOW: ordinary prose 0-10 -> usually words · large round amount -> figures + mila/milioni/miliardi · decimal separator -> comma, read virgola
