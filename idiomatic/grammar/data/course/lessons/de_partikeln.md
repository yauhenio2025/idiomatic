## SCRIPT

[CARD]
TITLE: The proposition stays; the social move changes
SVG: de_partikeln_c01f.svg
REF: 9

Modal particles do not normally add a new event or participant. They tell the listener how to take the whole utterance: as shared knowledge, a correction, a surprise, a softened request, or a warning. Compare a bare fact with the same fact placed against common ground:
TL: Du bist spät dran.
EN: You are running late.
[PAUSE:900]
TL: Du bist ja spät dran.
EN: You are running late, as you know.
[PAUSE:1200]
The clock has not changed. The second version also manages the relationship between speaker and listener. That extra conversational layer is the system behind the apparent clutter.
SHOW: same proposition · different conversational move
Flip the card.

[SIDE]
TITLE: Hear the diagnostic profile
REF: 9

A typical modal particle is unstressed, optional for sentence structure, and has scope over the clause. It usually lives inside the clause rather than taking the first position, and it cannot itself be negated. Remove it here and the report survives, but the appeal to shared knowledge disappears:
TL: Den Bericht hast du ja bereits gelesen.
EN: You have read the report already, as we both know.
[PAUSE:1000]
TL: Den Bericht hast du bereits gelesen.
EN: You have already read the report.
[PAUSE:1200]
These are diagnostics, not an airtight word class. Many of the same forms also work as conjunctions, adverbs, or focus particles. Function comes from stress, position, scope, and sentence type together.
SHOW: unstressed · optional · clause-wide · normally inside the clause
First learn to identify the job, then choose the shade.

[CARD]
TITLE: One form can do several jobs
REF: 9.1.1, 9.1.19, 9.1.34

Never memorize a particle as one English word. Start by separating its grammatical jobs. Ja can be an answer or a modal signal; aber can link two clauses or colour one clause; wohl can describe well-being or mark probability.
TL: Ja, ich übernehme den Dienst.
EN: Yes, I will take the shift.
[PAUSE:800]
TL: Du kennst ja den Ablauf.
EN: You know the procedure, of course.
[PAUSE:800]
TL: Ich wäre gekommen, aber mein Zug fiel aus.
EN: I would have come, but my train was cancelled.
[PAUSE:800]
TL: Das ist aber großzügig von dir!
EN: That is remarkably generous of you!
[PAUSE:1000]
The shape is identical; the syntactic environment reveals the job.
SHOW: classify the use before interpreting the shade
Flip the card for scope.

[SIDE]
TITLE: Clause scope versus focus scope
REF: 9, 9.1.4, 9.1.12, 9.1.25

A modal particle colours the speaker's stance toward the clause. A focus particle points at one constituent and ranks it against alternatives. Position now changes which piece is highlighted:
TL: Sogar die Direktorin blieb bis Mitternacht.
EN: Even the director stayed until midnight.
[PAUSE:900]
TL: Die Direktorin blieb ja bis Mitternacht.
EN: The director stayed until midnight, as we know.
[PAUSE:900]
TL: Erst am Freitag fiel der Fehler auf.
EN: The error was not noticed until Friday.
[PAUSE:1100]
Sogar focuses the director. Erst focuses the late time. Ja reaches over the entire proposition and marks it as common ground.
SHOW: focus particle → one constituent · modal particle → the whole clause
This distinction prevents a great deal of false translation.

[CARD]
TITLE: Shared ground or active correction?
SVG: de_partikeln_c03f.svg
REF: 9.1.7, 9.1.19

Ja and doch can both invite agreement, but they make opposite assumptions. Ja presents the proposition as already shared. Doch pushes against doubt, forgetfulness, or contradiction:
TL: Die Frist endet ja morgen.
EN: The deadline expires tomorrow, as we both know.
[PAUSE:1000]
TL: Die Frist endet doch morgen.
EN: The deadline does expire tomorrow, contrary to what you seem to think.
[PAUSE:1200]
Use ja when the listener is expected to nod. Use doch when the listener may resist and needs steering back.
SHOW: ja = shared ground · doch = counter-pressure
Flip the card for conclusions and reasons.

[SIDE]
TITLE: Mark the reasoning link
REF: 9.1.3, 9.1.8, 9.1.28

Some particles tell the listener how the current claim follows from the conversation. Also presents it as the conclusion or asks whether that conclusion is right:
TL: Du kommst also doch mit.
EN: So you are coming along after all.
[PAUSE:900]
Eben presents a conclusion as inescapable:
TL: Ohne Vollmacht können wir den Antrag eben nicht stellen.
EN: Without authorization, we simply cannot submit the application.
[PAUSE:900]
Schließlich asks the listener to accept a reason as valid:
TL: Wir sollten leiser sprechen; nebenan wird schließlich gearbeitet.
EN: We should speak more quietly; people are working next door, after all.
[PAUSE:1100]
The particle is a tiny instruction about the argument: conclusion, inevitability, or accepted reason.
SHOW: also = conclusion · eben = no escape · schließlich = valid reason
Now turn to surprise, where the contrast is especially sharp.

[CARD]
TITLE: Surprise has two axes
SVG: de_partikeln_c04f.svg
REF: 9.1.1, 9.1.7, 9.1.19, 9.1.33

German separates surprise that a fact is true from surprise at its degree. Ja or doch marks a difference in kind: the state itself contradicts expectation.
TL: Der Saal ist ja leer!
EN: Why, the hall is empty!
[PAUSE:1000]
Aber or unstressed vielleicht marks a difference in degree: the quality is stronger than expected.
TL: Der Saal ist aber groß!
EN: This hall is remarkably large!
[PAUSE:900]
TL: Du hast dich vielleicht verändert!
EN: My, how you have changed!
[PAUSE:1100]
English often lets intonation do this work. German can encode the axis directly.
SHOW: ja / doch = unexpected fact · aber / vielleicht = unexpected degree
Flip for contradiction and concession.

[SIDE]
TITLE: Do not flatten every concession to “but”
REF: 9.1.1, 9.1.2, 9.1.7, 9.1.14, 9.1.18

Within a clause, aber and doch can register contradiction. Allerdings and freilich concede a point, while immerhin rescues a limited positive result.
TL: Das war aber nicht vereinbart.
EN: That was not what we agreed, though.
[PAUSE:800]
TL: Der Vorschlag ist allerdings teuer.
EN: Admittedly, the proposal is expensive.
[PAUSE:800]
TL: Freilich bleibt ein Restrisiko.
EN: Admittedly, a residual risk remains.
[PAUSE:800]
TL: Der Entwurf ist noch roh, aber immerhin brauchbar.
EN: The draft is still rough, but usable at least.
[PAUSE:1100]
Think in discourse moves: oppose, concede, or salvage. The English surface word is secondary.
SHOW: contradiction → aber / doch · concession → allerdings / freilich · salvage → immerhin
Questions bring their own dedicated particle system.

[CARD]
TITLE: Questions can show their conversational history
SVG: de_partikeln_c05f.svg
REF: 9.1.6, 9.1.7, 9.1.10, 9.1.16

Denn connects a question to the situation and often makes a spoken wh-question less bare. Eigentlich opens the underlying question or changes the topic. Doch gleich asks the listener to restore forgotten information.
TL: Wie bist du denn auf diese Lösung gekommen?
EN: So how did you arrive at this solution?
[PAUSE:900]
TL: Was wolltest du eigentlich noch ansprechen?
EN: What else was it that you wanted to bring up?
[PAUSE:900]
TL: Wie hieß die Sachbearbeiterin doch gleich?
EN: What was the caseworker's name again?
[PAUSE:1100]
These particles locate the question in a dialogue: prompted now, underlying all along, or previously answered and forgotten.
SHOW: denn = contextual question · eigentlich = underlying issue · doch gleich = recall
Flip for questions that tilt the answer.

[SIDE]
TITLE: Expose the expected answer
REF: 9.1.13, 9.1.31, 9.1.33, 9.1.34

Etwa and particle-use vielleicht suggest that the feared answer may be yes, although the speaker wants no. Überhaupt challenges the premise itself. Wohl marks genuine uncertainty.
TL: Willst du den Vertrag etwa ungeprüft unterschreiben?
EN: Surely you are not going to sign the contract without checking it?
[PAUSE:850]
TL: Willst du vielleicht behaupten, das sei Zufall?
EN: You don't mean to claim that this is a coincidence, do you?
[PAUSE:850]
TL: Ist das überhaupt rechtlich zulässig?
EN: Is that legally permissible at all?
[PAUSE:850]
TL: Wer könnte wohl dahinterstecken?
EN: I wonder who might be behind it.
[PAUSE:1100]
Before answering such a question, hear its bias: feared confirmation, challenged premise, or open uncertainty.
SHOW: etwa / vielleicht = feared yes · überhaupt = doubtful premise · wohl = uncertainty
Commands make tone visible too.

[CARD]
TITLE: Soften the bare imperative
SVG: de_partikeln_c06f.svg
REF: 9.1.7, 9.1.22

A bare imperative can sound abrupt. Unstressed mal is the everyday softener. Doch adds encouragement or persuasion, and doch mal makes the request relaxed and conversational.
TL: Kommen Sie mal kurz herein.
EN: Please come in for a moment.
[PAUSE:850]
TL: Schau dir doch die Zahlen an.
EN: Do take a look at the figures.
[PAUSE:850]
TL: Frag doch mal bei der Personalstelle nach.
EN: Why don't you check with HR?
[PAUSE:1100]
None of these particles changes the requested action. They renegotiate how much pressure the command places on the listener.
SHOW: bare command → mal softens → doch encourages → doch mal relaxes
Flip for the command particles whose force depends on context.

[SIDE]
TITLE: Warning at one end, permission at the other
REF: 9.1.5, 9.1.19, 9.1.25, 9.1.27

Bloß and stressed ja usually warn. Ruhig grants permission and reassures. Nur can point either way: a stressed negative command threatens, while a calm positive command can encourage.
TL: Vergiss bloß nicht den Anhang!
EN: Just don't forget the attachment!
[PAUSE:800]
TL: Sei ja pünktlich!
EN: You had better be on time!
[PAUSE:800]
TL: Du kannst ruhig früher gehen.
EN: You can go earlier; that's perfectly fine.
[PAUSE:800]
TL: Frag ihn nur; er hilft dir gern.
EN: Go ahead and ask him; he is happy to help.
[PAUSE:1100]
Lexicon alone cannot settle the tone. Stress, polarity, and situation decide whether the same intensifier reassures or threatens.
SHOW: bloß / stressed ja = warning · ruhig = permission · nur = context decides
Next: particles that close off alternatives.

[CARD]
TITLE: Present the situation as inescapable
REF: 9.1.8, 9.1.11, 9.1.17, 9.1.22

Eben and halt present the state of affairs as settled: there is no useful alternative left to debate. Einfach excludes alternatives more directly. Nun einmal is stronger and sounds final.
TL: Der Server ist eben veraltet.
EN: The server is simply outdated; that is the reality.
[PAUSE:800]
TL: Dann müssen wir halt improvisieren.
EN: Then we will just have to improvise.
[PAUSE:800]
TL: Ruf einfach morgen noch einmal an.
EN: Just call again tomorrow.
[PAUSE:800]
TL: Manche Entscheidungen sind nun einmal unbequem.
EN: Some decisions are simply uncomfortable; that is how it is.
[PAUSE:1100]
The family resemblance is closure. The speaker reduces the space for alternatives, from practical simplicity to resignation.
SHOW: einfach = exclude options · eben / halt = accept reality · nun einmal = emphatic closure
Flip for “anyway” and “after all.”

[SIDE]
TITLE: Independent reason or accepted reason?
REF: 9.1.9, 9.1.20, 9.1.26, 9.1.28, 9.1.30

Sowieso, ohnehin, and colloquial eh say that the claim holds regardless of another reason. Jedenfalls preserves a minimum conclusion or instruction. Schließlich supplies a reason the listener should already accept.
TL: Ich fahre sowieso mit dem Zug.
EN: I am taking the train anyway.
[PAUSE:750]
TL: Die Genehmigung wäre ohnehin zu spät gekommen.
EN: The approval would have arrived too late in any case.
[PAUSE:750]
TL: Das wäre eh zu teuer.
EN: That would be too expensive anyway.
[PAUSE:750]
TL: Ruf mich jedenfalls an, sobald du Näheres weißt.
EN: In any event, call me as soon as you know more.
[PAUSE:750]
TL: Wir sollten ihm zuhören; er trägt schließlich die Verantwortung.
EN: We should listen to him; he bears the responsibility, after all.
[PAUSE:1100]
Regardless particles detach the conclusion from a reason. Schließlich does the reverse: it recruits an accepted reason.
SHOW: sowieso / ohnehin / eh = regardless · jedenfalls = minimum · schließlich = accepted reason
Now calibrate probability and concession.

[CARD]
TITLE: Probability versus confidence
REF: 9.1.29, 9.1.34

Unstressed wohl marks an inference as probable rather than certain. Schon looks forward with confidence and often reassures, though a hostile context can turn that confidence into a threat.
TL: Sie steckt wohl noch im Verkehr fest.
EN: She is probably still stuck in traffic.
[PAUSE:850]
TL: Er wird wohl vergessen haben, zurückzurufen.
EN: He has probably forgotten to call back.
[PAUSE:850]
TL: Das wird schon klappen.
EN: It will work out, don't worry.
[PAUSE:850]
TL: Du wirst das schon schaffen.
EN: You will manage it, I'm sure.
[PAUSE:1100]
Wohl lowers commitment. Schon raises confidence in the future result. Both can be translated by intonation instead of a separate English word.
SHOW: wohl = probable inference · schon = confident outcome
Flip for qualified agreement.

[SIDE]
TITLE: Concede, then delimit
REF: 9.1.2, 9.1.14, 9.1.18, 9.1.29, 9.1.34, 9.1.35

Zwar explicitly opens a concession and normally prepares an aber-clause. Stressed wohl or regional schon can perform a similar move. Allerdings and freilich concede more independently; immerhin preserves what remains positive.
TL: Der Ansatz ist zwar elegant, aber schwer skalierbar.
EN: The approach is elegant, but difficult to scale.
[PAUSE:800]
TL: Das ist wohl möglich, aber kaum praktikabel.
EN: That may well be possible, but it is hardly practical.
[PAUSE:800]
TL: Sie hat den Termin bestätigt, allerdings unter Vorbehalt.
EN: She confirmed the appointment, albeit provisionally.
[PAUSE:800]
TL: Es war knapp; immerhin haben wir die Frist eingehalten.
EN: It was close; even so, we met the deadline.
[PAUSE:1100]
Hear the two-part architecture: grant a point, then restrict what follows from it.
SHOW: zwar … aber = concession frame · allerdings / freilich = reservation · immerhin = retained positive
Focus particles now put expectations on a scale.

[CARD]
TITLE: Small words rank time and quantity
REF: 9.1.12, 9.1.21, 9.1.23, 9.1.25, 9.1.29

As focus particles, erst and schon are opposites. Erst says later or less than expected; schon says earlier or more. Nur restricts neutrally, while lediglich is its formal, emphatic relative. Noch adds another amount or reaches a deadline.
TL: Die Sitzung beginnt erst um elf.
EN: The meeting does not begin until eleven.
[PAUSE:750]
TL: Um elf war die Sitzung schon beendet.
EN: By eleven, the meeting was already over.
[PAUSE:750]
TL: Nur die Vorsitzende kann den Antrag zurückziehen.
EN: Only the chair can withdraw the motion.
[PAUSE:750]
TL: Die Änderung betrifft lediglich die Zahlungsfrist.
EN: The amendment concerns only the payment deadline.
[PAUSE:1100]
These uses contribute factual focus. Remove the particle and you remove the restriction or scale, not merely a conversational attitude.
SHOW: erst = later / less · schon = earlier / more · nur / lediglich = restriction
Flip: placement chooses the focused constituent.

[SIDE]
TITLE: Move the particle; move the focus
SVG: de_partikeln_c09b.svg
REF: 9.1.4, 9.1.15, 9.1.23, 9.1.25, 9.1.31

A focus particle normally sits beside what it ranks. Changing its position changes the alternatives under discussion:
TL: Nur Lena hat den Entwurf gelesen.
EN: Only Lena read the draft.
[PAUSE:900]
TL: Lena hat nur den Entwurf gelesen.
EN: Lena read only the draft.
[PAUSE:1000]
Other particles strengthen the edge of a scale. Gar and überhaupt reinforce negatives; noch raises a comparative.
TL: Er hat gar keinen Nachweis vorgelegt.
EN: He did not submit any evidence at all.
[PAUSE:750]
TL: Die Daten sind noch unvollständiger als erwartet.
EN: The data are even more incomplete than expected.
[PAUSE:1000]
Scope is local here: listen for the constituent immediately captured by the particle.
SHOW: adjacency marks focus · gar / überhaupt deepen zero · noch raises a comparison
One final card puts particles into the flow of a conversation.

[CARD]
TITLE: Navigate the conversation
REF: 9.1.3, 9.1.10, 9.1.24, 9.1.32

Eigentlich can reopen the underlying issue or pivot gently. Übrigens marks an incidental aside. Nun can close one topic or demand the next step. Also links the present turn to what came before.
TL: Eigentlich wollte ich noch etwas anderes fragen.
EN: Actually, I wanted to ask something else.
[PAUSE:750]
TL: Übrigens hat Frau Keller bereits zugesagt.
EN: By the way, Ms Keller has already agreed.
[PAUSE:750]
TL: Nun, damit wäre der erste Punkt geklärt.
EN: Well, that settles the first point.
[PAUSE:750]
TL: Du bleibst also bei deinem Einwand?
EN: So you are sticking to your objection?
[PAUSE:1100]
These forms do not merely colour a proposition. They organize turns, topics, and the relation to prior talk.
SHOW: eigentlich = pivot · übrigens = aside · nun = transition · also = link back
Flip for the system in one pass.

[SIDE]
TITLE: Build bundles, not dictionary equations
REF: 9, 9.1.7, 9.1.19, 9.1.22, 9.1.26, 9.1.34, 9.1.35

Particles can stack because each contributes a different instruction. Read the bundle from the dialogue outward: shared ground, correction, probability, mitigation, or focus.
TL: Du hast die Datei doch wohl nicht gelöscht?
EN: Surely you haven't deleted the file, have you?
[PAUSE:850]
TL: Komm doch einfach mal vorbei.
EN: Why don't you just drop by sometime?
[PAUSE:850]
TL: Das weißt du ja ohnehin.
EN: You know that anyway, of course.
[PAUSE:850]
TL: Er ist zwar erschöpft, kommt aber trotzdem mit.
EN: He is exhausted, but he is coming along all the same.
[PAUSE:1100]
Do not force a separate English word for every layer. First identify sentence type and scope; then ask what the speaker assumes, expects, and wants from the listener. Native control grows from hearing these contrasts in context, not from scattering particles everywhere.
SHOW: sentence type → scope → common ground → speaker pressure → register
That is the modal-particle system: small forms, precise social engineering.
