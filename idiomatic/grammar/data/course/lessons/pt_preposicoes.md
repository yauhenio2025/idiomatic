## SCRIPT

[CARD]
TITLE: Choose a preposition from the relationship, not from an English word
REF: 25.1

Portuguese prepositions organize relations such as destination, location, source, purpose, cause, and means. One English word can therefore map to several Portuguese choices.
TL: Vou ao banco. · Estou no banco. · Venho do banco.
EN: I am going to the bank. · I am at the bank. · I come from the bank.
[PAUSE:950]
First identify the relation. Then choose the Portuguese preposition and make any required contraction.
SHOW: destination -> a/para · location -> em · source -> de
Flip to build the four obligatory article contractions.

[SIDE]
TITLE: A, de, em, and por contract with the definite article
REF: 25.1.1, 25.1.3, 25.1.4, 25.1.6

Before a definite article, a forms ao, aos, à, and às; de forms do, dos, da, and das. Em forms no, nos, na, and nas; por forms pelo, pelos, pela, and pelas.
TL: ao mercado · à praia · do Brasil · na escola · pelo caminho
EN: to the market · to the beach · from/of Brazil · at school · along the way
[PAUSE:1000]
Do not leave a plus a as two words. The grave accent in à marks the contraction; it does not mark stress.
SHOW: a + o = ao · a + a = à · de + o = do · em + a = na · por + o = pelo

[CARD]
TITLE: A marks formal destinations, recipients, and fixed points
REF: 25.1.1

In careful usage, a introduces an indirect object and movement to a temporary destination. It also marks positions such as sitting at a table or stopping at a signal.
TL: Entreguei a carta ao diretor. · Fomos ao centro. · Sentaram-se à mesa.
EN: I gave the letter to the director. · We went downtown. · They sat at the table.
[PAUSE:950]
A also appears with clock time, age, rate, distance, and expressions such as daqui a dois dias.
TL: às oito · aos vinte anos · a cem quilômetros por hora · daqui a uma semana
EN: at eight · at age twenty · at one hundred kilometres per hour · in a week
SHOW: recipient/destination/fixed point/time -> a
Flip for the common Brazilian spoken alternatives.

[SIDE]
TITLE: Spoken Brazilian Portuguese often replaces formal a with para or em
REF: 25.1.1, 25.1.4, 25.1.5

For a recipient, conversation often uses para where careful writing uses a. For a temporary destination, speech commonly uses para or em.
TL: Dei o livro para a Ana. · Vou para o centro. · Vou no dentista.
EN: I gave the book to Ana. · I am going downtown. · I am going to the dentist.
[PAUSE:950]
For a permanent destination, para remains the natural choice in every register.
TL: Eles se mudaram para Curitiba.
EN: They moved to Curitiba.
[PAUSE:950]
SHOW: recipient: a formal / para spoken · temporary destination: a formal / para or em spoken · permanent destination: para
Twenty printed-key preposition choices follow.

[CARD]
TITLE: De packages source, possession, type, material, and means
REF: 25.1.3

Use de for origin and separation, and to link a noun to an owner, category, material, measure, or author.
TL: Ela voltou da Bahia. · o carro do Paulo · uma mesa de madeira · um romance de Clarice Lispector
EN: She returned from Bahia. · Paulo's car · a wooden table · a novel by Clarice Lispector
[PAUSE:950]
De also marks means of transport, appearance, cause, and many fixed expressions.
TL: de trem · de casaco · tremer de frio · de propósito
EN: by train · in a coat · shiver with cold · on purpose
SHOW: source/owner/type/material/means/cause -> de
Flip to separate accompaniment from location.

[SIDE]
TITLE: Com adds accompaniment, instrument, circumstance, or attitude
REF: 25.1.2

Com links an event to a companion or instrument. It can also introduce an accompanying condition.
TL: Viajei com amigos. · Cortei o papel com uma tesoura. · Dormiu com a janela aberta.
EN: I travelled with friends. · I cut the paper with scissors. · He slept with the window open.
[PAUSE:950]
With reactions and attitudes, Portuguese may use com where English uses at, to, or about.
TL: Ficamos contentes com o resultado. · Ela foi gentil comigo.
EN: We were pleased with the result. · She was kind to me.
[PAUSE:950]
SHOW: companion/instrument/circumstance/attitude -> com · source/type/cause -> de

[CARD]
TITLE: Em anchors an event in place or calendar time
REF: 25.1.4

Em is the general location preposition and can correspond to in, at, or on. It contracts with both definite and indefinite articles.
TL: Moro no Recife. · Trabalho numa escola. · O livro está na mesa.
EN: I live in Recife. · I work at a school. · The book is on the table.
[PAUSE:950]
Use em with years and months, and normally with seasons, dates, and days of the week.
TL: em 2026 · em agosto · no inverno · na quinta-feira
EN: in 2026 · in August · in winter · on Thursday
[PAUSE:950]
SHOW: general location · year/month · season/date/day -> em
Flip to contrast purpose and destination with cause and route.

[SIDE]
TITLE: Para points to a goal; por supplies a path, cause, means, or exchange
REF: 25.1.5, 25.1.6

Para presents a beneficiary, purpose, deadline, standard, or lasting destination. Before an infinitive it commonly means in order to.
TL: Isto é para você. · Saí para comprar pão. · O relatório é para amanhã.
EN: This is for you. · I went out to buy bread. · The report is due tomorrow.
[PAUSE:950]
Por presents an agent, means, route, cause, exchange, duration, or rate.
TL: escrito por ela · por e-mail · passar pelo parque · trocar por outro · duas vezes por dia
EN: written by her · by email · go through the park · exchange for another · twice a day
[PAUSE:1000]
SHOW: goal/beneficiary/deadline -> para · path/cause/means/exchange/rate -> por

[CARD]
TITLE: Time expressions divide work among a, de, em, por, and durante
REF: 25.1.1, 25.1.3, 25.1.4, 25.1.6, 25.2

Use a with clock time and age, de with parts of the day in fixed expressions, and em with months and years.
TL: às nove · aos dezoito anos · de manhã · em setembro · em 1999
EN: at nine · at age eighteen · in the morning · in September · in 1999
[PAUSE:950]
Use por for a rate or a bounded period viewed as duration. Durante explicitly means during or for.
TL: três vezes por semana · ficou lá por um mês · durante a reunião
EN: three times a week · stayed there for a month · during the meeting
[PAUSE:950]
SHOW: clock/age -> a · part of day -> de/a · month/year -> em · rate/duration -> por/durante
Sixteen time-expression translations follow.

[SIDE]
TITLE: Desde sets a starting point; até sets an endpoint or limit
REF: 25.2

Desde means since or starting from. A continuing situation normally stays in the Portuguese present tense.
TL: Trabalho aqui desde janeiro. · Vai de segunda a sexta.
EN: I have worked here since January. · It runs from Monday to Friday.
[PAUSE:950]
Até marks an endpoint in time or space and can also mean up to or even.
TL: Fico até amanhã. · Caminhamos até a praça. · Custa até cem reais.
EN: I am staying until tomorrow. · We walked as far as the square. · It costs up to one hundred reais.
[PAUSE:950]
SHOW: starting boundary -> desde/de · endpoint/limit -> até/a

[CARD]
TITLE: Simple prepositions encode relations beyond the basic six
REF: 25.2

Use entre for between or among, contra for opposition or contact, sem for absence, and sobre for a physical position above or a topic.
TL: entre amigos · contra a parede · sem dinheiro · um debate sobre educação
EN: among friends · against the wall · without money · a debate about education
[PAUSE:950]
Sob means under, especially in figurative settings. Segundo and conforme can attribute information or alignment.
TL: sob pressão · segundo a imprensa · conforme o combinado
EN: under pressure · according to the press · as agreed
[PAUSE:950]
SHOW: relation/opposition/absence/topic/control/source -> entre/contra/sem/sobre/sob/segundo
Six clean choices follow; the malformed tail is omitted.

[SIDE]
TITLE: Register distinguishes everyday items from formal alternatives
REF: 25.2

Common prose uses depois de, até, segundo, sem, and sob. Careful or institutional writing may also use após, ante, mediante, perante, salvo, or visto.
TL: após a reunião · mediante pagamento · perante a lei · salvo engano
EN: after the meeting · upon payment · before the law · unless I am mistaken
[PAUSE:1000]
Choose a formal item only when the surrounding register supports it; the relation itself still determines the meaning.
SHOW: neutral conversation -> common preposition · institutional/literary context -> formal alternative

[CARD]
TITLE: Compound prepositions locate one thing relative to another
REF: 25.3

Many spatial relations use a fixed phrase ending in de or a. Contract that final preposition with any following article.
TL: dentro da caixa · fora do prédio · atrás da porta · em frente ao hotel
EN: inside the box · outside the building · behind the door · in front of the hotel
[PAUSE:950]
Vertical and distance relations follow the same pattern.
TL: acima da média · embaixo da cama · longe de casa · perto da estação
EN: above average · under the bed · far from home · near the station
[PAUSE:950]
SHOW: compound + de/a + article -> contraction at the right edge
Flip for compounds that add route or extent.

[SIDE]
TITLE: Por plus a spatial noun highlights route, extent, or an intervening area
REF: 25.3

Por baixo de, por cima de, and por trás de often suggest movement across or along a space rather than a static point.
TL: passar por baixo da ponte · voar por cima da cidade · correr por trás da casa
EN: pass under the bridge · fly over the city · run behind the house
[PAUSE:950]
Compare static embaixo de with route-oriented por baixo de. Context, not English under alone, selects the phrase.
SHOW: static location -> embaixo de · route/extent -> por baixo de
Sixteen compound-preposition translations follow.

[CARD]
TITLE: Compound prepositions also organize cause, contrast, and viewpoint
REF: 25.5

Por causa de gives a cause, apesar de concedes a contrary circumstance, and em vez de replaces one option with another.
TL: por causa da chuva · apesar do atraso · em vez de café
EN: because of the rain · despite the delay · instead of coffee
[PAUSE:950]
Em nome de marks representation; de acordo com attributes agreement or a source; em relação a frames a topic.
TL: em nome da equipe · de acordo com o contrato · em relação ao projeto
EN: on behalf of the team · according to the contract · regarding the project
[PAUSE:950]
SHOW: cause/contrast/replacement/representation/source/topic -> fixed compound
Flip for compound time expressions.

[SIDE]
TITLE: Antes de and depois de place an event around a reference point
REF: 25.4

Antes de and depois de take a noun or an infinitive. When a new explicit subject belongs to the infinitive, Portuguese can use the personal infinitive.
TL: antes do almoço · depois de sair · antes de eles chegarem
EN: before lunch · after leaving · before they arrive
[PAUSE:950]
Após is a compact formal alternative to depois de. Written quando de means at the time of.
TL: após a votação · quando da mudança
EN: after the vote · at the time of the change
[PAUSE:950]
SHOW: before -> antes de · after -> depois de/após · formal time reference -> quando de

[CARD]
TITLE: Formal rewriting exposes the spoken a-para-em alternation
REF: 25.1.1, 25.1.4, 25.1.5

In edited prose, a is preferred for a temporary destination and many indirect objects. It is also preferred for fixed positions such as à mesa and ao sinal.
TL: ir ao cinema · perguntar ao professor · sentar-se à mesa · parar ao sinal
EN: go to the cinema · ask the teacher · sit at the table · stop at the signal
[PAUSE:950]
In conversation, ir no cinema, perguntar para o professor, and sentar na mesa are widespread Brazilian patterns. A formal-rewrite task tests register, not whether the spoken form communicates.
SHOW: spoken para/em -> formal a where the construction requires it
Ten printed-key register replacements follow.

[SIDE]
TITLE: Solve preposition choices in four passes
REF: 25.1, 25.2, 25.3, 25.4, 25.5

First name the relation: destination, location, source, goal, cause, route, time boundary, or discourse link. Next decide whether the phrase is simple or fixed.
[PAUSE:900]
Then select the register. Finally contract the preposition with the article or demonstrative when required.
TL: Vou ao trabalho. · Estou no trabalho. · Volto do trabalho. · Fiz isso para o trabalho. · Passei pelo trabalho.
EN: I go to work. · I am at work. · I return from work. · I did this for work. · I passed by work.
[PAUSE:1000]
SHOW: relation -> fixed phrase -> register -> contraction

[CARD]
TITLE: Keep the highest-value contrasts visible
REF: 25.1, 25.2, 25.3, 25.5

Use a or para for destinations according to permanence and register, but em for location. Use para for a goal and por for a cause, route, exchange, or rate.
TL: mudar para Lisboa · ir ao mercado · estar no mercado · trabalhar para viver · faltar por doença
EN: move to Lisbon · go to the market · be at the market · work in order to live · be absent because of illness
[PAUSE:1000]
Treat compounds as whole units and make the contraction only after retrieving the unit.
TL: apesar da chuva · em frente à escola · por causa do trânsito
EN: despite the rain · in front of the school · because of the traffic
SHOW: a/para vs em · para vs por · fixed compound first, contraction second
Flip for a final register check.

[SIDE]
TITLE: Variation is part of the system
REF: 25.1, 25.2, 25.5

Brazilian speech often prefers para for recipients and para or em for destinations where formal prose uses a. It also uses em for several affected-person constructions.
TL: Contei para ela. · Fui na farmácia. · Dei um abraço nele.
EN: I told her. · I went to the pharmacy. · I gave him a hug.
[PAUSE:950]
Learn the formal target and the spoken alternative together. That keeps register choice separate from grammaticality and meaning.
SHOW: formal target + spoken alternative -> one Brazilian Portuguese system
