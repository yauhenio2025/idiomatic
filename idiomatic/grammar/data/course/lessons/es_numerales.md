## SCRIPT

[CARD]
TITLE: Build cardinal numbers in stable layers
REF: 11.1

The forms from sixteen through twenty-nine are normally written as single words. From thirty-one through ninety-nine, tens and units are linked with y.
TL: El presupuesto asciende a veintidós millones treinta y cuatro mil euros.
EN: The budget amounts to twenty-two million thirty-four thousand euros.
[PAUSE:900]
Hundreds form another layer. Remember the irregular stems quinientos, setecientos and novecientos, and do not insert y between a hundred and the following tens.
TL: El archivo contiene setecientos cuarenta y seis documentos.
EN: The archive contains seven hundred and forty-six documents.
SHOW: dieciséis–veintinueve · treinta y uno · setecientos cuarenta y seis
Flip for thousands and dates.

[SIDE]
TITLE: Mil does not behave like an English noun phrase
REF: 11.1, 11.16

Use mil, not un mil, for an exact thousand in ordinary counting. Higher quantities place their multiplier directly before mil.
TL: La encuesta reunió mil respuestas en dos mil veintiséis.
EN: The survey collected one thousand responses in 2026.
[PAUSE:900]
Large figures can be grouped in threes for readability, but years, postal codes and street numbers stay ungrouped. When reading a year, treat it as a cardinal number rather than splitting it into English-style pairs.
TL: La cifra pasó de dos mil cien a doce mil cuatrocientos en un año.
EN: The figure rose from 2,100 to 12,400 in one year.
[PAUSE:1000]
SHOW: 1 000 = mil · 12 400 = doce mil cuatrocientos · 2026 = dos mil veintiséis
Five connected number-reading tasks follow.

[CARD]
TITLE: Uno changes beside a noun
REF: 11.3, 11.5

Uno becomes un before a masculine noun, while feminine reference selects una. The same contrast appears inside compounds ending in one.
TL: Recibimos veintiún informes y veintiuna solicitudes.
EN: We received twenty-one reports and twenty-one applications.
[PAUSE:1000]
When no noun follows, retain uno. A masculine noun beginning with a stressed a sound takes un, and a feminine one takes una only when the ordinary article pattern permits it.
TL: Necesitábamos treinta y uno, pero llegaron treinta y un paquetes.
EN: We needed thirty-one, but thirty-one packages arrived.
SHOW: alone: uno · masculine noun: un · feminine noun: una
Flip for hundreds.

[SIDE]
TITLE: Hundreds agree with the counted noun
REF: 11.2, 11.3

From doscientos upward, the hundreds element agrees in gender with the noun counted. This agreement remains visible even when mil follows.
TL: Asistieron doscientas personas y se enviaron quinientos mensajes.
EN: Two hundred people attended and five hundred messages were sent.
[PAUSE:900]
The words ciento and cien themselves do not alternate for gender. When a numeral is treated as a symbol or name, it is grammatically masculine.
TL: Escribió un siete donde debía ir un nueve.
EN: She wrote a seven where a nine should have gone.
[PAUSE:1000]
SHOW: doscientas páginas · quinientos archivos · el siete
Flip for the boundary at one hundred.

[CARD]
TITLE: Cien closes at exactly one hundred
REF: 11.6

Use cien before a noun and before mil or millones when it multiplies them. Modern usage also keeps cien when the number stands alone.
TL: Vendieron cien entradas y reservaron cien mil euros.
EN: They sold one hundred tickets and set aside one hundred thousand euros.
[PAUSE:1000]
Use ciento when another number follows inside the same cardinal expression.
TL: El informe tiene ciento dieciocho páginas.
EN: The report has one hundred and eighteen pages.
SHOW: cien páginas · cien mil · ciento dieciocho
Flip for approximate hundreds.

[SIDE]
TITLE: Cientos de names an indefinite multitude
REF: 11.2, 11.6

Plural cientos de does not state an exact figure. It presents an approximate quantity and takes de before the following noun.
TL: Cientos de vecinos participaron en la consulta.
EN: Hundreds of residents took part in the consultation.
[PAUSE:900]
This noun-like use is masculine even before a feminine plural noun. Keep it distinct from an exact agreeing cardinal such as doscientas consultas.
TL: Los cientos de páginas revisadas contenían doscientas correcciones.
EN: The hundreds of pages reviewed contained two hundred corrections.
[PAUSE:1000]
SHOW: exact: doscientas páginas · approximate: cientos de páginas
Nine cien-or-ciento decisions follow.

[CARD]
TITLE: Millón is a noun and introduces de
REF: 11.4

Millón, billón and trillón are masculine nouns. Before a following noun phrase they require de, and their grammatical number belongs to the numeral noun.
TL: Un millón de personas recibió la notificación.
EN: One million people received the notification.
[PAUSE:900]
Below a full million, mil connects directly to the counted noun without de.
TL: La campaña alcanzó a novecientas mil personas.
EN: The campaign reached nine hundred thousand people.
[PAUSE:1000]
SHOW: novecientas mil personas · un millón de personas
Flip for billion-scale values.

[SIDE]
TITLE: Spanish billón belongs to the long scale
REF: 11.4

In standard Spanish, mil millones expresses the quantity called one billion in current English. A Spanish billón is one million millions.
TL: La inversión superó los dos mil millones de euros.
EN: The investment exceeded two billion euros.
[PAUSE:1000]
For high-stakes figures, state the digits or clarify the scale rather than assuming that similar-looking words carry the same value.
TL: El documento define un billón como un millón de millones.
EN: The document defines a Spanish billón as a million millions.
SHOW: 10^9 = mil millones · 10^12 = un billón
Flip for ordinals.

[CARD]
TITLE: Everyday ordinals agree with their noun
REF: 11.12.1

The ordinary forms primero through décimo vary for gender and number. Primero and tercero lose their final o before a masculine singular noun.
TL: La primera fase terminó durante el tercer trimestre.
EN: The first phase ended during the third quarter.
[PAUSE:900]
An ordinal normally precedes its noun, although a following position is possible in established or classificatory uses.
TL: Revisaremos primero las tres últimas páginas.
EN: We will first review the last three pages.
[PAUSE:1000]
SHOW: el primer intento · la primera vez · el tercer capítulo
Flip for ordinals above tenth.

[SIDE]
TITLE: Higher positions often use cardinal forms
REF: 11.12.2, 11.12.3

Formal Spanish has special ordinals above tenth, but everyday usage frequently substitutes a cardinal number, especially for editions, meetings and anniversaries.
TL: Participó en la reunión número veintidós del comité.
EN: She took part in the committee's twenty-second meeting.
[PAUSE:900]
When a compound ordinal is written as separate words, its elements agree with the noun. A joined form carries agreement on its ending.
TL: Esta es la vigésima segunda sesión, también llamada la sesión veintidós.
EN: This is the twenty-second session, also called session twenty-two.
[PAUSE:1000]
SHOW: formal: vigésima segunda · everyday alternative: número veintidós
Eight ordinal translations follow.

[CARD]
TITLE: Digits and words divide the workload
REF: 11.16

Digits are efficient for long figures, decimals, dates, addresses and values attached to abbreviated units. Words suit short and round quantities, approximations and numbers quoted as speech.
TL: La muestra pesó 2,8 kg y contenía quince piezas.
EN: The sample weighed 2.8 kilograms and contained fifteen pieces.
[PAUSE:900]
Do not mix the conventions carelessly within one expression. The surrounding genre—technical table, prose, address or dialogue—guides the choice.
TL: Me aseguró que había llamado cien veces.
EN: He assured me that he had called a hundred times.
SHOW: technical value: 2,8 kg · ordinary prose: quince piezas · approximation: cien veces
Flip for decimal punctuation and telephone numbers.

[SIDE]
TITLE: Reading conventions vary by region and purpose
REF: 11.1, 11.17

Much of the Spanish-speaking world writes a decimal comma and reads the whole part, coma, then the decimal value. Mexico and parts of Central America commonly use the point instead.
TL: La distancia exacta es de tres coma cuarenta y cinco kilómetros.
EN: The exact distance is 3.45 kilometres.
[PAUSE:900]
Telephone numbers are often grouped into pairs, but reading each digit separately is always a clear option. Postal codes are likewise often read digit by digit.
TL: El código termina en cero siete cuatro dos.
EN: The code ends in zero seven four two.
[PAUSE:1000]
SHOW: decimal: tres coma cuarenta y cinco · code: cero siete cuatro dos
Flip for collective quantities.

[CARD]
TITLE: Collective numerals package approximate groups
REF: 11.8

Forms such as una decena, una veintena, un centenar and un millar name a group, often approximately. They connect to what is counted with de.
TL: Una veintena de especialistas evaluó la propuesta.
EN: About twenty specialists evaluated the proposal.
[PAUSE:900]
As collective nouns, these expressions commonly take a singular verb even when the following noun is plural.
TL: Un centenar de solicitudes quedó pendiente.
EN: About a hundred applications remained pending.
[PAUSE:1000]
SHOW: una docena de · una veintena de · un centenar de · un millar de
Flip for loose quantities and articles.

[SIDE]
TITLE: Articles depend on the number's syntactic role
REF: 11.8, 11.11

Use an article when a number identifies a numbered item or when a percentage functions as a bounded noun phrase. Plain ages and unbounded price ranges normally omit it.
TL: Vive en el doce y tiene cuarenta y dos años.
EN: She lives at number twelve and is forty-two years old.
[PAUSE:900]
The informal pattern y pico presents a quantity slightly above the stated round number.
TL: Llegaron treinta y pico mensajes durante la mañana.
EN: Thirty-something messages arrived during the morning.
[PAUSE:1000]
SHOW: el doce · cuarenta y dos años · treinta y pico
Flip for fractions.

[CARD]
TITLE: Common fractions have compact noun forms
REF: 11.10

Everyday Spanish uses mitad for a half, tercio for a third and cuarto for a quarter. From fifth through tenth, masculine ordinal forms are common in mathematical contexts.
TL: Dos tercios del equipo aprobaron la propuesta.
EN: Two-thirds of the team approved the proposal.
[PAUSE:900]
In ordinary prose, parte expressions often sound more natural than a technical fractional noun.
TL: Solo la cuarta parte del presupuesto estaba disponible.
EN: Only a quarter of the budget was available.
[PAUSE:1000]
SHOW: la mitad · un tercio · dos tercios · la cuarta parte
Flip for small and complex fractions.

[SIDE]
TITLE: Parte and -avo extend the fraction system
REF: 11.10, 11.12.2

Hundredths, thousandths and millionths can use ordinal-based nouns or feminine forms understood with parte. Technical language can derive further denominators with -avo.
TL: El margen fue de una centésima de punto.
EN: The margin was one hundredth of a point.
[PAUSE:900]
Medio is an agreeing adjective, while mitad is a noun. Cuarto can appear directly before a measure or introduce it with de.
TL: Necesitamos media hora y un cuarto de litro de agua.
EN: We need half an hour and a quarter of a litre of water.
[PAUSE:1000]
SHOW: medio/a + noun · la mitad de · un cuarto de · un milésimo de
Eight fraction-matching tasks follow.

[CARD]
TITLE: Arithmetic uses a small set of connectors
REF: 11.9

Addition uses y or más, multiplication uses por, subtraction uses menos, and division uses dividido por or entre. The result is commonly linked with son.
TL: Nueve por seis son cincuenta y cuatro.
EN: Nine times six is fifty-four.
[PAUSE:900]
Powers and roots use lexical expressions rather than English word order.
TL: Ocho es el cubo de dos y tres es la raíz cuadrada de nueve.
EN: Eight is the cube of two and three is the square root of nine.
[PAUSE:1000]
SHOW: más · menos · por · dividido por/entre · son
Flip for percentages.

[SIDE]
TITLE: Percentages form noun phrases
REF: 11.7, 11.11

Por ciento is the recommended general expression. Por cien is widespread in speech, while cien por cien can mean completely or one hundred percent.
TL: El cuarenta por ciento de las respuestas fue favorable.
EN: Forty percent of the responses was favourable.
[PAUSE:900]
A percentage may appear with a definite or indefinite article when it forms a bounded quantity. Agreement can follow the percentage as a unit or the plural complement, depending on how the speaker construes it.
TL: Un sesenta por ciento de los participantes llegó a tiempo.
EN: Sixty percent of the participants arrived on time.
[PAUSE:1000]
SHOW: por ciento · un/el + porcentaje · cien por cien
Flip for dimensions.

[CARD]
TITLE: Dimensions select a measure construction
REF: 11.15

Use medir for dimensions, ser de for an area, or tener plus a measure plus de largo, ancho or profundidad for one dimension.
TL: La sala mide cuatro coma dos por seis metros.
EN: The room measures 4.2 by 6 metres.
[PAUSE:900]
Square and cubic units follow the measure noun. Do not translate English compounds word for word.
TL: El depósito tiene una capacidad de doce metros cúbicos.
EN: The tank has a capacity of twelve cubic metres.
[PAUSE:1000]
SHOW: mide X por Y · metros cuadrados · metros cúbicos · X metros de ancho
Ten integrated numerical-expression tasks follow.

[SIDE]
TITLE: Temperatures and classifications are lexical
REF: 11.15

Temperatures below zero use bajo cero or menos before the value. Angles and powers have their own compact constructions.
TL: La temperatura descendió a ocho grados bajo cero.
EN: The temperature fell to eight degrees below zero.
[PAUSE:900]
Even, odd and prime numbers are pares, impares and primos. A right angle is un ángulo recto.
TL: Marca los números impares y dibuja un ángulo recto.
EN: Mark the odd numbers and draw a right angle.
[PAUSE:1000]
SHOW: bajo cero · pares/impares/primos · un ángulo recto
Flip for distribution and multiples.

[CARD]
TITLE: Distribution repeats the counting frame
REF: 11.13

Cada plus a number gives a recurring interval. De plus a repeated numeral distributes people or objects into equal groups or steps.
TL: Revisamos el sistema cada tres meses.
EN: We review the system every three months.
[PAUSE:900]
TL: Los asistentes entraron de dos en dos.
EN: The attendees entered two by two.
[PAUSE:1000]
The literary adjective sendos means one each, but cada uno is clearer in ordinary conversation.
SHOW: cada tres meses · de dos en dos · cada uno
Flip for multiples.

[SIDE]
TITLE: Double and triple can be nouns or operations
REF: 11.14

El doble and el triple compare quantities. The verbs duplicar and triplicar describe the operation that produces the new value.
TL: La segunda oferta es el doble de la primera.
EN: The second offer is twice the first.
[PAUSE:900]
Spanish chooses lexical expressions for many English compounds: individual for a single room, de ida for a one-way ticket, and de matrimonio for a double bed.
TL: Reservamos una habitación individual y duplicamos el plazo.
EN: We booked a single room and doubled the deadline.
[PAUSE:1000]
SHOW: el doble de · el triple de · duplicar · triplicar
End of the numerals lesson.
