## SCRIPT

[CARD]
TITLE: Choose the relationship before choosing the verb
REF: 23.1, 23.2, 23.3, 23.4

Portuguese divides English be among three common relationships. Ser identifies or classifies the subject, estar presents a state or movable location, and ficar often marks a fixed location or a transition.
TL: A Lúcia é médica. · A Lúcia está no consultório. · O consultório fica no centro.
EN: Lúcia is a doctor. · Lúcia is at the office. · The office is downtown.
[PAUSE:900]
The contrast is about how the speaker frames the information, not simply how long it lasts.
SHOW: identity or class -> ser · state or movable location -> estar · fixed location or change -> ficar
Flip to use permanence as a clue without turning it into a mechanical rule.

[SIDE]
TITLE: Inherent, current, and changed are three different viewpoints
REF: 23.2.3, 23.3.1, 23.4.2

Ser commonly presents an inherent or defining characteristic. Estar presents the subject's condition at a particular point, while ficar presents entry into a new condition.
TL: Ela é tranquila. · Ela está tranquila. · Ela ficou tranquila.
EN: She is a calm person. · She is calm now. · She became calm.
[PAUSE:900]
A current state can last a long time, and a defining role can change. Identify the viewpoint before judging duration.
TL: Ele é professor, mas está desempregado. · A cidade ficou silenciosa.
EN: He is a teacher, but he is unemployed. · The city became quiet.
[PAUSE:950]
SHOW: defining quality -> ser · observed condition -> estar · transition/result -> ficar

[CARD]
TITLE: Ser identifies, defines, and classifies
REF: 23.2.1, 23.2.2

Use ser before a noun that says who or what the subject is. A profession, nationality, or religion normally appears without an indefinite article.
TL: Esse é o meu marido. · Sou jornalista. · Elas são brasileiras.
EN: This is my husband. · I am a journalist. · They are Brazilian.
[PAUSE:900]
In an identifying equation with a personal pronoun, ser agrees with the expression that follows it.
TL: Quem é? Sou eu. · O maior problema são os mosquitos.
EN: Who is it? It is me. · The biggest problem is the mosquitoes.
[PAUSE:950]
SHOW: identity or category -> ser · profession/nationality -> normally no um or uma
Flip for descriptions, occasions, and measures.

[SIDE]
TITLE: Ser also gives defining qualities, event time, and measures
REF: 23.2.3, 23.2.4, 23.2.6, 23.2.7

With an adjective, ser can present an inherent characteristic or describe how someone behaved on a particular occasion.
TL: Os irmãos são altos. · Ela foi muito simpática comigo.
EN: The brothers are tall. · She was very nice to me.
[PAUSE:900]
Use ser to identify when an event occurs and in expressions of clock time, dates, or distance.
TL: O casamento é em junho. · Hoje é dia oito. · São cinco quilômetros até a praia.
EN: The wedding is in June. · Today is the eighth. · It is five kilometres to the beach.
[PAUSE:950]
SHOW: defining description · event time · clock/date/distance -> ser

[CARD]
TITLE: Estar presents a state, perception, or movable location
REF: 23.3.1, 23.3.2, 23.3.4, 23.3.5, 23.3.6

Use estar for a current physical or emotional condition and for the speaker's perception of appearance, taste, feel, or weather.
TL: Estamos cansados. · A sopa está salgada. · Está frio hoje.
EN: We are tired. · The soup tastes salty. · It is cold today.
[PAUSE:900]
Estar also locates people and movable things and reports a current level or quoted price.
TL: A chave está na mesa. · O dólar está a cinco reais.
EN: The key is on the table. · The dollar is at five reais.
[PAUSE:950]
SHOW: current state/perception · movable location · current level -> estar
Flip for nouns and participles after estar.

[SIDE]
TITLE: A temporary metaphor or resulting condition can follow estar
REF: 23.3.3, 23.3.7

A noun can follow estar when it works metaphorically as a temporary assessment rather than an identity.
TL: Eu estava um caco. · A cozinha está um brinco.
EN: I was a wreck. · The kitchen is spotless.
[PAUSE:900]
Estar plus a past participle inspects the state left by an earlier event. It does not itself narrate the action.
TL: O serviço está incluído. · As portas estão fechadas.
EN: Service is included. · The doors are closed.
[PAUSE:950]
SHOW: temporary metaphor -> estar + noun · resulting state -> estar + participle
Twenty clean three-verb choices follow.

[CARD]
TITLE: Fixed places take ser or ficar; temporary locations take estar
REF: 23.2.5, 23.3.5, 23.4.1

For a building, geographical feature, or another thing that does not normally move, Portuguese can use ser or ficar to give its permanent location.
TL: Onde é a Biblioteca Nacional? · Porto Alegre fica no sul do Brasil.
EN: Where is the National Library? · Porto Alegre is in southern Brazil.
[PAUSE:900]
For a person, animal, vehicle, key, or other movable thing at a particular location, use estar.
TL: O Sérgio está em casa. · Os talheres estão na mesa.
EN: Sérgio is at home. · The cutlery is on the table.
[PAUSE:950]
SHOW: fixed location -> ser/ficar · person or movable thing now -> estar
Flip to separate an event from its participants and objects.

[SIDE]
TITLE: An event is with ser; its participants are with estar
REF: 23.2.6, 23.3.5

When the subject is an event, ser identifies where or when it takes place. When the subject is a person or movable object, estar reports its location.
TL: A ópera é no Teatro Municipal. · Os músicos estão no teatro.
EN: The opera is at the Municipal Theatre. · The musicians are at the theatre.
[PAUSE:900]
Locate the grammatical subject before choosing. A wedding takes place in June, while its guests are at the venue.
TL: O casamento vai ser em junho. · Os convidados já estão no salão.
EN: The wedding will be in June. · The guests are already in the hall.
[PAUSE:950]
SHOW: event place/time -> ser · participant/object location -> estar

[CARD]
TITLE: Ficar marks a transition and the resulting appearance
REF: 23.4.2, 23.4.3, 23.4.4, 23.4.5

Before an adjective, ficar usually means to get or become. It presents the change that leads into a state, often as a reaction.
TL: O chefe vai ficar furioso. · Não fique triste.
EN: The boss is going to get furious. · Do not get sad.
[PAUSE:900]
Ficar can also report how something turns out, including a metaphorical noun, a perceived result, or an involuntary passive result.
TL: O cabelo ficou um lixo. · A sopa ficou gostosa. · Ficamos presos no elevador.
EN: The hair became a mess. · The soup turned out tasty. · We got stuck in the lift.
[PAUSE:950]
SHOW: entry into state · resultant appearance/taste · involuntary result -> ficar
Flip for the three possible perspectives on a past participle.

[SIDE]
TITLE: Ser, estar, and ficar plus a participle answer different questions
REF: 23.2.8, 23.3.7, 23.4.5

Ser plus a participle forms an eventive passive and says what was done. Estar plus a participle describes the state left afterward.
TL: A porta foi fechada às nove. · Às dez, a porta estava fechada.
EN: The door was closed at nine. · At ten, the door was closed.
[PAUSE:900]
Ficar plus a participle highlights entering a result, especially when that result is unintended or experienced by the subject.
TL: A porta ficou bloqueada. · Os passageiros ficaram presos.
EN: The door became blocked. · The passengers got trapped.
[PAUSE:950]
SHOW: action/event -> ser · existing result -> estar · entry into result -> ficar

[CARD]
TITLE: The imperfect sets a past scene with era, estava, or ficava
REF: 23.6.1, 23.6.2, 23.6.3

Use era for identity, defining characteristics, clock time, and fixed location in the background of a story. Use estava for a temporary state, perception, or movable location.
TL: Era inverno e estava frio. · A chave estava na fechadura.
EN: It was winter, and it was cold. · The key was in the lock.
[PAUSE:900]
Ficava can replace era for the fixed location of something that does not normally move.
TL: O banheiro era do outro lado. · O hotel ficava longe da praia.
EN: The bathroom was on the other side. · The hotel was far from the beach.
[PAUSE:950]
SHOW: background identity/fixed fact -> era · temporary scene/location -> estava · fixed place -> ficava
Flip when being itself is the bounded event.

[SIDE]
TITLE: The preterite reports a bounded stay, event, behavior, or reaction
REF: 23.6.4, 23.6.5, 23.6.6

Estive reports a state or location for a finite past period. Foi reports a one-off event, a person's behavior on an occasion, or a finite period of identity.
TL: Estive em São Paulo semana passada. · A prova foi difícil. · O atendente foi grosso.
EN: I was in São Paulo last week. · The test was difficult. · The attendant was rude.
[PAUSE:900]
Fiquei reports the transition into an emotional response rather than background emotion.
TL: Fiquei contente com a notícia. · Ficamos maravilhados com a vista.
EN: I was delighted by the news. · We were amazed by the view.
[PAUSE:950]
SHOW: bounded stay/state -> estive · one-off event/behavior -> foi · reaction -> fiquei

[CARD]
TITLE: English has been does not select one Portuguese tense
REF: 23.6.4, 23.6.5, 23.6.7, 23.6.8

A completed visit normally takes the preterite of estar. Ser in the preterite can likewise occur with já, sempre, or nunca for experience through the present.
TL: Já estive em Paris duas vezes. · Ela sempre foi simpática comigo.
EN: I have been to Paris twice. · She has always been friendly to me.
[PAUSE:900]
Use tem estado for repeated temporary states or locations continuing to now, and tem sido for the continuing nature or quality of a person or thing.
TL: Tem estado muito quente. · Não tem sido fácil.
EN: It has been very hot lately. · It has not been easy.
[PAUSE:950]
SHOW: completed experience -> esteve/foi · repeated temporary pattern -> tem estado · continuing nature -> tem sido
Flip for a compact past-time diagnostic.

[SIDE]
TITLE: Ask whether the past expression is background, bounded, or recurring
REF: 23.6

Use the imperfect to describe what already held in the scene. Use the preterite when the state, visit, event, behavior, or reaction is itself reported as a bounded occurrence.
TL: A festa estava chata. · O evento foi um sucesso. · Estivemos com Paulo ontem.
EN: The party was boring. · The event was a success. · We were with Paulo yesterday.
[PAUSE:900]
Use the compound present perfect only for the recurring or continuing viewpoint described by tem estado or tem sido.
TL: Minha vida tem sido estressante ultimamente.
EN: My life has been stressful lately.
[PAUSE:950]
SHOW: scene/background -> imperfect · bounded occurrence -> preterite · recurrence/continuation to now -> compound perfect

[CARD]
TITLE: Ser com and ser de build useful idiomatic relationships
REF: 23.7.1, 23.7.2

Ser com can mean that something is directed at someone, belongs to someone's department, or is their responsibility or strength.
TL: É comigo? · Abrir conta é com o gerente. · Futebol não é comigo.
EN: Do you mean me? · Opening an account is for the manager. · Soccer is not my strong point.
[PAUSE:900]
Ser de expresses origin, material, ownership, or a person's typical inclination.
TL: Sou de Recife. · O bule é de prata. · O Zé não é de beber.
EN: I am from Recife. · The teapot is silver. · Zé is not one to drink.
[PAUSE:950]
SHOW: directed to/responsibility -> ser com · origin/material/ownership/habit -> ser de
Flip for obligation and emphasis with ser.

[SIDE]
TITLE: É para, é, and é que organize obligation and emphasis
REF: 23.7.3, 23.7.4, 23.7.5

É para plus a person and infinitive informally states what someone is supposed to do.
TL: É para pagar primeiro? · Era para todos chegarem às nove.
EN: Are we supposed to pay first? · Everyone was supposed to arrive at nine.
[PAUSE:900]
É can follow the first verb for emphasis, while é que can stand between a focused subject and its verb.
TL: Quero é voltar para casa. · Eu é que tenho que pagar.
EN: What I want is to go home. · I am the one who has to pay.
[PAUSE:950]
SHOW: supposed to -> é para · focus a complement -> verb + é · focus a subject -> é que

[CARD]
TITLE: Estar com presents what someone currently has or feels
REF: 23.8.1, 23.8.2, 23.8.3

Estar com covers something on one's person, an illness, clothing, age, and temporary physical sensations.
TL: Você está com a chave? · Ele está com dengue. · Estou com fome.
EN: Have you got the key? · He has dengue. · I am hungry.
[PAUSE:900]
Estar sem negates many possession, clothing, and sensation expressions built with estar com.
TL: Estou sem dinheiro. · Ela está sem casaco. · Hoje estou sem fome.
EN: I have no money on me. · She has no coat on. · I am not hungry today.
[PAUSE:950]
SHOW: have on/with you · illness · sensation -> estar com · corresponding absence -> estar sem
Flip when the sensation begins rather than merely holds.

[SIDE]
TITLE: Ficar com marks entry into a sensation
REF: 23.8.2, 23.9.4

With hunger, thirst, sleepiness, cold, heat, fear, jealousy, or embarrassment, estar com presents the current state and ficar com presents the change into it.
TL: Estou com sono. · Depois do almoço, fico com sono.
EN: I am sleepy. · After lunch, I get sleepy.
[PAUSE:900]
The same contrast applies in a continuous transition.
TL: Estou com frio. · Estou ficando com frio.
EN: I am cold. · I am getting cold.
[PAUSE:950]
SHOW: current sensation -> estar com · onset/change -> ficar com

[CARD]
TITLE: Estar para and estar por point toward an unrealized action
REF: 23.8.4, 23.8.5, 23.8.6

Estar para plus an infinitive means to be due, about, or meaning to do something. Estar por plus an infinitive means that the action is yet to be done.
TL: O bebê está para nascer. · O pior ainda está por vir.
EN: The baby is due to be born. · The worst is yet to come.
[PAUSE:900]
Estar plus a gerund forms the ordinary continuous construction for an action in progress.
TL: Eles estão estudando. · Estou esperando uma resposta.
EN: They are studying. · I am waiting for an answer.
[PAUSE:950]
SHOW: due/about to -> estar para · yet to be done -> estar por · in progress -> estar + gerund
Flip for ficar beyond location and change.

[SIDE]
TITLE: Ficar can mean stay, turn out, keep, continue, or arrange
REF: 23.9.2, 23.9.3, 23.9.4, 23.9.5, 23.9.6

Ficar can mean to stay in a place or to turn out in a particular way. Ficar com can mean to keep something or receive a share or illness.
TL: Ficamos no hotel. · As fotos ficaram ótimas. · Vou ficar com uma cópia.
EN: We stayed at the hotel. · The photos came out well. · I will keep a copy.
[PAUSE:900]
Ficar plus a gerund can mean keep, go on, or remain doing; ficar de plus an infinitive means arrange or say one will do something.
TL: Ele fica me ligando. · Ficamos de sair na sexta.
EN: He keeps calling me. · We arranged to go out on Friday.
[PAUSE:950]
SHOW: stay/turn out · ficar com = keep/get · ficar fazendo = continue · ficar de fazer = arrange

[CARD]
TITLE: Run each sentence through a three-stage diagnostic
REF: 23.2, 23.3, 23.4, 23.6

First ask whether the sentence identifies or classifies, presents a current state or movable location, or describes a fixed location or change.
TL: O Amazonas é um rio. · O barco está no porto. · O porto fica no centro.
EN: The Amazon is a river. · The boat is in the port. · The port is downtown.
[PAUSE:900]
Then choose the time viewpoint: background, bounded occurrence, or recurrence continuing to now.
TL: Ela era alegre. · Ela ficou alegre. · Ela tem estado alegre.
EN: She was cheerful by nature. · She became cheerful. · She has repeatedly been cheerful lately.
[PAUSE:950]
SHOW: relationship -> viewpoint in time -> idiomatic frame
Flip for the final checks on the complement.

[SIDE]
TITLE: Let the subject and complement reveal the intended frame
REF: 23.2, 23.3, 23.4, 23.7, 23.8, 23.9

Identify the grammatical subject, then inspect what follows the verb: noun, adjective, place, participle, com or sem phrase, infinitive, or gerund.
[PAUSE:850]
Finally check the special patterns: event versus participant location, action versus resulting participle, estar com versus ficar com, and the idioms built around ser and ficar.
TL: A ópera é amanhã. · Os músicos estão aqui. · O público ficou animado.
EN: The opera is tomorrow. · The musicians are here. · The audience became excited.
[PAUSE:950]
SHOW: subject -> complement -> time viewpoint -> fixed idiom -> ser/estar/ficar
