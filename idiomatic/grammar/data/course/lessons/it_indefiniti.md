## SCRIPT

[CARD]
TITLE: Italian indefinites encode both meaning and grammatical role
REF: 9.1, 9.2, 9.4, 9.7, 9.9, 9.11, 9.12

First decide whether the unknown is a person, a thing, or a noun being quantified. Qualcuno and nessuno can stand for people; qualcosa and niente stand for things.
TL: Qualcuno ha lasciato una cartella sul tavolo, ma nessuno sa di chi sia.
EN: Somebody left a folder on the table, but nobody knows whose it is.
[PAUSE:900]
Forms such as qualche, qualunque, nessun, and ogni modify a following noun instead. Their number on the page does not always match the quantity in the meaning.
TL: Ogni proposta contiene qualche elemento utile.
EN: Every proposal contains some useful elements.
SHOW: person -> qualcuno/nessuno/chiunque · thing -> qualcosa/niente/qualunque cosa · noun modifier -> qualche/nessun/ogni/qualunque
Flip for the singular forms that can carry plural meaning.

[SIDE]
TITLE: Surface singular does not always mean one
REF: 9.7, 9.11, 9.12

Qualche is invariable and takes a singular noun, even when it means a few. Nessun also takes a singular noun, even where English naturally uses a plural.
TL: Ho ancora qualche dubbio, ma non vedo nessun ostacolo serio.
EN: I still have a few doubts, but I see no serious obstacles.
[PAUSE:900]
Ogni and ciascun likewise select a singular noun. Keep grammatical agreement singular throughout the clause.
TL: Ogni candidato riceverà un codice e ciascun codice resterà personale.
EN: Every candidate will receive a code, and each code will remain personal.
SHOW: qualche + singular noun -> often a few · nessun + singular noun -> no/any · ogni/ciascun + singular noun -> every/each

[CARD]
TITLE: Uno means an indefinite one; quello identifies the one
REF: 9.1

Use uno plus an adjective when you mean a member of a type without identifying a particular member: a blue one, a cheaper one, or a more robust one.
TL: Cerco un taccuino, ma me ne serve uno resistente.
EN: I am looking for a notebook, but I need a robust one.
[PAUSE:900]
Use quello plus an adjective or relative clause when context identifies the referent: the blue one, the one on the left, or the one we discussed.
TL: Ho confrontato i due preventivi e ho scelto quello più trasparente.
EN: I compared the two estimates and chose the more transparent one.
SHOW: uno + adjective -> an indefinite one · quello + adjective/clause -> the identified one
Eleven uno-versus-quello choices follow.

[SIDE]
TITLE: Uno and quello preserve gender; ne carries the missing noun
REF: 9.1

The pronoun agrees with the omitted noun. Una and quella are feminine; plural identified ones are quelli or quelle.
TL: Avevo ordinato due copie, ma me n'è arrivata una sola.
EN: I had ordered two copies, but only one arrived.
[PAUSE:900]
With an indefinite quantity, ne commonly replaces the noun phrase. An identified member instead uses a demonstrative form without ne.
TL: Quante sedie mancano? Ne manca una pieghevole; quella di legno è già qui.
EN: How many chairs are missing? One folding chair is missing; the wooden one is already here.
SHOW: omitted feminine noun -> una/quella · indefinite quantity -> ne + uno/una · identified referent -> quello/quella

[CARD]
TITLE: Qualcuno names a person; qualcosa names a thing
REF: 9.2, 9.4

Use qualcuno for an unspecified person. In this human meaning it is grammatically masculine, even when the person's identity and sex are unknown.
TL: C'è qualcuno disposto a controllare i calcoli?
EN: Is there somebody willing to check the calculations?
[PAUSE:900]
Use qualcosa for an unspecified thing, event, or idea. It is normally grammatically masculine.
TL: È successo qualcosa di insolito durante la prova.
EN: Something unusual happened during the test.
SHOW: unspecified person -> qualcuno · unspecified thing -> qualcosa · both normally trigger masculine agreement
Eleven mixed indefinite completions follow.

[SIDE]
TITLE: Qualcosa takes di before an adjective
REF: 9.2, 9.4, 9.5

Before a following adjective, qualcosa requires di, and the adjective is masculine singular. Qualcuno does not use this construction.
TL: Vorrei proporre qualcosa di più concreto.
EN: I would like to propose something more concrete.
[PAUSE:900]
For else, use qualcos'altro for a thing and qualcun altro for a person. When qualcuno means some of a group of objects, it can vary for gender.
TL: Conosco qualcun altro che potrebbe aiutarci; delle candidate, qualcuna ha già risposto.
EN: I know somebody else who might help us; of the female candidates, some have already replied.
SHOW: something + adjective -> qualcosa di + masculine adjective · something else -> qualcos'altro · somebody else -> qualcun altro

[CARD]
TITLE: Tale and tizio deliberately leave identity vague
REF: 9.3

Un tale presents a person as somebody or other. Tizio is more colloquial and can sound like guy or bloke; its feminine form is tizia.
TL: Mi ha chiamato un tale che sostiene di conoscerti.
EN: Some man who claims to know you called me.
[PAUSE:900]
The same vague style can label details that the speaker cannot or does not wish to specify. Register matters: tizio is conversational, while tale also appears in more neutral placeholders.
TL: All'ingresso c'era un tizio con una cartellina rossa.
EN: There was some guy with a red folder at the entrance.
SHOW: somebody or other -> un tale · colloquial unspecified person -> un tizio/una tizia · named detail withheld -> tale
Flip for two ways to divide an unspecified group.

[SIDE]
TITLE: Chi ... chi and altri partition a group
REF: 9.5, 9.6

Repeated chi can distribute unnamed people across contrasting actions or choices: some do one thing, others another.
TL: Alla riunione chi prendeva appunti, chi controllava i dati.
EN: At the meeting, some people took notes while others checked the data.
[PAUSE:900]
Plural altri means other people or others. In elevated prose, singular altri can mean another person, but ordinary speech usually chooses qualcun altro.
TL: Alcuni volevano rinviare la decisione; altri preferivano votare subito.
EN: Some wanted to postpone the decision; others preferred to vote immediately.
SHOW: some ... others ... -> chi ... chi ... · other people -> altri · another person -> usually qualcun altro

[CARD]
TITLE: Qualche and alcuni both mean some, with different focus
REF: 9.7, 9.8

Qualche is invariant, precedes a singular noun, and usually presents a small indefinite amount without emphasizing its exact membership.
TL: Dobbiamo chiarire ancora qualche dettaglio.
EN: We still need to clarify a few details.
[PAUSE:900]
Plural alcuni and alcune give more shape to a subset: some particular members, often contrasted with others.
TL: Alcuni partecipanti sono rimasti online, mentre altri sono usciti.
EN: Some participants stayed online, while others left.
SHOW: low-focus indefinite amount -> qualche + singular · delimited subset -> alcuni/alcune + plural
Eleven qualche, alcuno, and location completions follow.

[SIDE]
TITLE: Alcuno changes register; indefinite places use da
REF: 9.8, 9.10

Singular alcun or alcuno is uncommon in positive clauses. In formal negative language it can replace nessun, following the same sound-based forms as the indefinite article.
TL: La commissione non ha sollevato alcuna obiezione.
EN: The committee did not raise any objection.
[PAUSE:900]
For somewhere, Italian says da qualche parte. Somewhere else is da un'altra parte or the compact adverb altrove.
TL: Devo aver posato le chiavi da qualche parte; se non sono qui, cercherò altrove.
EN: I must have put the keys somewhere; if they are not here, I will look elsewhere.
SHOW: formal not any -> non ... alcun/alcuno/alcuna · somewhere -> da qualche parte · elsewhere -> altrove/da un'altra parte

[CARD]
TITLE: Chiunque is human; qualunque and qualsiasi attach to things
REF: 9.9

Chiunque stands alone for any person or whoever. It can be the subject or follow a preposition.
TL: Chiunque può consultare il verbale, e puoi chiedere chiarimenti a chiunque.
EN: Anyone can consult the minutes, and you can ask anyone for clarification.
[PAUSE:900]
Qualunque and qualsiasi are invariable adjectives before a noun. Qualunque cosa and qualsiasi cosa mean anything or whatever.
TL: Accetteremo qualsiasi proposta conforme ai requisiti, qualunque cosa accada.
EN: We will accept any proposal that meets the requirements, whatever happens.
SHOW: any person -> chiunque · any + noun -> qualunque/qualsiasi + noun · anything -> qualunque/qualsiasi cosa

[SIDE]
TITLE: Position and negation can turn free choice into just any
REF: 9.9

After an indefinite article and noun, qualsiasi or qualunque can mean any old or just any. Negating that phrase rejects ordinariness and implies something special.
TL: Non è una consulente qualunque: dirige tre laboratori internazionali.
EN: She is not just any consultant: she runs three international laboratories.
[PAUSE:900]
With a human pronoun, the same restriction uses chiunque. A negative or conditional context often means not just anybody rather than nobody.
TL: Non affiderei questi dati a chiunque.
EN: I would not entrust this data to just anybody.
SHOW: un + noun + qualunque/qualsiasi -> any old noun · non ... un noun qualunque -> no ordinary noun · not just anybody -> non ... chiunque

[CARD]
TITLE: Italian negative concord depends on position
REF: 9.11

When nessuno, niente, or nulla stands before the verb, it supplies the clause's negation and non is absent.
TL: Nessuno ha risposto alla convocazione e nulla è cambiato.
EN: Nobody replied to the summons, and nothing changed.
[PAUSE:900]
When the negative word follows the verb, put non before the verb. The two negative markers form one negative meaning; they do not cancel each other.
TL: Non ha risposto nessuno e non abbiamo ricevuto niente.
EN: Nobody replied, and we did not receive anything.
SHOW: negative word + verb -> no non · non + verb + negative word -> required concord · one negative meaning
Eleven nessuno-versus-niente completions follow.

[SIDE]
TITLE: Nessun modifies a noun; niente and nulla stand alone
REF: 9.11

Nessun, nessuno, and nessuna precede a singular noun and vary like the indefinite article. Niente and nulla are pronouns, take masculine agreement, and use di before an adjective.
TL: Nessuna proposta soddisfa i criteri, e non è emerso niente di convincente.
EN: No proposal meets the criteria, and nothing convincing has emerged.
[PAUSE:900]
Altro is the exception: say nient'altro, without di. In informal speech, niente can also precede a noun as an invariable no.
TL: Non aggiungo nient'altro; niente riunione oggi, perché il direttore è assente.
EN: I am adding nothing else; no meeting today, because the director is absent.
SHOW: no + noun -> nessun/nessuno/nessuna · nothing + adjective -> niente/nulla di · nothing else -> nient'altro · colloquial no + noun -> niente + noun

[CARD]
TITLE: Ogni covers every; ciascun singles members out
REF: 9.12

Ogni presents a whole class or a recurring interval. It is invariable and precedes a singular noun.
TL: Ogni candidato riceverà un codice personale.
EN: Every candidate will receive a personal code.
[PAUSE:900]
Ciascun or ciascuno also precedes a singular noun but highlights each member separately. Its form follows the same sound pattern as un and uno.
TL: Ciascun candidato deve firmare il modulo, e ciascuno studente deve mostrare un documento.
EN: Each candidate must sign the form, and each student must show an identity document.
SHOW: whole class or recurrence -> ogni · member-by-member -> ciascun/ciascuno + singular noun
Flip for the pronouns and the one construction restricted to ogni.

[SIDE]
TITLE: Ognuno stands alone; only ogni combines with a numeral
REF: 9.12

Ognuno and ciascuno are pronouns meaning each one or everyone. They can be followed by di plus a defined group.
TL: Ognuno deve verificare i propri dati; ciascuno dei finalisti presenterà due progetti.
EN: Everyone must check their own data; each of the finalists will present two projects.
[PAUSE:900]
For a repeating interval measured by a number, use ogni. Ciascun does not combine with that numeral construction.
TL: Il sensore registra un valore ogni dieci secondi.
EN: The sensor records a value every ten seconds.
SHOW: standalone each/everyone -> ognuno/ciascuno · each of -> ciascuno di · every + numeral + noun -> ogni dieci secondi

[CARD]
TITLE: Distributive expressions put the share after the amount
REF: 9.13

To assign an amount to each member, place ciascuno after the amount or use l'uno, l'una, gli uni, or le une with agreement.
TL: I consulenti hanno ricevuto cinquecento euro ciascuno.
EN: The consultants received five hundred euros each.
[PAUSE:900]
L'uno and l'una agree with the entities being priced or counted, not with the currency.
TL: Le licenze costano trenta euro l'una.
EN: The licences cost thirty euros each.
SHOW: amount + ciascuno -> each · amount + l'uno/l'una -> agree with distributed item
Eight hygiene-gated ogni-and-ciascuno completions follow; three judgment-dependent items are excluded.

[SIDE]
TITLE: A testa is conversational; cadauno belongs mainly to prices
REF: 9.13

A testa is a common way to say per person, especially for food, tickets, and shared costs.
TL: Abbiamo ordinato un caffè a testa e pagato dodici euro a testa.
EN: We ordered one coffee each and paid twelve euros per person.
[PAUSE:900]
Cadauno or cadauna survives chiefly in written prices and quotations, where it agrees with the item.
TL: Queste lampade costano ottanta euro cadauna.
EN: These lamps cost eighty euros each.
SHOW: per person -> a testa · written unit price -> cadauno/cadauna · neutral distribution -> ciascuno or l'uno/l'una
