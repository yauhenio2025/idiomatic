## SCRIPT

[CARD]
TITLE: Building cardinal numbers
SVG: de_zahlen_c01f.svg
REF: 8.1.1

German normally moves from larger blocks to smaller ones. The final two-digit block is the twist: between twenty-one and ninety-nine, the unit comes before the tens and the whole sequence is one word:
TL: Dreiundvierzig Gäste haben zugesagt.
EN: Forty-three guests have accepted.
[PAUSE:1000]
Forty-three is literally three-and-forty. Hundreds and thousands join the same compound without extra spaces:
TL: Die Sanierung kostet sechshundertzweiundachtzigtausend Euro.
EN: The renovation costs six hundred and eighty-two thousand euros.
[PAUSE:1200]
The conjunction inside the two-digit part is obligatory. An extra conjunction after a hundred or thousand survives in a few traditional patterns, but it is optional there and usually absent.
SHOW: 43 = drei + und + vierzig · the compound is one word
Flip the card.

[SIDE]
TITLE: Where punctuation reverses
REF: 8.1.1

Written figures hide a cross-language trap. German groups thousands with a point or a space, and marks decimals with a comma:
TL: An der Erhebung beteiligten sich 12.450 Haushalte.
EN: Twelve thousand four hundred and fifty households took part in the survey.
[PAUSE:1000]
TL: Die Fehlerquote sank auf 1,7 Prozent.
EN: The error rate fell to 1.7 percent.
[PAUSE:1000]
From one million upward, the scale words are nouns. They stand apart from the following compound and take plural endings. Even a decimal greater than one triggers the plural:
TL: Für den Ausbau stehen 2,4 Millionen Euro bereit.
EN: Two point four million euros are available for the expansion.
SHOW: 12.450 = twelve thousand · 1,7 = one point seven · Millionen is a noun
Next, make the number one change shape.

[CARD]
TITLE: One changes its shape
SVG: de_zahlen_c02f.svg
REF: 8.1.2

The number one has three grammatical jobs. In isolation, while counting or naming a route, it uses its standalone form:
TL: Wir nehmen die Linie eins bis zum Rathaus.
EN: We take the number one line to the town hall.
[PAUSE:1000]
Before a noun, it behaves like the indefinite article and carries the required gender and case ending:
TL: Die Entscheidung beruhte auf einem einzigen Gutachten.
EN: The decision was based on a single expert opinion.
[PAUSE:1000]
Used as a pronoun, it takes the endings of that pronoun family:
TL: Von den drei Entwürfen überzeugte mich nur einer.
EN: Of the three designs, only one convinced me.
SHOW: isolation: eins · before a noun: ein- · pronoun: einer / eine / eins
Flip the card.

[SIDE]
TITLE: Higher cardinals hardly decline
REF: 8.1.3

From two upward, cardinal numbers normally ignore gender and case. Formal writing has an optional genitive form for two and three:
TL: Wegen zweier kurzfristiger Absagen begann die Sitzung später.
EN: Because of two last-minute cancellations, the meeting began later.
[PAUSE:1100]
There is also a useful grouping contrast. One form presents a complete group acting together:
TL: Die Prüfer bewerteten den Entwurf zu dritt.
EN: The three examiners assessed the design together.
[PAUSE:1000]
The declined isolated form can instead distribute people into groups:
TL: Die Besucher wurden jeweils zu dreien in den Saal gelassen.
EN: The visitors were admitted to the hall three at a time.
SHOW: ordinary cardinal: invariant · formal genitive: zweier / dreier · zu dritt = the three together
Large scales and approximate numbers come next.

[CARD]
TITLE: Three scales, three behaviors
REF: 8.1.1, 8.1.5

Hundred and thousand stay inside the numeral and do not decline:
TL: Rund achthundert Fachleute nahmen an der Konferenz teil.
EN: Around eight hundred specialists took part in the conference.
[PAUSE:1000]
Million and the larger scale words are ordinary nouns, so they stand apart and agree in number:
TL: Die Stadt stellte eins Komma drei Millionen Euro bereit.
EN: The city provided 1.3 million euros.
[PAUSE:1000]
Hundred and thousand can themselves become quantity nouns when the number is deliberately vague:
TL: Mehrere Tausend Menschen verfolgten die Debatte.
EN: Several thousand people followed the debate.
SHOW: hundert / tausend stay inside · Million(en) stands outside · mehrere Tausend is a quantity
Flip the card.

[SIDE]
TITLE: Does the modifier govern the case?
SVG: de_zahlen_c03b.svg
REF: 8.1.6

Words meaning about, over, under or up to can merely modify a number. If that modifier can disappear without breaking the sentence, it does not govern the noun phrase. The real case source remains in charge:
TL: Mit über dreihundert freiwilligen Helfern gelang der Umzug.
EN: With more than three hundred volunteer helpers, the move succeeded.
[PAUSE:1200]
Here the outer preposition supplies the dative; the word before the number changes nothing. Some approximate expressions even leave a following adjective with a strong ending:
TL: An die vierzig internationale Fachleute nahmen an der Anhörung teil.
EN: About forty international experts took part in the hearing.
[PAUSE:1200]
But when the same-looking word cannot be removed, it is a real preposition and governs its own case:
TL: Kinder unter sieben Jahren dürfen das Labor nicht betreten.
EN: Children under seven years of age may not enter the laboratory.
SHOW: removable number modifier: no case government · required preposition: governs case
Now turn numerals into adjectives and nouns.

[CARD]
TITLE: The invariant number adjective
REF: 8.1.4

German derives adjectives in a fixed ending from cardinal numbers. They express value, size, age or vintage, and they do not decline like ordinary adjectives:
TL: Sie kaufte zwei achtziger Briefmarken.
EN: She bought two eighty-cent stamps.
[PAUSE:1000]
The same fixed shape survives where an ordinary adjective would show a different ending:
TL: Zum Jubiläum öffneten wir einen zweiundzwanziger Riesling.
EN: For the anniversary, we opened a 2022 Riesling.
[PAUSE:1100]
Used as nouns, these forms take ordinary noun inflection:
TL: Für den Automaten brauchen wir drei Zehner.
EN: We need three ten-euro notes for the machine.
SHOW: numeral + -er = invariant adjective · as a noun: ordinary noun endings
Flip the card.

[SIDE]
TITLE: A number name is feminine
REF: 8.1.1, 8.1.5

When a cardinal refers to the numeral itself, German treats it as a feminine noun. Its plural ends in the usual way:
TL: Die Null spielt in dieser Berechnung keine Rolle.
EN: Zero plays no role in this calculation.
[PAUSE:1000]
TL: Im Test bekam sie zwei Einsen und eine Zwei.
EN: In the test she got two top grades and one second-best grade.
[PAUSE:1100]
Do not confuse a named numeral with a neuter quantity noun. The gender marks the difference between the number as a symbol and a batch of that size:
TL: Das zweite Hundert wurde deutlich schneller geprüft als das erste.
EN: The second batch of one hundred was reviewed much faster than the first.
SHOW: numeral itself: die Eins, die Hundert · quantity: das Hundert
Ordinals use a different construction engine.

[CARD]
TITLE: Build the ordinal stem
SVG: de_zahlen_c05f.svg
REF: 8.2

Ordinals from two through nineteen add one stem suffix. From twenty upward, they add the superlative-looking suffix instead. Then the whole ordinal takes an ordinary adjective ending:
TL: Der sechste Antrag war vollständig.
EN: The sixth application was complete.
[PAUSE:1000]
TL: Beim einundzwanzigsten Versuch funktionierte der Sensor.
EN: On the twenty-first attempt, the sensor worked.
[PAUSE:1100]
First, third and seventh have irregular stems, but their adjective endings are perfectly regular:
TL: Der dritte Entwurf überzeugte die Jury.
EN: The third design convinced the jury.
SHOW: 2–19: stem + -t- · 20 upward: stem + -st- · then add the adjective ending
Flip the card.

[SIDE]
TITLE: Ordinals become people and discourse markers
REF: 8.2

An ordinal used as a noun is capitalized and declines like a nominalized adjective:
TL: Leila war die Erste, die den Rechenfehler bemerkte.
EN: Leila was the first person to notice the calculation error.
[PAUSE:1000]
TL: Als Zweiter stellte der Gutachter Rückfragen.
EN: The expert was the second person to ask follow-up questions.
[PAUSE:1000]
The interrogative form asks for a position in a sequence, including repetitions:
TL: Zum wievielten Mal wurde die Abstimmung vertagt?
EN: How many times was the vote postponed?
[PAUSE:1000]
And the adverbial series organizes an argument:
TL: Erstens fehlt ein Beleg; zweitens widersprechen sich die Zahlen.
EN: First, one piece of evidence is missing; second, the figures contradict one another.
SHOW: die Erste = noun · der dritte Versuch = adjective · erstens = discourse adverb
Fractions preserve the same ordinal stem.

[CARD]
TITLE: Fractions are neuter nouns
REF: 8.3.1

A fraction takes the ordinal stem and adds a noun-forming ending. The result is neuter, and its plural has no extra ending:
TL: Ein Viertel der Fläche bleibt unbebaut.
EN: One quarter of the area remains undeveloped.
[PAUSE:1000]
TL: Drei Viertel der Wohnungen sind bereits vermietet.
EN: Three quarters of the apartments have already been rented.
[PAUSE:1200]
Careful with agreement: formal written German follows a plural fraction with a plural verb. The dative plural can add an ending of its own:
TL: Die Reform wurde von zwei Dritteln der Mitglieder unterstützt.
EN: The reform was supported by two thirds of the members.
SHOW: ordinal stem + -el = neuter fraction · plural fraction → plural verb
Flip the card.

[SIDE]
TITLE: The decimal comma speaks digit by digit
SVG: de_zahlen_c06b.svg
REF: 8.3.1

The written decimal comma is spoken explicitly. After it, the safest system is to read every digit separately:
TL: Der Messwert liegt bei null Komma null sieben Milligramm.
EN: The reading is 0.07 milligrams.
[PAUSE:1100]
TL: Die Quote stieg von vier Komma zwei auf vier Komma sieben fünf Prozent.
EN: The rate rose from 4.2 to 4.75 percent.
[PAUSE:1200]
With two decimal places, everyday speech also often groups those last two digits as a two-digit number:
TL: Umgangssprachlich hört man auch vier Komma fünfundsiebzig.
EN: In everyday speech, you also hear four point seventy-five.
SHOW: written comma · spoken Komma · safest reading: each following digit separately
Now choose among the three meanings of half.

[CARD]
TITLE: Route half by grammatical job
SVG: de_zahlen_c07f.svg
REF: 8.3.2

English half hides three German constructions. A free-standing half, or a partitive half of something, is normally a feminine noun:
TL: Nur die Hälfte der Bewerbungen war vollständig.
EN: Only half of the applications were complete.
[PAUSE:1100]
Before a noun, use the adjective and give it the ending demanded by the phrase:
TL: Wir bestellten einen halben Laib Sauerteigbrot.
EN: We ordered half a loaf of sourdough bread.
[PAUSE:1100]
With an adjective or participle, half is an uninflected adverb:
TL: Die Tür stand nur halb offen.
EN: The door was only half open.
SHOW: partitive noun: die Hälfte · before noun: halb + ending · adverb: halb
Flip the card.

[SIDE]
TITLE: One and a half never declines
REF: 8.3.2

The two forms for one and a half are invariant, and every larger number-plus-half form is invariant too:
TL: Die Reparatur dauerte anderthalb Stunden.
EN: The repair took one and a half hours.
[PAUSE:1000]
TL: Nach zweieinhalb Wochen lag das Gutachten vor.
EN: After two and a half weeks, the expert report was ready.
[PAUSE:1100]
In fixed phrases, the adjective still takes the case ending supplied by its surroundings:
TL: Schließlich kam uns die Gegenseite auf halbem Weg entgegen.
EN: In the end, the other side met us halfway.
SHOW: eineinhalb / anderthalb / zweieinhalb: invariant · halbem Weg: adjective ending
Six clean book exercises now make you choose the right half construction.

[CARD]
TITLE: Distribute, multiply, classify
REF: 8.4.1, 8.4.2

For equal distribution, a short invariant word goes directly before the allocated amount:
TL: Die vier Teams erhielten je zwei zusätzliche Plätze.
EN: The four teams each received two additional places.
[PAUSE:1000]
The productive suffix for a multiple can describe several distinct functions or instances:
TL: Die Regelung verfolgt einen zweifachen Zweck.
EN: The regulation serves a twofold purpose.
[PAUSE:1000]
The ordinary adjective for double often means two of the same thing:
TL: Das Protokoll liegt in doppelter Ausfertigung vor.
EN: The minutes are available in duplicate.
[PAUSE:1000]
Another invariant formation classifies kinds rather than quantities:
TL: Der Ausschuss prüfte dreierlei Einwände.
EN: The committee examined three kinds of objection.
SHOW: je = equal shares · -fach = multiple · doppelt = two alike · -erlei = kinds
Flip the card.

[SIDE]
TITLE: Arithmetic is spoken syntax
REF: 8.4.4

Mathematical notation becomes a small clause. Addition allows either the everyday conjunction or the international loanword; multiplication uses the same little word that will matter on the next card:
TL: Sieben mal acht ist sechsundfünfzig.
EN: Seven times eight is fifty-six.
[PAUSE:1000]
Division uses a participle-plus-preposition expression, and equality can be stated explicitly:
TL: Neun geteilt durch drei ist gleich drei.
EN: Nine divided by three equals three.
[PAUSE:1000]
Powers use an adverb before the exponent:
TL: Fünf hoch zwei ist fünfundzwanzig.
EN: Five squared is twenty-five.
SHOW: × = mal · ÷ = geteilt durch · exponent = hoch · = ist / gleich
Next, separate frequency from the noun meaning an occasion.

[CARD]
TITLE: Three routes through mal
SVG: de_zahlen_c09f.svg
REF: 8.4.3

A cardinal plus the frequency suffix forms one lower-case adverb:
TL: Ich habe den Entwurf dreimal überarbeitet.
EN: I revised the draft three times.
[PAUSE:1000]
When you mean a countable occasion, use the separate neuter noun with a determiner or adjective:
TL: Beim dritten Mal stimmte der Ausschuss zu.
EN: On the third occasion, the committee agreed.
[PAUSE:1100]
A related adverbial suffix attaches to a small lexical set and often highlights first occurrence or repetition:
TL: Erstmals wurden auch externe Sachverständige befragt.
EN: For the first time, external experts were also consulted.
SHOW: dreimal = frequency adverb · das dritte Mal = occasion noun · erstmals = adverb
Flip the card.

[SIDE]
TITLE: The noun carries noun grammar
REF: 8.4.3

Because the occasion word is a noun, its determiner and adjective are separate and inflected:
TL: Das war das einzige Mal, dass die Anlage ausfiel.
EN: That was the only time the system failed.
[PAUSE:1100]
Its plural is visible when several occasions are counted as entities:
TL: Beide Male erhielten wir dieselbe Auskunft.
EN: Both times we received the same information.
[PAUSE:1000]
A lexicalized adverb can still fuse into one lower-case word:
TL: Darüber sprechen wir ein andermal.
EN: We will talk about that another time.
SHOW: das Mal · beide Male · ein andermal · spelling follows grammatical status
Nine book exercises now test the boundary between suffix and noun.

[CARD]
TITLE: Informal time aims at the next hour
SVG: de_zahlen_c10f.svg
REF: 8.5.1

Everyday German normally uses the twelve-hour clock. Past and before work as expected, but the half-hour points forward to the upcoming full hour:
TL: Wir treffen uns um Viertel nach sieben.
EN: We are meeting at quarter past seven.
[PAUSE:900]
TL: Der Vortrag beginnt um halb acht.
EN: The lecture begins at half past seven.
[PAUSE:1100]
TL: Der letzte Bus fährt um zehn vor zwölf.
EN: The last bus leaves at ten to twelve.
[PAUSE:1000]
Regional systems in the South and East also count quarter-hours toward the upcoming hour:
TL: In Sachsen hört man für „Viertel nach sieben“ oft „viertel acht“.
EN: In Saxony, you often hear quarter eight for quarter past seven.
SHOW: halb acht = 7.30 · the named hour is the destination
Flip the card.

[SIDE]
TITLE: Official time stays absolute
REF: 8.5.1

Timetables, broadcasts, opening hours and meetings favor the twenty-four-hour clock. The hour comes first, then the clock noun, then the minutes; there is no a.m. or p.m.:
TL: Der Zug fährt um neunzehn Uhr siebenunddreißig ab.
EN: The train departs at 19:37.
[PAUSE:1000]
At a full official hour, the clock noun stays audible:
TL: Die Sitzung beginnt um vierzehn Uhr.
EN: The meeting begins at 14:00.
[PAUSE:1000]
Midnight can begin the new day at zero:
TL: Der Bereitschaftsdienst endet um null Uhr dreißig.
EN: The on-call service ends at 00:30.
SHOW: official time = hour + Uhr + minutes · no a.m. / p.m.
Dates close the system.

[CARD]
TITLE: A date is a declined ordinal
REF: 8.5.3

The day of the month is an ordinal, so its ending reveals the case. With the copular verb, the date is nominative:
TL: Heute ist der neunte August.
EN: Today is the ninth of August.
[PAUSE:900]
With the possession verb used for dates, it is accusative:
TL: Wir haben heute den neunten August.
EN: Today is the ninth of August.
[PAUSE:900]
After the contracted time preposition, it is dative:
TL: Die Ausstellung eröffnet am neunten August zweitausendsiebenundzwanzig.
EN: The exhibition opens on 9 August 2027.
[PAUSE:1100]
In figures, German writes day before month and marks the ordinal day with a point.
SHOW: der neunte · den neunten · am neunten · written order: day.month.year
Flip the card.

[SIDE]
TITLE: Day plus date — then the address
REF: 8.5.3, 8.6

After a weekday with the contracted preposition, print often appends the date in the same dative case:
TL: Die Tagung beginnt am Dienstag, dem zwölften Oktober.
EN: The conference begins on Tuesday, the twelfth of October.
[PAUSE:1100]
A weekday without that preposition can instead be followed by an accusative date:
TL: Wir reisen Montag, den elften Oktober, wieder ab.
EN: We leave again on Monday, the eleventh of October.
[PAUSE:1100]
In a domestic postal address, the postcode precedes the town. The street number follows the street name:
TL: Bitte senden Sie die Unterlagen an Frau Lena Vogt, Lindenweg 8, 04109 Leipzig.
EN: Please send the documents to Ms Lena Vogt, 8 Lindenweg, 04109 Leipzig.
SHOW: weekday + date: case can mirror the frame · address: street number · postcode before town
