## SCRIPT

[CARD]
TITLE: Treat word formation as a map, not a machine
REF: 20.1, 20.2

Italian builds words by combining bases, changing a word's grammatical role, and adding prefixes or suffixes. These patterns help you recognize families, but they do not license every imaginable form.
TL: Abbiamo riaperto il fascicolo dopo la verifica.
EN: We reopened the file after the review.
[PAUSE:800]
TL: Il documento contiene un'informazione incompleta.
EN: The document contains incomplete information.
[PAUSE:900]
In riaprire the prefix has a transparent meaning. In many other formations, convention decides both the form and its specialized meaning.
SHOW: identify the base -> identify the added material -> verify the attested meaning
Flip to separate productive clues from lexical facts.

[SIDE]
TITLE: A recognizable family does not guarantee predictability
REF: 20.1, 20.2

A derived word may be easy to segment and still impossible to interpret literally. Compounds and suffixes often develop meanings that must be learned with the whole word.
TL: Il telecomando è sul tavolino accanto al televisore.
EN: The remote control is on the small table beside the television.
[PAUSE:800]
TL: L'orologiaio ha riparato il vecchio orologio.
EN: The watchmaker repaired the old watch.
[PAUSE:900]
Use morphology to organize vocabulary: telecomando belongs with tele-, and orologiaio with orologio. Use a dictionary or known usage to confirm meaning, spelling, and register.
SHOW: pattern = memory aid · attested word = authority

[CARD]
TITLE: Read compounds as wholes
REF: 20.1

Italian compounds can join nouns, adjectives, verbs, or other elements and are commonly written as one word. Their internal pieces suggest a history, not necessarily a complete definition.
TL: La cassaforte custodisce i documenti originali.
EN: The safe holds the original documents.
[PAUSE:800]
TL: Il coprifuoco entrerà in vigore a mezzanotte.
EN: The curfew will take effect at midnight.
[PAUSE:900]
Cassa plus forte remains fairly transparent, while coprifuoco has a conventional meaning that cannot be recovered by translating its pieces one by one.
SHOW: segment the compound -> learn its established meaning -> keep its established spelling

[SIDE]
TITLE: Conversion changes function without changing form
REF: 20.1

Conversion lets an existing form do a new grammatical job. A masculine adjective can become a noun with an article, and an infinitive can name an activity or process.
TL: Dobbiamo distinguere il vero dal falso.
EN: We must distinguish truth from falsehood.
[PAUSE:800]
TL: Il camminare quotidiano gli fa bene.
EN: Daily walking is good for him.
[PAUSE:900]
The article is the visible signal: il vero and il falso refer to abstract categories, while il camminare presents an action as a noun-like topic.
SHOW: article + adjective -> abstract or class · article + infinitive -> activity

[CARD]
TITLE: Prefixes modify a complete base
REF: 20.2

Many prefixes contribute a reusable direction. Ri- often signals repetition or return, pre- anticipation, ex- former status, and anti- opposition. Stra- and super- can intensify.
TL: Dovremo riscrivere il paragrafo e preannunciare la modifica.
EN: We will have to rewrite the paragraph and announce the change in advance.
[PAUSE:800]
TL: L'ex-direttore ha criticato la proposta antidemocratica.
EN: The former director criticized the anti-democratic proposal.
[PAUSE:900]
Do not assume that every base accepts every prefix or that the meaning is always compositional. Learn the derived form with its real context.
SHOW: ri- -> again/back · pre- -> beforehand · ex- -> former · anti- -> against

[SIDE]
TITLE: Negative prefixes are selected word by word
REF: 20.2

Italian has several negative or privative prefixes, including s-, dis-, in- or im-, a-, and de-. Their broad tendencies help recognition, but no simple rule predicts the right prefix for every base.
TL: La risposta è scorretta e il modulo è incompleto.
EN: The answer is incorrect and the form is incomplete.
[PAUSE:800]
TL: Il caffè è decaffeinato, ma non è insapore.
EN: The coffee is decaffeinated, but it is not tasteless.
[PAUSE:900]
Memorize scorretto, incompleto, decaffeinato, and insapore as whole forms. A plausible invented alternative may still be nonstandard or mean something else.
SHOW: recognize a negative family -> retrieve the attested member

[CARD]
TITLE: Attach suffixes to the appropriate stem
REF: 20.2

Suffixation usually removes a final unstressed vowel before attaching the new ending. The result may name a person, a place, a collection, or a property.
TL: Il fioraio lavora vicino all'orologeria.
EN: The florist works near the watch shop.
[PAUSE:800]
TL: Dietro la casa si estende un piccolo oliveto.
EN: A small olive grove extends behind the house.
[PAUSE:900]
Fiore supplies fior- in fioraio; orologio supplies the family orologiaio and orologeria; olivo gives oliveto. The stem change is regular enough to notice, but suffix choice remains lexical.
SHOW: base -> stem -> attested suffix -> new word

[SIDE]
TITLE: One function can have many suffixes
REF: 20.2

Agent nouns show why suffixes must be learned in families. Italian uses -aio, -ista, -ino, -iere, -ante, and -tore among other endings, without one universal choice.
TL: Il postino ha parlato con il giardiniere e l'insegnante.
EN: The postman spoke with the gardener and the teacher.
[PAUSE:800]
TL: Un lavoratore ha consegnato il pacco al gommista.
EN: A worker delivered the parcel to the tyre dealer.
[PAUSE:900]
The shared human-role meaning does not make postino, giardiniere, insegnante, lavoratore, and gommista interchangeable patterns.
SHOW: semantic role does not predict one ending

[CARD]
TITLE: Derive adjectives by recognizing productive families
REF: 20.2

The suffixes -abile and -ibile often form possibility adjectives from transitive verbs. Other common adjective families include -ale, -ano, -ese, -oso, and -uto.
TL: Il testo è leggibile, ma la conclusione non è dimostrabile.
EN: The text is legible, but the conclusion cannot be demonstrated.
[PAUSE:800]
TL: L'orgoglioso autore milanese ha accettato la critica.
EN: The proud Milanese author accepted the criticism.
[PAUSE:900]
The broad relationship may be transparent, yet the exact stem and suffix require an attested model: leggere gives leggibile, while dimostrare gives dimostrabile.
SHOW: verb -> -abile/-ibile adjective · noun/place -> conventional adjective family

[SIDE]
TITLE: Derive nouns through several competing patterns
REF: 20.2

Nouns can emerge from adjectives or verbs with endings such as -ità, -ismo, -mento, -zione, -io, or -ume. Some administrative nouns use the bare verbal stem plus -o or -a.
TL: La nazionalità è indicata nel modulo di registrazione.
EN: Nationality is stated on the registration form.
[PAUSE:800]
TL: Il decollo è stato rinviato e l'amministrazione ha emesso un avviso.
EN: Takeoff was postponed, and the administration issued a notice.
[PAUSE:900]
Relate each noun to a known base, but learn its gender and specialized meaning with it. A suffix may carry several meanings, and a base may produce several distinct nouns.
SHOW: identify the base · name the word class · learn form + gender + meaning

[CARD]
TITLE: The ending -ata can package a bounded event
REF: 20.3

A feminine past-participle form may become a noun for a quick, bounded, or completed action. The same -ata shape can also attach to nouns and name a blow, a quantity, a session, or another conventional event.
TL: Ho dato un'occhiata alla relazione prima della riunione.
EN: I took a quick look at the report before the meeting.
[PAUSE:800]
TL: Dopo pranzo si è concesso una dormita.
EN: After lunch he had a nap.
[PAUSE:900]
The construction often appears with dare or fare, but meanings remain lexical: learn occhiata and dormita as nouns, not as freely generated paraphrases.
SHOW: verbal action -> bounded event noun · conventional meaning still matters

[SIDE]
TITLE: Zero suffixation can hide derivation
REF: 20.2

Some nouns are built from a verb stem with only a noun ending, so there is no conspicuous derivational suffix. This pattern is frequent in institutional and administrative vocabulary.
TL: Chiederemo una proroga prima della scadenza.
EN: We will request an extension before the deadline.
[PAUSE:800]
TL: Il sistema conferma l'inoltro della domanda.
EN: The system confirms the forwarding of the application.
[PAUSE:900]
Connect proroga with prorogare and inoltro with inoltrare, while remembering that gender and meaning cannot be calculated from the verb alone.
SHOW: verb stem + noun ending -> conventional event/result noun

[CARD]
TITLE: Evaluatives preserve the word class
REF: 20.4

Evaluative suffixes usually keep the base word's grammatical class while adding size, affection, disapproval, attenuation, or a conversational attitude. Productive families include -one, -ino, -etto, -uccio, and -accio.
TL: Abbiamo preso un tavolino vicino alla finestra.
EN: We took a small table near the window.
[PAUSE:800]
TL: Ci ha servito un vinaccio imbevibile.
EN: He served us an awful, undrinkable wine.
[PAUSE:900]
Tavolino presents the referent as small or familiar; vinaccio adds a negative evaluation. Context decides how literal the size meaning is.
SHOW: base class stays stable · suffix adds evaluation and stance

[SIDE]
TITLE: Size labels are only a starting point
REF: 20.4

-One often suggests enlargement or playful excess. -Ino and -uccio can suggest smallness or affection, -etto often sounds less affectionate, and -accio is strongly pejorative.
TL: Mi ha fatto uno dei suoi soliti discorsoni.
EN: He gave me one of his usual interminable speeches.
[PAUSE:800]
TL: Il bambino stringeva un orsacchiotto consumato.
EN: The child was clutching a worn teddy bear.
[PAUSE:900]
Discorsone may evaluate a speech as excessive without measuring it. Orsacchiotto is a conventional lexical item. Learn the speaker's attitude as part of the suffix's contribution.
SHOW: literal dimension + affection/disapproval + discourse attitude

[CARD]
TITLE: Expect stem adjustments before evaluative suffixes
REF: 20.4

Evaluative formation may alter the base. Words ending in -one often insert -c- before -ino, and several frequent nouns use special stems.
TL: Hanno noleggiato un camioncino per il trasloco.
EN: They rented a small van for the move.
[PAUSE:800]
TL: Il bambino raccolse un sassolino e attraversò il ponticello.
EN: The child picked up a little stone and crossed the little bridge.
[PAUSE:900]
Compare camion with camioncino, sasso with sassolino, and ponte with ponticello. These adjustments belong to established word families, not to a single automatic replacement rule.
SHOW: inspect the base ending -> retrieve the family stem -> attach the suffix

[SIDE]
TITLE: Existing words can block the expected form
REF: 20.4

A formally possible diminutive may be avoided when it would collide with an existing word. Lexicalized forms can also lose the transparent meaning suggested by their suffix.
TL: Facciamo un giretto prima di cena.
EN: Let us take a little walk before dinner.
[PAUSE:800]
TL: Il cavalletto è accanto alla tela.
EN: The easel is beside the canvas.
[PAUSE:900]
Giretto avoids girino, which means tadpole. Cavalletto normally means easel rather than small horse. When a familiar derived-looking form has its own dictionary meaning, that lexical meaning wins.
SHOW: check for collision -> check for lexicalized meaning -> choose the attested form

[CARD]
TITLE: Evaluatives also signal register and relationship
REF: 20.4

Transparent evaluatives are especially at home in informal speech. They can make a request gentler, sound playful with a child, understate value, or express irony and irritation.
TL: Avrei una domandina da farti.
EN: I have a little question to ask you.
[PAUSE:800]
TL: Hai scelto proprio un bel posticino.
EN: You really chose a nice little spot.
[PAUSE:900]
Neither noun must be objectively small. The suffix frames the interaction: domandina can soften an approach, while posticino can sound warm or playful.
SHOW: referential meaning + speaker attitude + social setting

[SIDE]
TITLE: Attenuatives shade qualities rather than measure them
REF: 20.4

Suffixes such as -astro and -iccio can mean roughly -ish, often with an unfavorable nuance. With nouns, -astro commonly suggests an inferior or incompetent example of the category.
TL: Il cielo era grigiastro e il muro bianchiccio.
EN: The sky was greyish and the wall whitish.
[PAUSE:800]
TL: Quel poetastro pubblicava versi mediocri.
EN: That third-rate poet published mediocre verse.
[PAUSE:900]
The exact shade is not predictable from the suffix alone. Learn the conventional form, then use context to judge whether the effect is neutral attenuation or criticism.
SHOW: adjective + attenuative -> approximate quality · noun + -astro -> disparaging category

[CARD]
TITLE: Build and interpret with a four-part check
REF: 20.1, 20.2, 20.3, 20.4

First identify the base and its word class. Second locate any prefix, suffix, or conversion. Third ask what meaning the pattern usually contributes. Fourth verify whether the resulting word is established and appropriate here.
TL: La rilettura ha rivelato un errore nell'introduzione.
EN: Rereading revealed an error in the introduction.
[PAUSE:800]
TL: Il nuovo regolamento è difficilmente applicabile.
EN: The new regulation is difficult to apply.
[PAUSE:900]
Rilettura belongs to a recognizable family around leggere, and applicabile to one around applicare. The family helps analysis, but use depends on the attested form and context.
SHOW: base -> process -> contribution -> attested use

[SIDE]
TITLE: Let morphology organize vocabulary safely
REF: 20.1, 20.2, 20.4

When reading, morphology can reveal a likely base and a broad semantic direction. When speaking or writing, the standard is stricter: retrieve a form you have encountered and confirm uncertain formations.
TL: Conosco il verbo, ma controllerò il sostantivo nel dizionario.
EN: I know the verb, but I will check the noun in the dictionary.
[PAUSE:800]
TL: Il suffisso suggerisce un tono ironico, non soltanto una dimensione.
EN: The suffix suggests an ironic tone, not merely a size.
[PAUSE:900]
This asymmetry is useful. Be adventurous in analysis and conservative in production: use structure to understand, then rely on evidence to choose the word.
SHOW: recognize broadly · produce from evidence · learn the whole lexical family
