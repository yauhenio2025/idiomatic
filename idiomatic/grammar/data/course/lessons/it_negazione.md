## SCRIPT

[CARD]
TITLE: Negation begins with a scope decision
REF: 18.1, 18.5

Italian negation is not just the insertion of non. First decide whether you are rejecting the whole proposition, one embedded action, or a single constituent. Then place the negative expression where that scope is visible.
TL: Non abbiamo ancora approvato il bilancio.
EN: We have not yet approved the budget.
[PAUSE:800]
TL: Abbiamo deciso di non rinviare la votazione.
EN: We have decided not to postpone the vote.
[PAUSE:900]
The first sentence denies a completed event. The second affirms the decision but negates the postponement. The position of non tells the listener which claim survives.
SHOW: choose the scope -> choose the negative expression -> place it
Flip to anchor ordinary non in the verbal group.

[SIDE]
TITLE: Non stands before what it directly negates
REF: 18.1

For ordinary sentence negation, non precedes the finite verb. With an auxiliary or a progressive construction, it precedes the auxiliary. An unstressed object-pronoun cluster stands between non and the verb.
TL: Non le abbiamo ancora risposto.
EN: We have not answered her yet.
[PAUSE:800]
TL: Non gliene sto parlando adesso.
EN: I am not talking to him about it now.
[PAUSE:900]
Before an infinitive, place non with that infinitive when the embedded action is the target. Do not automatically carry English auxiliary patterns into Italian.
TL: Preferisco non discuterne per telefono.
EN: I prefer not to discuss it by phone.
SHOW: non + clitics + finite verb · non + auxiliary · non + infinitive

[CARD]
TITLE: Keep non outside the clitic-verb unit
REF: 18.1, 18.4

Non comes before every unstressed pronoun attached to a finite verb. In a negative singular informal imperative, non is followed by the infinitive; its clitics may attach to that infinitive. Plural and formal commands use their ordinary imperative forms after non.
TL: Non te lo consegnerò senza una ricevuta.
EN: I will not hand it to you without a receipt.
[PAUSE:800]
TL: Non raccontarglielo prima della riunione.
EN: Do not tell it to him before the meeting.
[PAUSE:800]
TL: Non aprite quella cartella.
EN: Do not open that folder.
[PAUSE:900]
Treat the clitics and verb as one unit. The negator does not split them.
SHOW: non + clitic cluster + verb · tu command -> non + infinitive

[SIDE]
TITLE: Non can negate a constituent instead of the clause
REF: 18.1

Put non immediately before a noun, adjective, participle, quantifier, or phrase when that constituent alone is being rejected or contrasted. Non before a quantifier differs from negating the verb after it.
TL: Non molti revisori hanno approvato la proposta.
EN: Not many reviewers approved the proposal.
[PAUSE:800]
TL: Molti revisori non hanno approvato la proposta.
EN: Many reviewers did not approve the proposal.
[PAUSE:900]
A non ma contrast makes the intended replacement explicit. Non before an adjective or noun can also create the meaning non-, un-, or someone or something that is not of that class.
TL: Cerchiamo una soluzione non provvisoria, ma duratura.
EN: We are looking for a solution that is not temporary but lasting.
SHOW: non + constituent -> local contrast · non + verb -> clause negation

[CARD]
TITLE: Use no when an entire alternative is understood
REF: 18.2

After o, Italian uses no, not non, when the missing alternative is a negated verb phrase or whole clause. Meno is a more formal alternative in indirect questions and nominal assessments.
TL: Parteciperai alla conferenza o no?
EN: Will you attend the conference or not?
[PAUSE:800]
TL: Dobbiamo stabilire se il dato sia rilevante o meno.
EN: We must establish whether the datum is relevant or not.
[PAUSE:900]
With an understood noun or adjective, e non and sometimes e no can mean and those that are not. Keep non when it directly forms a non-class rather than replacing a whole proposition.
TL: Il seminario è aperto a specialisti e non specialisti.
EN: The seminar is open to specialists and non-specialists.
SHOW: clause alternative -> o no · formal polarity -> o meno · non-class -> non + noun
Eleven placement choices follow.

[SIDE]
TITLE: Mica rejects an expectation
REF: 18.3

Colloquial mica does more than intensify a negative. It signals that the proposition conflicts with an expectation attributed to the speaker, the listener, or the situation.
TL: Non è mica una procedura automatica.
EN: It is not an automatic procedure, contrary to what you might think.
[PAUSE:800]
TL: Sai mica chi ha modificato il documento?
EN: You would not happen to know who changed the document, would you?
[PAUSE:900]
Mica usually follows the finite verb or auxiliary, with non before the verb. In questions non can be absent, and fronted mica strongly presents the expected answer as negative. It is normally excluded from subordinate clauses that merely restrict or background information.
TL: Mica avrai cancellato anche la copia di sicurezza?
EN: Surely you did not delete the backup as well?
SHOW: expectation contradicted -> (non) + verb + mica · expected no -> mica + verb

[CARD]
TITLE: A postverbal negative word requires non
REF: 18.5

When nessuno, niente, nulla, mai, neanche, nemmeno, neppure, or a né phrase follows the verb, non must precede the verb. This is negative concord, not logical cancellation.
TL: Non è intervenuto nessuno durante il dibattito.
EN: Nobody spoke during the debate.
[PAUSE:800]
TL: Non avevo mai esaminato un caso simile.
EN: I had never examined a similar case.
[PAUSE:800]
TL: Non hanno consultato né l'archivio né il registro.
EN: They consulted neither the archive nor the register.
[PAUSE:900]
English may use one visibly negative word, but Italian builds a licensed negative frame around the verb.
SHOW: non + verb ... negative word · non + verb ... né X né Y
Twelve word-order transformations follow.

[SIDE]
TITLE: A preverbal negative word normally excludes non
REF: 18.5

When the negative subject, object, adverb, or conjunction moves before the verb, ordinary standard Italian omits non. Initial placement also gives that expression greater emphasis.
TL: Nessun candidato ha contestato il risultato.
EN: No candidate challenged the result.
[PAUSE:800]
TL: Mai avrei previsto una reazione simile.
EN: Never would I have predicted such a reaction.
[PAUSE:800]
TL: Neppure il direttore conosceva la password.
EN: Not even the director knew the password.
[PAUSE:900]
Preverbal negative objects can sound literary or strongly marked. Prefer the postverbal pattern with non unless the discourse genuinely calls for fronting.
SHOW: negative word + verb -> no non · fronting -> emphasis or literary register

[CARD]
TITLE: Non più closes a former state
REF: 18.6

Non più says that an earlier state, habit, quantity, or availability has ended. With a simple tense, più usually follows the verb; with a compound tense, it normally follows the auxiliary.
TL: Marta non abita più in centro.
EN: Marta no longer lives in the city centre.
[PAUSE:800]
TL: Dopo il trasferimento non l'ho più incontrata.
EN: After the move, I did not meet her again.
[PAUSE:900]
With clitics, keep non before the clitic cluster and place più after the finite verb or auxiliary. The exact English equivalent may be no longer, not any more, or not again.
TL: Questi dati non ci servono più.
EN: We no longer need these data.
SHOW: former state ended -> non + verb/auxiliary + più
Ten source polarity answers follow.

[SIDE]
TITLE: Separate not yet, no longer, never, and not at all
REF: 18.6

Non ancora denies that an expected event has happened so far. Non più says that something formerly true has stopped. Non with mai rejects occurrence over the relevant time span, while non affatto and non per niente reject a degree or assessment completely.
TL: Il comitato non ha ancora pubblicato il rapporto.
EN: The committee has not published the report yet.
[PAUSE:800]
TL: Il comitato non pubblica più rapporti mensili.
EN: The committee no longer publishes monthly reports.
[PAUSE:800]
TL: La conclusione non è affatto ovvia.
EN: The conclusion is not at all obvious.
[PAUSE:900]
Start from the positive expectation: already becomes not yet, still becomes no longer, always becomes never, and a positive degree becomes not at all.
SHOW: già -> non + verb + ancora · ancora -> non + verb + più · sempre -> non + verb + mai

[CARD]
TITLE: Negation inside an infinitive preserves the main claim
REF: 18.1

With two verbs, placing non before the infinitive denies the embedded action while affirming the main verb. Placing it before the main verb normally denies the attempt, decision, or claim itself.
TL: Ho cercato di non interrompere la relatrice.
EN: I tried not to interrupt the speaker.
[PAUSE:800]
TL: Non ho cercato di interrompere la relatrice.
EN: I did not try to interrupt the speaker.
[PAUSE:900]
The difference is structural, not stylistic. Identify which verb contributes the proposition you intend to reject.
SHOW: main verb + non + infinitive -> embedded negation · non + main verb -> matrix negation
Flip for verbs that allow the interpretation to drift upward.

[SIDE]
TITLE: Opinion and volition verbs can blur the surface scope
REF: 18.1

With verbs of believing, seeming, expecting, wishing, and intending, negation before the main verb can be understood as applying to the subordinate event. Italian can therefore present similar meanings with two surface placements.
TL: Credo che Elena non accetterà l'incarico.
EN: I think Elena will not accept the assignment.
[PAUSE:800]
TL: Non credo che Elena accetti l'incarico.
EN: I do not think Elena will accept the assignment.
[PAUSE:900]
The second pattern normally requires the subjunctive after a negated opinion verb. With volere, non before the main verb is especially normal even when the practical aim is that the subordinate event not happen.
TL: Non voglio che diffondiate questi dati.
EN: I do not want you to circulate these data.
SHOW: literal scope + lexical convention + mood of the subordinate clause

[CARD]
TITLE: A negative question advertises an assumption
REF: 18.4

Non in a question often invites confirmation of something the speaker already expects to be true. It is not a neutral request for information.
TL: Non avevi già inviato la versione definitiva?
EN: Had you not already sent the final version?
[PAUSE:800]
TL: La scadenza è venerdì, non è vero?
EN: The deadline is Friday, isn't it?
[PAUSE:900]
Colloquial no can serve as a compact confirmation tag. Choose an unmarked question when you do not want to preload the answer.
TL: Ci incontriamo davanti alla biblioteca, no?
EN: We are meeting in front of the library, right?
SHOW: negative question -> expected confirmation · statement + no? -> colloquial tag

[SIDE]
TITLE: Exclamatory non can reinforce rather than reverse
REF: 18.4

In exclamations, a formally negative clause can emphasize the surprising extent of a positive event. Context and exclamatory intonation prevent a literal negative reading.
TL: Quante difficoltà non abbiamo superato insieme!
EN: How many difficulties we have overcome together!
[PAUSE:800]
TL: Che cosa non farebbe pur di ottenere quel posto!
EN: The things he would do to get that position!
[PAUSE:900]
This is a marked rhetorical construction. Use ordinary affirmative wording when emphasis does not justify the interpretive extra work.
SHOW: exclamatory non -> emphatic extent, not factual denial

[CARD]
TITLE: Coordinate negative elements under one verbal frame
REF: 18.2, 18.5

Né coordinates two or more rejected alternatives. When the coordinated phrase follows the verb, non normally supplies the preverbal negation. A preverbal negative subject instead licenses the clause without non.
TL: Non abbiamo né confermato né annullato la prenotazione.
EN: We have neither confirmed nor cancelled the reservation.
[PAUSE:800]
TL: Né Giulia né Paolo hanno risposto al messaggio.
EN: Neither Giulia nor Paolo answered the message.
[PAUSE:900]
Do not confuse né with o no. Né joins alternatives that are both rejected; o no asks whether one proposition holds.
TL: Devi decidere se partecipare o no.
EN: You must decide whether to take part or not.
SHOW: both rejected -> né X né Y · polarity alternative -> o no
Flip for a final scope and concord check.

[SIDE]
TITLE: Run the scope, position, and discourse checks
REF: 18.1, 18.2, 18.3, 18.5, 18.6

First identify exactly what is denied. Second choose non, a negative pronoun or adverb, o no, o meno, or mica. Third place that element relative to the finite verb, clitics, and any infinitive. Finally check whether the sentence carries contrast, emphasis, an expected answer, or a contradicted assumption.
TL: Nessuno ha ancora risposto, ma non è mica troppo tardi.
EN: Nobody has answered yet, but it is not too late, contrary to what you might think.
[PAUSE:900]
A negative word before the verb normally excludes non; the same word after the verb requires it. Non ancora and non più point in opposite directions along a timeline. Mica and negative questions add discourse commitments beyond simple truth conditions.
SHOW: scope -> expression -> position -> concord -> discourse force
