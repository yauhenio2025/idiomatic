## SCRIPT

[CARD]
TITLE: Government before translation
SVG: de_praepositionen_c01f.svg
REF: 18

A preposition does two things at once. It frames a relationship, and it governs the case of the noun phrase that follows. The case is therefore part of the preposition's grammar, not a decoration added at the end.
TL: Wir verhandeln ohne unnötige Verzögerungen.
EN: We are negotiating without unnecessary delays.
[PAUSE:1100]
The phrase after the preposition is accusative because that preposition governs the accusative. Translation gives you the broad relationship; government gives you the German form.
SHOW: relationship + governed case = prepositional phrase
Flip the card.

[SIDE]
TITLE: Four families, one moving boundary
REF: 18, 18.1.4, 18.2.5, 18.3.4, 18.4.1

The system has four families: fixed accusative, fixed dative, two-way, and genitive. Hear one member of each family in context.
TL: Gegen den Vorschlag gab es erheblichen Widerstand.
EN: There was considerable opposition to the proposal.
[PAUSE:800]
TL: Mit dem Gegenvorschlag begann eine neue Verhandlungsrunde.
EN: A new round of negotiations began with the counterproposal.
[PAUSE:800]
TL: Während der Sitzung blieb der Ton sachlich.
EN: The tone remained measured during the meeting.
[PAUSE:800]
TL: Die Unterlagen liegen auf dem Tisch.
EN: The documents are lying on the table.
The first two families are lexical: learn the case with the preposition. The two-way family makes a semantic choice. The genitive family also carries a strong register signal.
SHOW: fixed accusative · fixed dative · two-way · genitive
First, the fixed families.

[CARD]
TITLE: The accusative family traces a path
REF: 18.1, 18.1.2, 18.1.5, 18.1.6

The common accusative prepositions often frame a path, an encircling relation, opposition, or absence. Those meanings help memory, but the government stays fixed even when the use becomes abstract.
TL: Wir liefen durch den Innenhof und um das Hauptgebäude.
EN: We walked through the inner courtyard and around the main building.
[PAUSE:1000]
TL: Ohne einen Lageplan hätten wir den Nebeneingang nicht gefunden.
EN: Without a site plan, we would not have found the side entrance.
[PAUSE:1000]
Do not run a fresh spatial test every time. Once you have selected one of these fixed prepositions, accusative is already decided.
SHOW: fixed accusative: bis · durch · für · gegen · ohne · um
Flip the card for the unusual member of the family.

[SIDE]
TITLE: The endpoint word recruits help
SVG: de_praepositionen_c02b.svg
REF: 18.1.1

The endpoint preposition is rarely a full preposition on its own. Before a bare place name or a simple time expression, it can stand alone.
TL: Die Delegation fuhr bis Brüssel.
EN: The delegation travelled as far as Brussels.
[PAUSE:900]
If a determiner follows, it normally recruits a second preposition, and that second preposition governs the case.
TL: Die Besucher gingen bis an die äußere Absperrung.
EN: The visitors went as far as the outer barrier.
[PAUSE:900]
Its fixed combination with the surface preposition can also mean except for.
TL: Bis auf zwei Rückfragen war alles geklärt.
EN: Apart from two follow-up questions, everything had been clarified.
SHOW: bare endpoint · endpoint + second preposition · bis auf = except for
Five endpoint decisions next.

[CARD]
TITLE: Origin is not the same as departure
SVG: de_praepositionen_c03f.svg
REF: 18.2.1, 18.2.8

The dative family includes two different English from-relations. One looks back into an interior, an origin, or a material.
TL: Die Probe stammt aus einem stillgelegten Bergwerk.
EN: The sample comes from a disused mine.
[PAUSE:1000]
The other starts at a person, a point, or a source.
TL: Der Bericht kam von einer externen Prüfstelle.
EN: The report came from an external inspection body.
[PAUSE:1000]
Think origin or out of for the first, departure point or source for the second. Both govern dative.
SHOW: origin / out of = aus · point / source = von
Flip the card for instrument and means.

[SIDE]
TITLE: Instrument is not the same as means
REF: 18.1.2, 18.2.5

German separates the concrete instrument used by an actor from the means or process through which a result comes about.
TL: Die Technikerin öffnete die Abdeckung mit einem Spezialschlüssel.
EN: The technician opened the cover with a special key.
[PAUSE:1000]
TL: Durch eine automatische Sperre ließ sich größerer Schaden verhindern.
EN: More serious damage was prevented by means of an automatic lockout.
[PAUSE:1100]
The instrument preposition belongs to the dative family. The means preposition belongs to the accusative family. The English word by cannot make that decision for you.
SHOW: concrete instrument = mit + dat. · means/process = durch + acc.
Mixed fixed-preposition practice comes after the system is complete.

[CARD]
TITLE: Put time on a line
SVG: de_praepositionen_c04f.svg
REF: 18.1.1, 18.2.7, 18.2.10

Time prepositions become easier when you stop translating one English word and locate the interval instead. One preposition opens a span in the past that still reaches the reference point.
TL: Seit dem Frühjahr arbeitet sie in Kopenhagen.
EN: She has been working in Copenhagen since the spring.
[PAUSE:900]
Another closes the span at an endpoint.
TL: Bis Ende September bleibt sie dort.
EN: She is staying there until the end of September.
[PAUSE:900]
A third establishes a new starting boundary.
TL: Ab Oktober übernimmt sie ein neues Projekt.
EN: From October onward, she is taking over a new project.
[PAUSE:1000]
Notice the present tense in the first sentence: the situation still holds now.
SHOW: seit = open from the past · bis = endpoint · ab = new start
Flip for three different views of time.

[SIDE]
TITLE: Clock neighborhood, point, or duration
REF: 18.1.6, 18.2.9, 18.3.7

With the noun meaning time, three prepositions divide the work. One places you around an approximate clock point.
TL: Um diese Zeit ist die Bibliothek fast leer.
EN: Around this time, the library is almost empty.
[PAUSE:900]
One identifies a particular historical point or limited occasion.
TL: Zu jener Zeit wurden die Akten noch handschriftlich geführt.
EN: At that time, the records were still kept by hand.
[PAUSE:900]
One treats the interval as a container in which something is completed.
TL: In erstaunlich kurzer Zeit wurde das gesamte Archiv digitalisiert.
EN: The entire archive was digitized in an astonishingly short time.
[PAUSE:1000]
The noun is the same; the perspective changes.
SHOW: um diese Zeit · zu jener Zeit · in kurzer Zeit

[CARD]
TITLE: Two-way means relation, not motion
SVG: de_praepositionen_c05f.svg
REF: 18.3.1

The nine two-way prepositions do not ask whether anything moves. They ask whether the phrase presents an existing spatial scene or a new endpoint relation.
TL: Der Kurier legt die Mappe auf den Schreibtisch.
EN: The courier puts the folder onto the desk.
[PAUSE:900]
The folder enters a new relation to the desk, so the endpoint phrase is accusative.
TL: Die Mappe liegt auf dem Schreibtisch.
EN: The folder is lying on the desk.
[PAUSE:900]
Now the phrase locates an existing scene, so it is dative.
SHOW: new endpoint relation → accusative · existing scene → dative
Flip the card and break the motion myth.

[SIDE]
TITLE: Moving bodies can stay dative
REF: 18.3.1

Movement inside a stable relation does not create a new endpoint. A patrol can move while remaining in front of a building.
TL: Die Wachleute patrouillieren vor dem Gebäude.
EN: The guards are patrolling in front of the building.
[PAUSE:900]
An aircraft can move while remaining above a location.
TL: Ein Hubschrauber kreist über dem Hafen.
EN: A helicopter is circling above the harbour.
[PAUSE:900]
Two people can walk while their position relative to each other stays constant.
TL: Sie geht neben ihrer Kollegin.
EN: She is walking beside her colleague.
[PAUSE:1000]
Ask whether the spatial relation changes, not whether the subject has legs.
SHOW: motion inside a stable relation still takes dative
Next, the exceptions that reveal how German packages an event.

[CARD]
TITLE: Arrival presents the achieved scene
REF: 18.3.1

Verbs of arrival, appearance, and disappearance normally use dative with a two-way preposition. German presents the achieved location, not the path into it.
TL: Der Nachtzug kommt auf dem äußersten Gleis an.
EN: The night train arrives on the outermost track.
[PAUSE:1000]
TL: Die Rednerin erscheint hinter dem Vorhang.
EN: The speaker appears behind the curtain.
[PAUSE:1000]
This is why a mechanical destination question is not enough. The verb's event structure can foreground the resulting scene.
SHOW: arrive · appear · disappear → achieved location → dative
Flip for a revealing simple-verb contrast.

[SIDE]
TITLE: A prefix can package completion
REF: 18.3.1

With a few related verbs, the simple form presents placement toward an endpoint, while the prefixed form packages the action as completed at a location.
TL: Der Hausmeister hängt den Aushang an die Glastür.
EN: The caretaker hangs the notice onto the glass door.
[PAUSE:900]
TL: Der Aushang hängt an der Glastür.
EN: The notice is hanging on the glass door.
[PAUSE:900]
TL: Der Hausmeister hängt den Aushang an der Glastür auf.
EN: The caretaker puts the notice up on the glass door.
[PAUSE:1100]
The prefix changes the way the event is viewed, and the case follows that view.
SHOW: placement in progress → acc. · completed location → dat.
Now build the spatial map itself.

[CARD]
TITLE: Inside, surface, edge
SVG: de_praepositionen_c07f.svg
REF: 18.3.2, 18.3.4, 18.3.7

Three high-frequency prepositions divide space by topology. One places something at an edge or side.
TL: Die Fahrräder stehen an der Mauer.
EN: The bicycles are standing by the wall.
[PAUSE:800]
One places it on a supporting surface.
TL: Die Schlüssel liegen auf der Ablage.
EN: The keys are lying on the shelf.
[PAUSE:800]
One encloses it inside a space.
TL: Die Gäste warten in der Eingangshalle.
EN: The guests are waiting in the entrance hall.
[PAUSE:900]
All three phrases describe a scene, so all three are dative.
SHOW: edge = an · surface = auf · interior = in
Flip the topology into destinations.

[SIDE]
TITLE: Destinations preserve the topology
REF: 18.3.3, 18.3.5, 18.3.8, 18.5.1

When the same three relations become endpoints, the preposition stays and the case changes to accusative.
TL: Wir stellen die Fahrräder an die Mauer.
EN: We put the bicycles by the wall.
[PAUSE:800]
TL: Leg bitte die Schlüssel auf die Ablage.
EN: Please put the keys onto the shelf.
[PAUSE:800]
TL: Die Gäste treten in die Eingangshalle.
EN: The guests step into the entrance hall.
[PAUSE:900]
Learn the location first. It predicts both the precise destination preposition and the case of the endpoint phrase.
SHOW: at the endpoint: an + acc. · auf + acc. · in + acc.
Ten sentence-building exercises on this topology next.

[CARD]
TITLE: Chosen motive or involuntary trigger
SVG: de_praepositionen_c08f.svg
REF: 18.2.1, 18.3.14

Two prepositions divide causes by agency. A deliberate motive or reason is viewed as something a person acts out of.
TL: Aus Verantwortungsgefühl meldete sie den Fehler sofort.
EN: Out of a sense of responsibility, she reported the error immediately.
[PAUSE:1000]
An involuntary trigger produces a bodily or emotional reaction.
TL: Vor Erleichterung musste er lachen.
EN: He had to laugh with relief.
[PAUSE:1000]
The contrast is not simply because of. Ask whether the cause motivates an action or overwhelms the experiencer.
SHOW: chosen motive = aus + dat. · involuntary reaction = vor + dat.
Eight cause contrasts next.

[SIDE]
TITLE: Common genitive frames mark register
REF: 18.4.1

Four common prepositions take the genitive in standard formal German. In everyday speech, the dative is widespread; in edited prose, the genitive remains the controlled choice.
TL: Trotz des knappen Zeitplans wurde der Bericht gründlich geprüft.
EN: Despite the tight schedule, the report was examined thoroughly.
[PAUSE:800]
TL: Während der Verhandlungen blieben beide Seiten sachlich.
EN: During the negotiations, both sides remained measured.
[PAUSE:800]
TL: Wegen eines Formfehlers musste die Abstimmung wiederholt werden.
EN: Because of a procedural error, the vote had to be repeated.
[PAUSE:1000]
At C1, the issue is not whether you will hear the dative. You will. The issue is whether you can control the register you are writing in.
SHOW: formal standard: statt · trotz · während · wegen + genitive
Flip for the spatial genitive family.

[CARD]
TITLE: Genitive relations draw boundaries
REF: 18.4.3

A productive group of genitive prepositions locates something relative to a boundary: inside it, outside it, above it, below it, or beyond it. These describe position, not a path toward a destination.
TL: Innerhalb des Schutzgebiets gelten strengere Auflagen.
EN: Stricter requirements apply within the protected area.
[PAUSE:900]
TL: Jenseits des Gebirgspasses änderte sich das Wetter schlagartig.
EN: Beyond the mountain pass, the weather changed abruptly.
[PAUSE:900]
In speech, and often in writing, a phrase with the source preposition can replace the genitive.
TL: Außerhalb von Ballungsräumen ist das Netz lückenhaft.
EN: Outside urban areas, the network has gaps.
SHOW: boundary relation + genitive · von-phrase common, especially in speech
Flip for the preposition with three competing shapes.

[SIDE]
TITLE: Along can stand in three places
REF: 18.4.2

The preposition meaning along is unusually mobile. In a common movement pattern, it follows an accusative noun phrase.
TL: Wir gingen den alten Kanal entlang.
EN: We walked along the old canal.
[PAUSE:900]
Before a noun phrase, formal writing often gives it the genitive.
TL: Entlang des alten Kanals wurden neue Bäume gepflanzt.
EN: New trees were planted along the old canal.
[PAUSE:900]
A very natural alternative frames the route with the edge preposition in the dative and places the along-word after it.
TL: Am alten Kanal entlang verläuft ein schmaler Radweg.
EN: A narrow cycle path runs along the old canal.
[PAUSE:1000]
Here position and case form a construction. Learn the whole shape.
SHOW: acc. + entlang · entlang + gen. · an + dat. + entlang
Now turn English to into German geometry.

[CARD]
TITLE: To is an endpoint prediction
SVG: de_praepositionen_c10f.svg
REF: 18.2.6, 18.2.9, 18.5

English to hides several German relationships. Start by predicting the scene at arrival. A geographical name without an article normally takes the directional dative preposition.
TL: Nach der Sitzung fährt Lea nach Oslo.
EN: After the meeting, Lea travels to Oslo.
[PAUSE:900]
A person or that person's home takes the general-direction dative preposition.
TL: Danach geht sie zu ihrer Gastgeberin.
EN: Afterwards, she goes to her host's home.
[PAUSE:900]
If the endpoint is an interior, use the interior preposition with accusative.
TL: Am Abend geht sie in ein kleines Theater.
EN: In the evening, she goes into a small theatre.
SHOW: name without article = nach · person = zu · interior = in + acc.
Flip for precision versus general direction.

[SIDE]
TITLE: Name the final relation precisely
REF: 18.5.1, 18.5.2

The precise two-way prepositions tell you what relation will hold at the endpoint: adjacent to an edge, supported by a surface, or enclosed in an interior.
TL: Der Fotograf geht ans Ufer, stellt die Tasche auf den Steg und legt die Kamera in den Koffer.
EN: The photographer goes to the bank, puts the bag onto the jetty, and places the camera in the case.
[PAUSE:1200]
The general-direction preposition is vaguer. It points toward a place without specifying the final topology.
TL: Später fährt er zum Hotel, ohne entschieden zu haben, ob er dort übernachtet.
EN: Later, he drives to the hotel without having decided whether he will stay there.
[PAUSE:1000]
Do not translate to. Predict the endpoint scene, then choose its preposition.
SHOW: precise endpoint: an / auf / in · general direction: zu
Six destination exercises next.

[CARD]
TITLE: A three-pass decision
REF: 18.2.1, 18.3.1, 18.3.8, 18.4.1

Use three passes. First choose the relationship or the lexical construction. Second, if the preposition is two-way and spatial, decide between an existing scene and a new endpoint. Third, check whether register affects a genitive choice.
TL: Wegen des Sturms bringt die Crew die Geräte aus dem Hangar in die Werkstatt.
EN: Because of the storm, the crew moves the equipment out of the hangar and into the workshop.
[PAUSE:1200]
The cause phrase takes formal genitive. The origin phrase has fixed dative. The new interior endpoint takes accusative. One sentence, three different reasons for case.
SHOW: choose relation → apply government → check viewpoint and register
Flip for the limit of translation.

[SIDE]
TITLE: Translation cannot choose the construction
REF: 18.2.3, 18.3.2, 18.3.11

At advanced level, a single English preposition may correspond to several German constructions, and a single German preposition may cover several English ones. Learn the relationship with the noun or verb, not a one-word equation.
TL: Sie arbeitet bei einer Stiftung an einem Bericht über die Energiepolitik.
EN: She works for a foundation on a report about energy policy.
[PAUSE:1200]
The first phrase identifies the employer, the second an ongoing project, the third a topic. English uses three different words; German chooses three relationships from its own system.
SHOW: learn governed relationships, not bilingual equals signs
