## SCRIPT

[CARD]
TITLE: A targets an endpoint; en locates
REF: 38.2.1, 38.2.2, 38.11

Start from the event, not from an English preposition. Directed motion normally selects a; a static location normally selects en.
TL: Mañana iremos al archivo, pero hoy trabajamos en la biblioteca.
EN: Tomorrow we will go to the archive, but today we are working in the library.
[PAUSE:900]
Arrival also profiles an endpoint, even when English says in.
TL: El tren llegó a Zaragoza con veinte minutos de retraso.
EN: The train arrived in Zaragoza twenty minutes late.
[PAUSE:900]
With no motion, ordinary institutions and meeting points take en.
TL: Te esperaré en la estación junto a la entrada principal.
EN: I will wait for you at the station beside the main entrance.
[PAUSE:1000]
SHOW: directed endpoint → a · static setting → en
Flip for motion verbs that package the relation differently.

[SIDE]
TITLE: Learn the verb together with its path
REF: 38.2.1, 38.11

Entrar most often takes en in Spain, while entrar a is widespread in Latin America. Do not flatten a real regional choice into a universal correction.
TL: Entraron en el despacho sin llamar.
EN: They entered the office without knocking.
[PAUSE:900]
Subirse selects a for the vehicle entered; quedarse selects en for the resulting location.
TL: Se subió al autobús y se quedó en la última fila.
EN: She got on the bus and stayed in the last row.
[PAUSE:900]
Before aquí, ahí, and allí, a motion verb normally needs no a.
TL: Ven aquí un momento y deja la carpeta en la mesa.
EN: Come here for a moment and leave the folder on the table.
[PAUSE:1000]
SHOW: entrar en · subirse a · quedarse en · venir aquí
Twenty-four hygiene-gated a-or-en choices follow.

[CARD]
TITLE: A and en build lexical expressions
REF: 38.2.3, 38.2.4, 38.2.7, 38.11

Beyond path and place, a appears in conventional phrases of manner, time, rate, and distance.
TL: Redactó el informe a mano y lo entregó a las nueve.
EN: She wrote the report by hand and submitted it at nine.
[PAUSE:900]
En forms its own lexical clusters; replace it only when the whole expression licenses the change.
TL: Hablaban en voz baja, pero discutían el asunto en serio.
EN: They were speaking quietly, but they were discussing the matter seriously.
[PAUSE:900]
The article contracts after a and de: a plus el becomes al, and de plus el becomes del. Proper names that contain El may preserve the article.
TL: Llegamos al puerto antes del amanecer.
EN: We reached the port before dawn.
[PAUSE:1000]
SHOW: store the phrase, not an English gloss · a + el = al · de + el = del
Flip for a useful error-check.

[SIDE]
TITLE: Do not make a universal at-equation
REF: 38.2.2, 38.11

English at does not mechanically produce a. Spanish often uses en for a stable setting.
TL: Marta estudia en Salamanca y vive en las afueras.
EN: Marta studies in Salamanca and lives on the outskirts.
[PAUSE:900]
Static a survives in restricted expressions of proximity, posture, and orientation.
TL: El abogado permaneció a la puerta hasta que terminó la vista.
EN: The lawyer remained at the door until the hearing ended.
[PAUSE:900]
When neither rule nor analogy decides the form, the expression itself is the unit to learn.
SHOW: rule first · lexical expression second · never translate at in isolation
Eight idiomatic a-or-en completions follow.

[CARD]
TITLE: Desde foregrounds the starting boundary
REF: 38.8, 38.9

De can mark source, origin, possession, material, content, or classification. Desde specifically points to the boundary from which space, time, or observation begins.
TL: Desde la azotea se ve toda la bahía.
EN: From the roof terrace, the whole bay is visible.
[PAUSE:900]
When origin is merely part of a route, de is often enough.
TL: Volvieron de Lisboa el domingo por la noche.
EN: They returned from Lisbon on Sunday night.
[PAUSE:900]
For an interval with two explicit endpoints, pair desde with hasta, or de with a.
TL: La exposición estará abierta desde mayo hasta septiembre.
EN: The exhibition will be open from May until September.
[PAUSE:1000]
SHOW: profiled starting point → desde · general source → de · desde ... hasta
Flip for combinations that must stay parallel.

[SIDE]
TITLE: Keep range pairings aligned
REF: 38.8, 38.9, 38.14

Do not normally cross desde with a or de with hasta when both endpoints form one range.
TL: Atienden de nueve a dos y desde las cuatro hasta las siete.
EN: They see clients from nine to two and from four until seven.
[PAUSE:900]
Desde also introduces a standpoint from which a judgment is made.
TL: Desde una perspectiva jurídica, el acuerdo plantea varias dudas.
EN: From a legal perspective, the agreement raises several doubts.
[PAUSE:900]
De remains the productive connector inside noun phrases.
TL: El informe de la comisión consta de tres capítulos.
EN: The committee's report consists of three chapters.
[PAUSE:1000]
SHOW: de ... a · desde ... hasta · viewpoint → desde
Nine unflagged de-or-desde choices follow.

[CARD]
TITLE: Para aims forward; por explains or traverses
REF: 38.16.1, 38.17.1, 38.17.5, 38.17.6

The most reliable starting contrast is orientation. Para points toward a recipient, destination, purpose, or intended result. Por looks backward to a cause or motive, or sideways through a route, means, or exchange.
TL: Preparé esta síntesis para el comité.
EN: I prepared this summary for the committee.
[PAUSE:900]
TL: Reescribí el apartado por petición de la directora.
EN: I rewrote the section at the director's request.
[PAUSE:900]
With an infinitive, para normally states the intended purpose; por can state the motive or the step chosen first.
TL: Para resolver el problema, empezaremos por revisar los datos.
EN: To solve the problem, we will start by reviewing the data.
[PAUSE:1000]
SHOW: intended endpoint → para · cause, route, means, exchange → por
Flip for pairs where both forms are grammatical.

[SIDE]
TITLE: The noun after the preposition does not decide alone
REF: 38.16.1, 38.17.1, 38.17.10

The same person can be a beneficiary with para, a motive with por, or someone represented by por.
TL: Compré el billete para Ana, pero hice la gestión por ella.
EN: I bought the ticket for Ana, but I handled the paperwork on her behalf.
[PAUSE:900]
Por can mark an exchange or price; para can mark the thing's intended use.
TL: Cambió su cámara por una tableta para el trabajo.
EN: He exchanged his camera for a tablet for work.
[PAUSE:900]
Do not ask which word means for. Ask what relation the speaker is constructing.
SHOW: beneficiary ≠ motive ≠ substitute · relation before translation
Fifteen general por-or-para decisions follow.

[CARD]
TITLE: Place and time reveal viewpoint
REF: 38.16.3, 38.16.9, 38.17.14, 38.17.15

In space, por traces an area or route; para names a destination.
TL: Caminamos por el casco antiguo antes de salir para Granada.
EN: We walked through the old quarter before leaving for Granada.
[PAUSE:900]
In time, por can locate an approximate period, while para can set a deadline or projected time.
TL: Pasaré por tu oficina por la mañana; necesito la copia para el viernes.
EN: I will stop by your office in the morning; I need the copy by Friday.
[PAUSE:900]
Para with a clock time often means no later than or scheduled for, not duration.
TL: La reunión quedó fijada para las seis.
EN: The meeting was scheduled for six.
[PAUSE:1000]
SHOW: through or around → por · toward or by → para
Flip for duration and expected completion.

[SIDE]
TITLE: A duration may need no preposition
REF: 38.16.9, 38.17.14, 38.17.15

Spanish can express a completed duration directly, especially when the time phrase answers how long.
TL: Vivió tres años en Quito.
EN: She lived in Quito for three years.
[PAUSE:900]
Por can present a duration as temporary, intended, or approximate.
TL: Han cerrado el museo por dos semanas.
EN: They have closed the museum for two weeks.
[PAUSE:900]
Para projects an endpoint from the speaker's present plan.
TL: Esperamos terminar las obras para finales de agosto.
EN: We expect to finish the work by the end of August.
[PAUSE:1000]
SHOW: bare measured duration · temporary span → por · deadline → para
Eighteen clean place-and-time contrasts follow.

[CARD]
TITLE: Government is part of the lexical entry
REF: 38.16, 38.17

Many verbs, adjectives, and nouns select por or para independently of the broad semantic contrast. Learn the frame with a natural complement.
TL: Se interesó por la propuesta y se preparó para la entrevista.
EN: She became interested in the proposal and prepared for the interview.
[PAUSE:900]
Por commonly appears with motive, concern, admiration, blame, and choice.
TL: Lo criticaron por su falta de rigor, pero acabaron votando por él.
EN: They criticized him for his lack of rigour, but ended up voting for him.
[PAUSE:900]
Para commonly appears with readiness, capacity, and a reason that supports a future action.
TL: Tenemos motivos para aplazar la decisión.
EN: We have reasons to postpone the decision.
[PAUSE:1000]
SHOW: interesarse por · votar por · prepararse para · motivos para
Flip for discourse expressions.

[SIDE]
TITLE: Store fixed expressions whole
REF: 38.16, 38.17

Por cierto changes the discourse topic; por fin marks a delayed outcome; por poco marks a narrowly avoided event.
TL: Por cierto, por fin aprobaron el presupuesto.
EN: By the way, they finally approved the budget.
[PAUSE:900]
Por si acaso introduces a precaution, while para colmo adds a final aggravating circumstance.
TL: Guardé una copia por si acaso y, para colmo, perdí el original.
EN: I kept a copy just in case and, to make matters worse, I lost the original.
[PAUSE:900]
These are constructions, not free combinations generated anew each time.
SHOW: lexical frame + complement · discourse phrase as one unit
Eighteen hygiene-gated lexical choices follow.

[CARD]
TITLE: With people, identify the role precisely
REF: 38.16.1, 38.16.5, 38.16.8, 38.17.1, 38.17.10

Para can mark the intended recipient or the person whose judgment defines a standard.
TL: Este mensaje es para Lucía; para mí, la conclusión está clara.
EN: This message is for Lucía; in my view, the conclusion is clear.
[PAUSE:900]
Por can mark action on someone's behalf, a cause, or the person about whom one asks.
TL: Firmé por mi socio y luego pregunté por la asesora fiscal.
EN: I signed on my partner's behalf and then asked for the tax adviser.
[PAUSE:900]
Para sí means kept for oneself; por sí can mean by oneself or through one's own agency.
TL: Guardó la noticia para sí, pero resolvió el conflicto por sí sola.
EN: She kept the news to herself, but resolved the conflict by herself.
[PAUSE:1000]
SHOW: recipient or viewpoint → para · behalf, cause, inquiry → por
Flip for a minimal contrast.

[SIDE]
TITLE: Por ti and para ti can both be right
REF: 38.16.1, 38.17.1

Para ti presents a destination, gift, use, or judgment. Por ti presents motive, substitution, or effort on your behalf.
TL: Reservé una plaza para ti porque nadie podía hacer el trámite por ti.
EN: I reserved a place for you because nobody could complete the procedure on your behalf.
[PAUSE:900]
The contrast can change the whole scenario without changing the noun.
TL: Lo hice por ti, no para ti.
EN: I did it because of you or for your sake, not for you to receive.
[PAUSE:1000]
SHOW: para ti = endpoint · por ti = motive or behalf
Twenty person-centred choices follow.

[CARD]
TITLE: Delante de locates; ante frames confrontation
REF: 38.3, 38.4

Delante de gives a literal position in front of something. Ante is common when a person or institution faces a situation, authority, or evidence.
TL: Dejaron las bicicletas delante del edificio.
EN: They left the bicycles in front of the building.
[PAUSE:900]
TL: Ante la falta de pruebas, el juez archivó la causa.
EN: Faced with the lack of evidence, the judge closed the case.
[PAUSE:900]
Ante can be spatial in formal written language, but delante de is the ordinary literal choice.
SHOW: physical position → delante de · faced with or before an authority → ante
Flip to look below.

[SIDE]
TITLE: Debajo de is spatial; bajo extends abstractly
REF: 38.4

Debajo de identifies a concrete lower position. Bajo is less spatially specific and is required in many figurative relations.
TL: Encontraron la llave debajo del archivador.
EN: They found the key underneath the filing cabinet.
[PAUSE:900]
TL: El proyecto continúa bajo la supervisión de una comisión independiente.
EN: The project continues under the supervision of an independent committee.
[PAUSE:900]
Bajo also forms compact conditions and classifications.
TL: El informe se publicó bajo seudónimo y bajo estrictas condiciones.
EN: The report was published under a pseudonym and under strict conditions.
[PAUSE:1000]
SHOW: exact lower location → debajo de · authority, condition, heading → bajo
Sixteen unambiguous ante-delante and bajo-debajo choices follow.

[CARD]
TITLE: Tras compresses two different relations
REF: 38.21

Tras can mean behind in space or after in time. It is concise and common in written and journalistic registers.
TL: Tras la votación, los portavoces comparecieron ante la prensa.
EN: After the vote, the spokespeople appeared before the press.
[PAUSE:900]
In a spatial reading, detrás de is usually more explicit in everyday speech.
TL: El jardín queda detrás de la iglesia.
EN: The garden is behind the church.
[PAUSE:900]
The complement decides whether después de or detrás de is a valid expansion.
TL: Tras el biombo había una puerta estrecha.
EN: Behind the screen there was a narrow door.
[PAUSE:1000]
SHOW: time → tras or después de · space → tras or detrás de
Flip for sequencing and repetition.

[SIDE]
TITLE: Register and construction limit substitution
REF: 38.21

Before an infinitive, tras normally marks temporal sequence.
TL: Tras revisar el contrato, solicitaron dos cambios.
EN: After reviewing the contract, they requested two changes.
[PAUSE:900]
Repeated formulas such as día tras día are fixed; después de does not replace tras there.
TL: Día tras día, el equipo fue recuperando la confianza.
EN: Day after day, the team gradually regained confidence.
[PAUSE:900]
Use the expansion as a diagnostic, then keep tras where its compact register is appropriate.
SHOW: expand to test meaning · preserve fixed repetition
Eight clean tras rewrites follow.

[CARD]
TITLE: The remaining simple prepositions map relations
REF: 38.6, 38.7, 38.12, 38.13, 38.14, 38.15

Con marks accompaniment or instrument; contra marks opposition, contact, or directed impact; entre covers both between and among.
TL: Abrieron la caja con una palanca y apoyaron la tapa contra la pared.
EN: They opened the box with a crowbar and propped the lid against the wall.
[PAUSE:900]
TL: El acuerdo se negoció entre las tres delegaciones.
EN: The agreement was negotiated among the three delegations.
[PAUSE:900]
Hacia gives direction or approximation; hasta gives a reached boundary; mediante foregrounds a means or procedure.
TL: Avanzaron hacia el norte hasta el amanecer mediante rutas secundarias.
EN: They advanced northwards until dawn by means of secondary routes.
[PAUSE:1000]
SHOW: relation inventory → company · opposition · grouping · direction · boundary · means
Flip for dependency, absence, and topic.

[SIDE]
TITLE: Según, sin, and sobre organize a clause compactly
REF: 38.18, 38.19, 38.20

Según attributes information or makes an outcome depend on a variable.
TL: Según el informe, los resultados varían según la región.
EN: According to the report, the results vary by region.
[PAUSE:900]
Sin can precede a noun or an infinitive; the infinitive may describe an action not performed or a resulting state.
TL: Salió sin despedirse y dejó dos asuntos sin resolver.
EN: He left without saying goodbye and left two matters unresolved.
[PAUSE:900]
Sobre can mean on a surface, above, approximately, or about a topic.
TL: Dejaron sobre la mesa un estudio sobre vivienda pública.
EN: They left a study about public housing on the table.
[PAUSE:1000]
SHOW: según = source or dependency · sin = absence · sobre = support, approximation, topic
Twelve broad-relation choices follow.

[CARD]
TITLE: Complex prepositions behave as constructions
REF: 38.23

Multiword prepositions package recurring relations such as cause, contrast, replacement, direction, and conformity.
TL: A raíz del incidente, revisaron el protocolo de acuerdo con la normativa vigente.
EN: As a result of the incident, they reviewed the protocol in accordance with the current regulations.
[PAUSE:900]
The internal preposition is fixed. Learn con motivo de, en vez de, and a la sombra de as complete frames.
TL: Con motivo del aniversario, celebraron un acto a la sombra del antiguo claustro.
EN: On the occasion of the anniversary, they held an event in the shade of the old cloister.
[PAUSE:900]
Register matters: a formal construction may be accurate without being the natural choice in ordinary conversation.
SHOW: store the full frame · preserve its internal preposition · check register
Flip for attachment.

[SIDE]
TITLE: Do not compress every English noun phrase
REF: 38.1.4, 38.23

English freely attaches a prepositional phrase to a noun. Spanish often prefers de, para, or a relative clause that states the hidden relation.
TL: El vuelo que va a Bogotá ya está embarcando.
EN: The flight to Bogotá is now boarding.
[PAUSE:900]
A phrase is safer when the noun itself licenses its complement.
TL: Publicaron un estudio sobre movilidad y un plan para reducir el tráfico.
EN: They published a study on mobility and a plan to reduce traffic.
[PAUSE:900]
When attachment could describe the action instead of the noun, expand the relation.
SHOW: noun licensing first · otherwise restore the hidden relative clause
Three unflagged phrase translations follow; seven judgment-flagged items remain excluded.

[CARD]
TITLE: Put the preposition before its complement
REF: 38.1.1, 38.1.2

Spanish prepositions precede noun phrases, pronouns, and infinitives. They do not strand at the end as English prepositions can.
TL: ¿En qué criterio se basaron?
EN: What criterion did they base their decision on?
[PAUSE:900]
If coordinated verbs govern different prepositions, repeat each full frame instead of forcing one complement to serve both.
TL: Lo acusaron de ocultar datos y de colaborar con la empresa rival.
EN: They accused him of concealing data and collaborating with the rival company.
[PAUSE:900]
The preposition belongs to the governing word, not to the English translation.
SHOW: preposition + complement · no stranding · repeat distinct government
Flip for infinitive frames.

[SIDE]
TITLE: Learn verb, preposition, and infinitive together
REF: 22.2.2

Some verbs take a before an infinitive, some take de, en, or por, and others take no preposition at all.
TL: Se negó a firmar, dejó de insistir y consiguió salir a tiempo.
EN: She refused to sign, stopped insisting, and managed to leave on time.
[PAUSE:900]
Changing the preposition can change the construction rather than merely its style.
TL: Empezó por resumir los hechos y terminó por admitir el error.
EN: He began by summarizing the facts and ended up admitting the mistake.
[PAUSE:900]
Store a compact paradigm with each verb and test it in a complete sentence.
TL: Confío en que todo salga bien, pero me acuerdo de haber dudado al principio.
EN: I trust that everything will go well, but I remember having doubted at first.
[PAUSE:1000]
SHOW: negarse a · dejar de · insistir en · optar por · no preposition with conseguir
Ten government choices close the unit.
