## SCRIPT

[CARD]
TITLE: Build cardinals in spoken groups joined by e
REF: 6.1.1

Portuguese joins tens and units with e. The words remain separate: vinte e um, trinta e dois, and noventa e nove.
TL: A sala tem trinta e seis lugares.
EN: The room has thirty-six seats.
[PAUSE:850]
The forms um and dois change before feminine nouns, including inside a compound number.
TL: Convidamos vinte e uma pesquisadoras e trinta e dois pesquisadores.
EN: We invited twenty-one female researchers and thirty-two male researchers.
[PAUSE:950]
Choose the noun's gender before producing the final unit. Counting in the abstract keeps um and dois.
SHOW: tens + e + units · M: um/dois · F: uma/duas · write each word separately
Flip to cross the hundred boundary.

[SIDE]
TITLE: Cem is exact; cento introduces a remainder
REF: 6.1.1, 6.1.2

Use cem for exactly one hundred. Before a smaller number, replace it with cento and join the remainder with e.
TL: O arquivo contém cem páginas e cento e três imagens.
EN: The file contains one hundred pages and one hundred and three images.
[PAUSE:850]
Hundreds from duzentos to novecentos agree with a following feminine noun.
TL: A editora imprimiu seiscentas e cinquenta cópias.
EN: The publisher printed six hundred and fifty copies.
[PAUSE:950]
In abstract counting, use the masculine forms. Agreement belongs to the full noun phrase, not to the last digit alone.
SHOW: 100 -> cem · 101+ -> cento e ... · 200-900 + F noun -> -as

[CARD]
TITLE: Mil stays invariable, but its surrounding numerals can agree
REF: 6.1.2

Mil does not take a plural ending. The preceding two can still be duas before a feminine noun, and the following hundreds also agree.
TL: A campanha distribuiu duas mil e quinhentas cartilhas.
EN: The campaign distributed two thousand five hundred booklets.
[PAUSE:850]
After mil, use e before a round hundred or a number below one hundred. Omit it before a full hundreds group that has its own remainder.
TL: Registramos mil e duzentos casos, mas mil oitocentos e quarenta respostas.
EN: We recorded one thousand two hundred cases, but one thousand eight hundred and forty responses.
[PAUSE:950]
Written Brazilian numbers normally use a full stop to separate thousands and a comma for decimals.
SHOW: mil is invariable · mil e + round hundred/small remainder · mil + hundreds-and-units · 2.500
Flip to scale beyond a thousand.

[SIDE]
TITLE: Milhão and bilhão behave as countable nouns
REF: 6.1.3

Unlike mil, milhão, bilhão, and trilhão take um and form plurals in -ões.
TL: O relatório prevê dois milhões de usuários e um bilhão de acessos.
EN: The report forecasts two million users and one billion visits.
[PAUSE:850]
When one of these scale nouns directly quantifies a following noun, insert de. A more complex number continues without de at that boundary.
TL: O projeto custará um milhão e quinhentos mil reais.
EN: The project will cost one million five hundred thousand reais.
[PAUSE:950]
News writing often abbreviates the scale as mi, bi, or tri, but speech restores the complete noun.
SHOW: um milhão/bilhão/trilhão · plural -ões · exact scale + de + noun · complex number -> no de inside it

[CARD]
TITLE: Ordinals are agreeing adjectives placed before the noun
REF: 6.2

An ordinal precedes the noun and agrees with it in gender and number. The written abbreviation marks gender with superscript o or a.
TL: Ela apresentou a vigésima proposta na trigésima reunião.
EN: She presented the twentieth proposal at the thirtieth meeting.
[PAUSE:850]
In a compound ordinal, every element agrees.
TL: A equipe terminou na vigésima quarta posição.
EN: The team finished in twenty-fourth position.
[PAUSE:950]
Do not carry a masculine digit label into a feminine noun phrase; the entire spoken ordinal is adjectival.
SHOW: ordinal + noun · every element agrees · 24.º M / 24.ª F
Flip for large ordinals and the forms speakers usually avoid.

[SIDE]
TITLE: Large ordinals are systematic but often replaced in speech
REF: 6.2

Portuguese has learned forms such as centésimo, milésimo, and milionésimo. When a large ordinal is used, every numerical component is ordinal.
TL: O segundo milionésimo visitante recebeu um prêmio.
EN: The two millionth visitor received a prize.
[PAUSE:850]
Forms for two hundredth through nine hundredth are rare in ordinary speech. A cardinal paraphrase is usually clearer.
TL: A cidade comemorou seus quinhentos anos.
EN: The city celebrated its five-hundredth anniversary.
[PAUSE:950]
Use a lexical ordinal when the register supports it; otherwise recast the measure as a cardinal expression.
SHOW: large ordinal -> every component ordinal · rare learned hundreds -> prefer a cardinal paraphrase
Ten source ordinal expansions follow.

[CARD]
TITLE: Collective numerals package an approximate quantity
REF: 6.3.1, 6.3.2

Dezena, centena, and milhar are nouns. Their plurals commonly express approximate scale and require de before the counted noun.
TL: Centenas de mensagens chegaram em poucos minutos.
EN: Hundreds of messages arrived in a few minutes.
[PAUSE:850]
The expressions às dezenas, às centenas, and aos milhares describe entities arriving or occurring in large groups.
TL: Os candidatos se inscreveram aos milhares.
EN: Candidates registered by the thousands.
[PAUSE:950]
Dúzia means an actual set of twelve; meia dúzia means six, not an approximation.
SHOW: dezenas/centenas/milhares de + noun · às dezenas/centenas · aos milhares · dúzia = twelve
Flip to numbers used as names.

[SIDE]
TITLE: A numeral used as a noun inherits the hidden category's gender
REF: 6.12

When a number identifies a bus route, apartment, card, or similar item, its article reflects the understood noun.
TL: Pegue a cento e quarenta e sete e desça perto do museu.
EN: Take the 147 bus and get off near the museum.
[PAUSE:850]
TL: Ele mora no quatrocentos e um.
EN: He lives in apartment 401.
[PAUSE:950]
Numbers ending in a vowel or nasal sound can take plural s when the numeral itself is being counted; consonant-final forms remain unchanged.
SHOW: hidden linha -> feminine · hidden apartamento -> masculine · uns/noves · dois dez

[CARD]
TITLE: Dates use cardinal days except for the first
REF: 6.4.1, 6.4.2

Say dates with dia and a cardinal number. The first day of the month is the exception: it uses primeiro.
TL: A exposição abre no dia primeiro de março e fecha no dia vinte e quatro de abril.
EN: The exhibition opens on March first and closes on April twenty-fourth.
[PAUSE:850]
Insert de between the day and month and between the month and year. Formal written references can omit dia and the article.
TL: Respondemos à sua carta de dois de fevereiro.
EN: We replied to your letter of February second.
[PAUSE:950]
English ordinal endings do not license ordinals on the other dates.
SHOW: dia + cardinal + de + month + de + year · day 1 -> primeiro · formal writing may omit dia
Flip to read years and centuries.

[SIDE]
TITLE: Read years and centuries as cardinal numbers
REF: 6.4.3, 6.4.4

Read a year as an ordinary cardinal number rather than splitting it into pairs.
TL: O arquivo foi criado em mil novecentos noventa e oito.
EN: The archive was created in 1998.
[PAUSE:850]
Centuries also use cardinals, whether the written form is Arabic or Roman.
TL: O romance se passa no século dezenove.
EN: The novel is set in the nineteenth century.
[PAUSE:950]
The English translation may use an ordinal, but Portuguese keeps século followed by a cardinal.
SHOW: year -> one cardinal number · século XIX -> século dezenove

[CARD]
TITLE: Clock time agrees with the hour
REF: 6.5.1, 6.5.2

Ask Que horas são? Use É uma hora for one o'clock and São duas horas for two o'clock. Hour numerals are feminine because hora is understood.
TL: É uma hora, mas a reunião começa às duas.
EN: It is one o'clock, but the meeting begins at two.
[PAUSE:850]
After a preposition, the time takes a definite article. The preposition a contracts to à before uma and às before plural hours.
TL: O laboratório funciona das oito às dezesseis horas.
EN: The laboratory operates from eight a.m. to four p.m.
[PAUSE:950]
Keep the singular-plural contrast aligned across the verb, numeral, and article.
SHOW: É uma · São duas+ · a + a uma -> à uma · a + as horas -> às
Flip to place minutes around the hour.

[SIDE]
TITLE: Use e after the hour and para before the next hour
REF: 6.5.3, 6.5.4, 6.5.5

Minutes past the hour follow e. Half past uses e meia, while quarter past is expressed with quinze rather than a noun meaning quarter.
TL: O trem chega às nove e meia; a palestra começa às dez e quinze.
EN: The train arrives at half past nine; the lecture begins at quarter past ten.
[PAUSE:850]
For minutes to the next hour, use para plus that hour's article, or state the current hour and minutes directly.
TL: São cinco para as quatro, ou três e cinquenta e cinco.
EN: It is five to four, or three fifty-five.
[PAUSE:950]
Faltar offers another natural frame: faltam dois minutos para a meia-noite.
SHOW: hour + e + minutes · e meia · quinze, not quarter · minutes + para + next hour

[CARD]
TITLE: Noon and midnight are nouns with articles
REF: 6.5.6

Use meio-dia for noon and meia-noite for midnight in the twelve-hour system. Both take a definite article after a preposition.
TL: O intervalo começa ao meio-dia e termina à uma.
EN: The break begins at noon and ends at one.
[PAUSE:850]
TL: Voltamos para casa dez minutos antes da meia-noite.
EN: We returned home ten minutes before midnight.
[PAUSE:950]
Reserve doze as an hour label for the twenty-four-hour clock.
SHOW: noon -> o meio-dia · midnight -> a meia-noite · 12-hour speech avoids doze
Flip to choose twelve-hour or twenty-four-hour framing.

[SIDE]
TITLE: Conversation names the part of day; schedules favor twenty-four hours
REF: 6.5.7, 6.5.8

Everyday speech can add da manhã, da tarde, da noite, or da madrugada when context does not identify the half of the day.
TL: O telefone tocou às três da madrugada.
EN: The telephone rang at three in the morning.
[PAUSE:850]
Schedules and precise appointments commonly use the twenty-four-hour clock, still with feminine hour forms and the required article.
TL: O voo das vinte e duas horas foi cancelado.
EN: The ten p.m. flight was canceled.
[PAUSE:950]
Choose the clock system for the situation, then apply the same agreement and article rules.
SHOW: everyday: hour + part of day · schedule: 19h45 -> dezenove e quarenta e cinco

[CARD]
TITLE: Fractions choose an ordinal denominator or avos
REF: 6.6.1, 6.6.2

Down to a tenth, and again from a hundredth upward, most denominator names match ordinal forms. One third is the lexical exception um terço.
TL: Dois terços dos participantes responderam.
EN: Two thirds of the participants responded.
[PAUSE:850]
Between a tenth and a hundredth, build the denominator with a cardinal number plus avos.
TL: A parcela corresponde a três dezesseis avos do total.
EN: The share corresponds to three sixteenths of the total.
[PAUSE:950]
The numerator counts the fractional parts; the denominator names their scale.
SHOW: 1/3 -> um terço · through 1/10 -> ordinal denominator · 1/11-1/99 -> cardinal + avos
Flip to separate adjectival meio from nominal metade.

[SIDE]
TITLE: Meio agrees with a unit; metade names one half of a whole
REF: 6.6.3, 6.6.4

Before a noun, meio is an adjective and agrees. After a measured quantity, e meio also agrees with the understood unit.
TL: Compramos meia tonelada de areia e dois litros e meio de tinta.
EN: We bought half a ton of sand and two and a half liters of paint.
[PAUSE:850]
Use the feminine noun metade for half as an independent portion. Follow it with de when naming the whole.
TL: Metade dos arquivos já foi revisada.
EN: Half of the files have already been reviewed.
[PAUSE:950]
Ask whether half modifies the measurement unit or names a portion in its own right.
SHOW: meio/meia + unit · quantity + e meio/meia · metade de + whole

[CARD]
TITLE: A decimal comma is spoken as vírgula
REF: 6.7

Brazilian notation uses a comma as the decimal separator. Read the digits after it individually when the expression requires that precision.
TL: A taxa caiu de oito vírgula sete cinco para oito vírgula zero cinco.
EN: The rate fell from 8.75 to 8.05.
[PAUSE:850]
Do not confuse the decimal comma with the full stop that groups thousands.
TL: O valor passou de dois mil e quinhentos para dois mil e quinhentos vírgula cinco.
EN: The value went from 2,500 to 2,500.5.
[PAUSE:950]
Interpret the punctuation convention before voicing the number.
SHOW: 8,75 -> oito vírgula sete cinco · full stop groups thousands · comma starts decimals
Flip to percentages and the agreement around them.

[SIDE]
TITLE: Por cento is fixed, but modifiers around a percentage are plural
REF: 6.8

Express a percentage with the cardinal number followed by por cento.
TL: Sessenta por cento dos eleitores compareceram.
EN: Sixty percent of the voters turned out.
[PAUSE:850]
An adjective accompanying a percentage appears in the masculine plural, and the article os may frame a definite percentage.
TL: Os vinte por cento restantes serão pagos amanhã.
EN: The remaining twenty percent will be paid tomorrow.
[PAUSE:950]
The following de phrase does not control that modifier's gender.
SHOW: number + por cento · modifier -> masculine plural · definite percentage may take os

[CARD]
TITLE: Money joins the currency unit and its fraction with e
REF: 6.9

In speech, link the main currency amount and the smaller unit with e. In writing, the decimal comma separates them.
TL: O ingresso custou oito reais e cinquenta centavos.
EN: The ticket cost eight reais and fifty centavos.
[PAUSE:850]
Without an explicit currency name, context may allow the short reading oito e cinquenta. Monetary amounts are grammatically plural, and surrounding gender follows the currency noun.
TL: Esses cinquenta dólares são suficientes, mas preciso de outras quinhentas libras.
EN: Those fifty dollars are enough, but I need another five hundred pounds.
[PAUSE:950]
Keep numerical agreement tied to reais, dólares, libras, or the other named unit.
SHOW: R$ 8,50 -> oito reais e cinquenta centavos · amount -> plural · gender follows currency noun
Flip for names followed by Roman numerals.

[SIDE]
TITLE: Monarchs and popes switch from ordinals to cardinals after ten
REF: 6.10

After the name of a monarch or pope, read Roman numerals up to ten as ordinals. Do not insert a definite article between the name and number.
TL: Dom Pedro Segundo governou o Brasil no século dezenove.
EN: Pedro the Second ruled Brazil in the nineteenth century.
[PAUSE:850]
Above ten, read the Roman numeral as a cardinal.
TL: Papa Bento Dezesseis renunciou em dois mil e treze.
EN: Pope Benedict the Sixteenth resigned in 2013.
[PAUSE:950]
First identify the construction: the same Roman numeral uses a cardinal after século and follows the special ordinal-to-ten rule after a ruler's name.
SHOW: ruler I-X -> ordinal · ruler XI+ -> cardinal · no inserted article · século + cardinal

[CARD]
TITLE: N and enésimo leave the exact number open
REF: 6.11

The letter N can function as an indefinite cardinal meaning any number of or umpteen.
TL: O atraso pode ter ocorrido por N motivos.
EN: The delay may have occurred for any number of reasons.
[PAUSE:850]
The related adjective enésimo means umpteenth and agrees with its noun.
TL: Ela refez a análise pela enésima vez.
EN: She redid the analysis for the umpteenth time.
[PAUSE:950]
These forms deliberately withhold a precise value; they do not stand for a particular hidden digit in ordinary discourse.
SHOW: N, pronounced ene -> indefinite cardinal · enésimo/enésima -> indefinite ordinal
Flip for a final production checklist.

[SIDE]
TITLE: Let the construction decide form, agreement, and connector
REF: 6.1, 6.2, 6.4, 6.5, 6.6, 6.7, 6.8, 6.9, 6.10, 6.12

Before producing a number, classify its job: count, order, date, time, fraction, decimal, percentage, money, or label.
TL: A vigésima segunda candidata chegou às duas e vinte e recebeu metade do prêmio.
EN: The twenty-second candidate arrived at two twenty and received half the prize.
[PAUSE:850]
Then apply agreement and choose the connector: e inside many cardinals and clock times, de after exact scale nouns and collective quantities, para before the next hour, or vírgula at a decimal boundary.
TL: Duas mil e trezentas pessoas compraram ingressos de vinte e cinco reais.
EN: Two thousand three hundred people bought tickets costing twenty-five reais.
[PAUSE:950]
Structure first, vocabulary second: Portuguese numerals expose the grammatical role of the quantity.
SHOW: classify -> agree -> connect -> voice · count/order/date/time/measure each has its own frame
