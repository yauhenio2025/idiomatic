# Source survey for the personal grammar reader

Verified 2026-08-01. This survey applies a deliberately narrow reuse rule: a
source is eligible for adaptation only when an authoritative source or catalog
page identifies it as **CC BY**, **CC BY-SA**, or **public domain**. “Free to
read” is not enough. CC BY-NC, CC BY-NC-SA, CC BY-NC-ND, ND, unclear-license,
paywalled, and all-rights-reserved works are not adapted here.

The license links below point either to the publisher's own record or to the
official license deed. Individual images, audio, quotations, and embedded media
inside an otherwise open textbook can carry different licenses; this reader
adapts only the authors' grammar prose, never those separately credited assets.

License shorthand:

- [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/): adaptation and
  redistribution allowed with attribution and an indication of changes.
- [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/): the same,
  but adaptations must be distributed under the same license.
- **Public domain**: no copyright restriction in the work itself. Project
  Gutenberg's transcriptions retain its distribution notice; territorial
  status must still be checked where relevant.

### Rejected or not used

- *Français inclusif* and *Enhancing French Skills* are CC BY-NC-SA; the NC
  condition is outside the commission's accepted set.
- *Liberté* and many cloned Pressbooks editions are CC BY-NC-SA or CC BY-NC.
- Commercial references such as *Le Bon Usage* and *A Comprehensive French
  Grammar* are all-rights-reserved/paywalled. They are not reproduced or
  adapted.

## Adaptation policy used in the chapters

Every chapter declares one of three statuses in its final paragraph:

1. **Adapted, CC BY** — the chapter reorganizes openly licensed source
   explanations, condenses them, and replaces examples; its final note names
   author, title, source URL, license, and changes.
2. **Adapted, CC BY-SA** — the same, with an explicit statement that the
   chapter itself is distributed under CC BY-SA 4.0 and must remain
   share-alike.
3. **Original, CC BY** — no source prose was adapted. Grammar facts are not
   copyrightable, but the explanation and examples are newly written for this
   learner. The final note marks that status unambiguously.

No chapter combines BY-SA-adapted prose with a weaker downstream license. No
chapter adapts the excluded NC/ND/all-rights-reserved sources.
