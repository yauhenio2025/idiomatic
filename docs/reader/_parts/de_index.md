# German reader: order, licensing, audio suitability

## Evidence-priority reading order

Read in filename order. The order follows the learner’s attested error profile, not a generic CEFR syllabus.

1. **01 — Adjective endings as an information system.** Six retained errors across 2019–2024 make this the clearest fossilized category. Read twice: once for the three-pattern logic, once for the diagnostic routine.
2. **02 — Gender stays; case moves.** Four retained gender errors and the dependence of every adjective ending on gender/case make this the support chapter for 01.
3. **03 — The dative is a verb’s decision.** Four retained object-case errors, repeated work on *gehören*, and teacher-supplied *widmen/widerstehen/entsprechen* justify immediate attention.
4. **04 — Process passive without fear of *werden*.** Roughly 35 teacher-supplied passives over five years, with no recorded spontaneous production, indicate avoidance rather than a single malformed rule.
5. **05 — N-nouns, real plurals, and false *-n*.** Directly separates the attested *einen Jungen* correction from spurious *Schillingen/Spielfilmen* and missing plural *Honorare*.
6. **06 — Genitive for written public prose.** Fifteen teacher-supplied genitives and documented reliance on *von* make this especially useful for books, essays, and political commentary.
7. **07 — Verb–preposition frames and da-compounds.** Seven corrections/repeated supplies (*darauf ankommen, daraus machen, davon überzeugen, darüber hinausgehen*) close the loop between governance, reference, and journalistic cohesion.

Suggested cadence: one chapter per session; reread 01 after 03, then reread 07 after 06. Each callout contains a production from the learner corpus or explicitly labels avoidance/supply evidence rather than inventing an error.

## Podcast-conversion candidates

- **Best:** 04 (passive), 06 (genitive), and 07 (da-compounds). Each has a strong conceptual arc, news/publishing examples, and contrasts that remain clear without a table.
- **Good with light scripting:** 02 (gender/case) and 03 (dative governance). Read the examples as minimal pairs and verbalize the case labels.
- **Needs an audio rewrite:** 01 and 05. Their small paradigms are valuable on a tablet but should become call-and-response “choose the ending/form” segments rather than a straight table reading.

For a spoken season, preserve the exact learner-error callouts but introduce them as “corpus checkpoint,” not as public biography. No source audio is licensed into this package; a podcast recording would be a new performance of the attributed adapted prose.
