## [Unreleased]

### Added
- **Podcast cards episode 5 (de, "Die Fälle")** ([source](../idiomatic/grammar/data/podcast_cards/de_case-system.md) + 10 SVG diagrams): the case-system season episode as 5 two-sided cards — the three-person scene, der/den/dem/des landmark, three gates (structure, verb government incl. the versorgen frame, prepositions + two-way), give-and-tell rhythm with the recorded errors, gender-trap quick fire; 49/55 TL lines season-verbatim.
- **Podcast cards episode 1 (es, "El subjuntivo")** ([source](../idiomatic/grammar/data/podcast_cards/es_subjunctive-machine.md) + 10 SVG diagrams): the subjunctive-machine season episode as 5 two-sided cards — stance vs assertion, the vowel switch, influence family + compression rule, reaction/doubt with the no-dudo trap, pending time/unidentified antecedent/si-firewall, quick fire; second of the approved batch, 33/45 TL lines season-verbatim.
- **Podcast cards episode 4 (fr, "Le genre")** ([source](../idiomatic/grammar/data/podcast_cards/fr_gender-is-memory.md) + [10 SVG diagrams](../idiomatic/grammar/data/podcast_cards/svg/)): the gender-is-memory season episode restructured into 5 two-sided cards per the approved ep-3 format — packet principle, final-E trap, l'/mon disguises, suffix compressors + head-noun rule, quick-fire — first of the seven-episode batch (episode-4 checkpoint per [PODCAST_CARDS_BATCH_COMMISSION.md](commissions/PODCAST_CARDS_BATCH_COMMISSION.md)); 49/54 TL lines verbatim from the season script (TTS cache-shared).
- **Podcast cards pilot (episode 3, fr)** ([idiomatic/grammar/podcast_cards.py](../idiomatic/grammar/podcast_cards.py)): grammar-walk episodes as ~5 two-sided Anki notes — per-side narration through the shared explainer clip cache, rich HTML ([CARD]/[SIDE]/TITLE:/SHOW:/TL-: markup, [source](../idiomatic/grammar/data/podcast_cards/fr_quantity-system.md)), and one visual per side. New frozen `Idiomatic Podcast Lesson v1` model (14 fields, slug+seq GUIDs); delivery via `apkgs.kind='podcast_lesson'` per lang into `…::0 Écoute::03 Beaucoup de` subdecks, zero add-on changes; `POST /admin/podcast-cards-build?lang&episode`, `GET /admin/podcast-cards-list`; `gemini.generate_image` + `gemini_image_model` setting. PILOT format approved by user 2026-08-03 after two review rounds; batching episodes 1+4-9 is the next step.
- **Podcast-card visuals are authored SVG diagrams** ([svg sidecars](../idiomatic/grammar/data/podcast_cards/svg/)): pilot review showed generated images drift semantically (Gemini read "de" as a language code), so the `SVG:` markup line inlines hand-authored, sanitized SVG into the card fields — night-mode themed via a shared `s-*` palette in the model CSS, no media files, no JS, $0/episode. `IMG:` Nano-Banana generation remains available per side for mood scenes.

### Fixed
- Uneven loudness between the English narrator and target-language voices in all stitched lessons (French examples were much quieter): the shared explainer renderer now levels every speech clip to −16 LUFS before stitching (`leveled_speech_clip` in [idiomatic/grammar/explainers.py](../idiomatic/grammar/explainers.py) — static gain + limiter, ebur128 with volumedetect fallback for one-word clips, cached beside the raw clip; renderer revision bumped so every rebuild re-stitches). The global loudnorm in `concat_mp3s` could never fix imbalance inside one file. Applies to podcast lesson cards, season MP3s, and explainer cards on their next rebuild.
